Hi there! We’re the Levin Family: Basya, Mimi, Chaim and

Yossi—chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

I am so excited; Sukkos is here! I love Sukkos! Especially the part where we all get to sit together in the sukkah.

Yup, and the sukkos are just like army tents. We can show everyone that we’re real Tzivos Hashem soldiers!

You know, we talk all the time about us being in the army, and how we’re battling the Yetzer Hara and stuff, but real armies have weapons. Like guns and grenades and missiles. I think we should get that stuff.

What are you saying? Yossi, we’re in Hashem’s army!

Exactly—ARMY! Armies need weapons to fight, right? How about I bring in a professional weapon maker to make us some real—

What? But in Tzivos Hashem, we don’t use that stuff to fight. We don’t need—

Yossi, why’d you start calling already? We didn’t all agree on a guest yet!

Shhh, Mimi. The guest is already answering. We might just have to join Yossi for this one…

Elad the professional weapon maker, welcome to our Parsha Podcast, and thank you for joining us today.

I’ll get straight to business. We’re in an army, so we need stuff to fight with. Can you tell us what the best weapons are?

We’re not just in any army. We’re in Hashem’s army.The best army in the world.

Whoa! Tzivos Hashem?! Wow! You kids are my heroes! Wait, what do you need weapons for?

Hahaha! You’re joking, right? You don’t need my silly weapons to fight him! My weapons hurt people. But you, you have the best weapons of all—Torah and mitzvos!

Yes! Exactly. Can you please convince Yossi of this? I mean, I don’t know who he’s planning on shooting anyway.

Listen kid, you just finished a huge battle: the Aseres Yemei Teshuvah. That was one tough fight against the Yetzer Hara. But do you all know who won?

See, that’s another reason why we need your kind of weapons! To show who won. In regular armies, when they win, they march proudly and hold their weapons up high in the air. It’s a victory sign! We need something to hold up and show that we won, too.

But, but … Did we win? How do we even know if we won?

Soldier, on Sukkos,we show the whole world that we are victorious. We proudly hold up our weapons for everyone to see.

Tzivos Hashem has risen above the Yetzer Hara as the victors! After having done a complete teshuvah, and being forgiven by Hashem, you are all tzaddikim!
