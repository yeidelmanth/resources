Hi there! We’re the Levin Family: Basya, Mimi, Chaim and

Yossi—chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Note: These images are for illustrative purposes only and are not meant to be an accurate depiction of the holy faces of the Tzaddikim.

Remember when Yosef Hatzaddik came? Can we bring him again? I want to ask him something.

Basya, don’t worry, if you have a question, we gotta get it answered! Yossi, can you bring us Yosef Hatzaddik?

Yep! The number is still in the recent calls list, I just need to…

Ah! You chayolim again! How can I help you this week?

Well, I was just wondering, why aren’t you upset at your brothers? They sent you into Mitzrayim!

Hashem sent me ahead to Mitzrayim so that I’d be able to feed my family when there was no more food. I wasn’t there to suffer, chas v’shalom. I was there to keep my family alive.

Like Chazal teaches us that Hashem creates the cure before the illness. Hashem sent you to prepare food before the hunger even started!

Yes. And you kids are in the same situation as me! You’re the cure for the illness.

What illness? How are we a cure? We don’t feed people!

Oh yes, you do! You feed the world with your Torah and mitzvos. Mitzvos are the flame, and Torah is the light that keeps the world alive.

That sounds silly. We’re in Golus, and I'm just a kid. How would I be able to keep the world alive? I’m not the king of Mitzrayim like you!

Hmm. Maybe you need to tell your Yetzer Hara that he’s got it all backwards. The whole reason you were put into Golus is so you could feed the world with your Torah and mitzvos!

So obviously we can do it! We were put here to do it!

Especially you chayolim. Hashem knew that in Golus there would be many kids who don’t get to learn in cheder and don’t know much about Yiddishkeit. So Hashem made the cure before the illness—He made you to help them!

You go to a wonderful school and learn so much about Torah and mitzvos. Hashem needs you to teach the Yiddishe children who don’t know those important things. You need to keep them alive by teaching them Torah!

Hey, I just realized something. Torah is called “bread”. It’s like we’re actually feeding them, just like you fed your family in Mitzrayim!

Yes, and even more so! Because you will teach those kids, and they will teach their friends and parents, who’ll teach their friends … until you reach the whole world.

So we’re actually keeping the whole world alive with Torah and mitzvos!
