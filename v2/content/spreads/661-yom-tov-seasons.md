So the limbs, all the days of the year, will be led,

Hashem recreates the entire world for us on Rosh Hashana, giving us new chayus in our service of Hashem

Tzivos Hashem is also a “head”—the head of the world, because we lead by positive example and work to bring Moshiach. So next time when the Yetzer Hara says, “What do you need to make a bracha for?”, tell him: “Hashem created the world, so certainly we must thank Him!”
