chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

This Shabbos is Shavuos. So, instead of reading the regular parsha in shul, we read a part of the Torah specifically for Shavuos.

Ooh, yay! I love Shavuos! We get ice cream and cheesecake and blintzes and so many other yummy things!

Yeah, but it’s also really hard to sit quietly and listen to the whole Aseres Hadibros. I wish we could get the ice cream without all that.

Yeah, I guess so. Anyway, I miss Zeidy, so we’re calling him today.

Kinderlach! So good to hear from you! Are you ready for Shavuos?

Oh yeah! We already made five cheesecakes and bought three flavors of ice cream!

And yesterday, we sat and learned all ten of the Aseres Hadibros so we can follow along and know what’s going on.

Baruch Hashem! So nice, so nice. What about invitations? Did you invite everyone?

Uh well, Mommy and Tatty invited some people. But that’s not really our job, is it?

But how is it our party? Isn’t the Torah for everyone?

Yes, yes, the Torah is for everyone. But we really only got it thanks to you kinderlach. Hashem wouldn’t give us the Torah until we chose someone to guarantee that it would be treasured and kept. And it wasn’t the zeidies, bubbies, teachers, or even Kohanim who could guarantee that. Do you know who did?

Exactly! As soon as we said that the kinderlach will be the guarantors for the Torah, Hashem agreed, and Matan Torah was able to happen.

I see. It’s only because of the kids that the Torah was given to us in the first place. Therefore, the celebration of Shavuos is for us. When everyone goes to shul to hear the Aseres Hadibros, it’s actually our party.

Well then, Zeidy, will you join us at shul to hear the Aseres Hadibros?

Ah, thank you for the invitation, Basyale. Yes, I will join you, b’ezras Hashem. And now, let’s ask all the other chayolim to think about it: How will you show your important part of this year's Matan Torah?

…and that’s another fabulous episode of the Parsha Podcast. Thank you, Zeidy, for joining us! We’ll be sure to think the question over. See you all chayolim next week!
