<h3>The Last Escape Room</h3>

Mendel and Sara Shir have just finished turning the newly- renovated garage into an escape room. Their younger siblings must find the clues and figure out which niggun to sing in order to leave the room. Freidel, Tzvi, and Yossi enter.

Click. The door closes behind them.

“Hey!” Tzvi says. “Where are the clues?”

“Yes, the garage is empty! There’s not even an old sefer on the shelves!” says Freidel.

So may I look for You in the Mikdash

“And no Rebbe picture or pushka on the walls!” says Yossi.

“Why is there a steam machine in the corner? It’s too hot.” complains Tzvi.

Freidel sneezes. “It’s dusty too.”

The door opens. “You figured it out!” says Mendel. “This niggun is about how Golus is like being locked up in a hot, dry place, far from Hashem. Our neshamos cry for Hashem to take us out. And just like we opened the door for you, Hashem will open the door for all of us, to the Geula Shelaima!”

Yossi starts to kvetch. “I’m thirsty!”

Crying in a hot, dusty, empty room? Tzvi has a thought.

“Tzama lecha nafshi...” he sings.

“That was a scary room!” exclaims Yossi, as he accepts the iced tea and chocolate cookies that Sara has waiting for them. “I’m glad we’re out!”
