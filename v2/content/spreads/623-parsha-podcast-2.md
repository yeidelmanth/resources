chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

In today’s Chitas, there’s a passuk that says, “שְאַל אָבִיָך וְיַגֵדְָך—ask your elders and they will tell you.” Yossi, can we call one of our great-grandparents?

Let me check this week’s guest … sure enough, it’s our great-great- great—I don’t even know how many “greats”! A relative who lived many generations ago: Sabba Aviezer, servant to Dovid Hamelech.

Well, there is something that’s on my mind. And it’s bothering me. Basically, a group of my friends like to sit right near the bus monitor and whisper all about her. I know it’s not nice, but everyone does it … and it really is lots of fun…

Oh, Basya! That’s horrible. I remember when some girls in my class kept whispering about me last year. I cried into my pillow many nights because of it.

I know, and I feel so bad. But everyone does it! It’s too hard not to join, so I’ve decided that I’m just not gonna go back to school.

You know what, Basya? What do you say we make the call? Maybe Sabba Aviezer has some advice.

Hello there, chayolim! Baruch Hashem, I am sitting and learning in a beautiful and calm valley. My doctor says that the air here is good for my lungs.

But you know, it wasn’t always so peaceful here in the Valley of Eila.

Eila … oooh, that’s where Dovid fought Golyas! He was this twelve-foot-tall Plishti giant dressed in huge armor, and Dovid was a little shepherd in regular clothes. But Hashem made a big neis, and Dovid won the fight, killing Golyas with just a tiny stone!

Exactly, mein kind. Hashem saves us again, and again, and again. We may be one small nation, but we have Hashem on our side.

Of course! After Hashem chose Yaakov, every Yid became “חֶבֶל נַחֲלָתו—the rope of His inheritance,” which means that each and every Jew is chosen by Hashem and connected to Him. Even though there are so many other nations, we're the ones whom Hashem chose to be His inheritance.

So it's like Hashem chose Yaakov, and then decided that all the Yidden that come after him will automatically get this special gift.

That’s exactly what it is! Basya, mein kind, you’re shy?

Well, it’s really great that we’re all chosen to get this special gift from Hashem, but I’m just a little girl. How am I supposed to fight my Yetzer Hara and do the right thing when it’s so so hard?

Oy, Basya’le, Basya’le! Look around and think about what happened right here! I saw with my own eyes how a little shepherd was victorious over the most powerful giant. You have Hashem on your side, just like Dovid Hamelech!

When Hashem chose us, we got a special connection with Him. And because of this special connection, you can win any war.

Of course! And it gets better: On Rosh Hashana, you strongly and joyously proclaimed that Hashem is “מֶלְֶך עַל ּכָל הָאָרֶץ—King over the whole world.” That gave us all the strength to be one nation, separate from everyone else. And that overpowers anything that disturbs our fulfillment of Torah—including the Yetzer Hara!

Yeah, wow. Okay. Here’s my plan: I’m gonna go to school tomorrow and be so strong and stand up to anyone who’s mean to the bus monitor!

Amazing. Such nachas! And now, let’s ask all the other chayolim to think about it: How will you use the power from Hashem to do good?

...and that’s another fabulous episode of the Parsha Podcast. Thank you, Sabba Aviezer, for joining us! We’ll be sure to think the question over. See you all chayolim next week!
