chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Today I babysat for the first time ever, and I made fifteen dollars! You know how many chocolates I could buy with that? If I babysit again next week, then I’ll have thirty dollars. I should really find more people to babysit for, because then I could get way more—

Wow, someone is excited! I mean, Mimi, I am happy for you, but you’re kinda obsessed with this babysitting thing.

You don’t get it. I need to find more babysitting jobs, so that I can make more money, so that I can buy more nice things! Maybe I could make a flier and ask Mommy to send it to all her friends? Hmm … that wouldn’t be enough, I need to—

I think Basya is right. You can’t stop talking about your new job, Mimi! It’s a bit much, really.

Yeah, Mimi, and I am sure you can live without the money. Mommy and Tatty already buy us everything we need.

Actually Mimi, maybe you can talk to this week’s guest about it? He’s a taxi driver.

A taxi driver? Since when are we calling a taxi driver?

Tatty says that taxi drivers are the best philosophers. And this one is also a frum Yid!

It sounds like you’re driving really fast! Are you in the middle of an emergency?

Mimi, I might be driving a bit quickly, but I heard that your ideas are also pretty wild right now!

What’s that supposed to mean? My ideas are just—well, maybe a bit … excited.

Okay then, I too am driving with excitement. But you really wish I would calm down, don’t you?

Okay, okay, I’ll slow down and explain. You see, Mimi has a new job. Jobs are good. We are supposed to work, as Hashem says,שֵׁשֶׁת יָמִים ּתַעֲשֶׂה מְלָאכָה ּובַּיֹום ” .”הַּשְׁבִיעִי יִהְיֶה לָכֶם קֹדֶׁש

That means, “For six days you should work and on the seventh it will be for you ‘קֹדֶׁש.” Shabbos Kodesh.

But she’s not even an adult yet! She doesn't need to work. And she can’t stop thinking about it.

Exactly! I drive this taxi because I need to support my family. But I do it with my hands; I don’t need to do it with my whole head and heart! No, no. We keep our heads and hearts—our main focus and energy—for Hashem, to think and get excited about Torah and mitzvos!

But if I don’t care enough, then I won’t make as much money! There are things that I really want to buy. Like a fancy new watch, presents for my friends’ birthdays, and—

Wait, wait, wait. Your thoughts and ideas have taken you on a wild ride. Here is where you are confused: It is not our hard work that brings in the money.

No way. My parnassa comes straight from Hashem! He has already decided how much I will have. There is no need for me to spend my energy worrying about it.

So that means I should just babysit responsibly, and then not think about it the rest of the day? Keep my thoughts focused on Torah and mitzvos?
