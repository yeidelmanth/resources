chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Parshas Vayikra talks about robbers. It says, “וְהֵׁשִיב אֶת הַגְזֵלָה אֲׁשֶר גָזָל” That means that robbers have to return whatever they stole.

It’s okay Basya, the robber can’t hurt you through a phone call.

Okay, I’m calling a robber who had to return what he stole...

Aaah! You scary Yetzer Hara! What are you doing here? Yossi was dialing a robber!

But of course, I am the best robber there is. I am the king of robbers! I invented robbery!

What do I steal? I steal from Hashem! I steal all of the kedusha that I can!

But you don’t like kedusha! Kedusha is for Torah and mitzvos. You like kelipa. Kelipa hides Hashem. So why do you steal kedusha?

I steal it because I don’t like it! I steal all of the kedusha, and I make it into kelipa! Where do you think kelipa came from in the first place? Everything Hashem makes can be used for kedusha. But then I come and steal it! I make people use it for not good stuff. Mwahahaha!

Simple. When I see someone about to do a mitzvah, I take it away! I stop them! Like if someone is about to make a bracha on an apple, which would then become kedusha, I tell them not to because “people are watching”, or something silly like that.

Then, when they don’t say the bracha, the apple becomes mine! It’s now kelipa! I’m the best robber ever!

Oh, I steal lots of other things. I can even steal time. Sometimes I see that someone has free time and they’re going to use it to learn Torah, so I tell them to do something else instead. Then, bingo! The time is now mine! I stole it from Hashem!

Silly Yetzer Hara! Hashem sees you stealing from Him! Hashem is always watching!

Hey, that’s what the passuk is talking about! Instead of using the word “ּגְנֵבָה”, which means to steal when no one is watching, the Torah calls it “גְזֵלָה”. G’zeila is stealing by force, right in front of the owner’s eyes.

Well, we’re not going to let you do that anymore. You’re never going to steal any mitzvos from me! Never ever again! You’re gonna go out of business and fall off the planet forever and ever!

Oh, but it’s too late. I already stole a lot from Hashem. What are you gonna do about that? Huh?

We’re gonna get it back from you. We’re gonna get all of the kedusha back by using everything in the world for Hashem, until there’s no kelipa left!

Yup, and Hashem is going to help us. We’re going to work so hard until you, Yetzer Hara, are the poorest thing ever!

Then everything will be kedusha, and the Geulah will be here! Yay!

Arrrggg… So, chayolim, think about it: How will you return everything that I, the Yetzer Hara, tries to steal?

…and that’s another fabulous episode of the Parsha Podcast. Mr. Yetzer Hara, thank you for joining us! We’ll be sure to think the question over. See you all chayolim next week!
