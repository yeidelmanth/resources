One Sunday, our family friend Chaya was preparing for her flight, which was just a week away. But when she started packing, she saw that her passport had expired!

They won’t let me on my flight with an expired passport, she realized. I must renew it!

Usually, passport appointments are scheduled far in advance. Chaya searched online for an urgent passport appointment, but all the ones in New York were booked. There was only one appointment available—in Boston.

Boston? She thought in despair. That’s a few hours away! If I go there, I’ll have to miss a whole day of teaching.

Chaya loved teaching her class and didn’t want to miss school. She needed help! Right away, Chaya went on the Ohel website and began to type: Rebbe, please give me a bracha to get my passport renewed in time, without missing school.

Later that day, Chaya heard of a lady who helps people get urgent passport appointments. But when Chaya texted her, the woman responded, I don’t work on Sundays. Message me tomorrow, and I’ll try to help.

Chaya’s heart sank. She couldn’t wait until Monday morning to find out if the lady could help her, because that’s when the appointment in Boston was.

It looks like I’ll have to miss class after all, she sighed, booking the appointment in Boston.

Just a little while later, Chaya’s phone buzzed. It was a message from the lady. I found an appointment for you in New York: Tuesday at 9 am.
