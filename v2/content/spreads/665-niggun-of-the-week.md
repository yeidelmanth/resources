<h3>Victory March</h3>

Everyone knows that Tzvi loves Napoleon’s March. Last year, he sat at the keyboard for hours each day till he figured it out perfectly. And a few months ago, he had the brilliant idea to program the tune onto his alarm clock. Since then he's been out of bed on time, every time!

Napoleon’s March! The Rebbe said, ‘This is how an army is raised.’”

"How did you know it would work?" asks Esther Miriam.

Tzvi bangs his chest. “Every morning, my yetzer hara tells me not to get up. But with Napoleon's march, I’m on my feet before he wakes up."

"Easy!" says Tzvi. "When the Alter Rebbe first heard Napoleon's March, he said, ‘This is a victory song.’ Then he went into a state of dveikus, meditation. When he woke up, he said, ‘But Didan Natzach—ultimately the victory is ours!’”

Esther Miriam thinks for a moment. “Hey! Why don’t we get a bunch of kids and make a rally to greet Moshiach? We can start with Napoleon’s March.”

Esther Miriam grins. “I like that.”

Tzvi jumps up. “I’m in!"
