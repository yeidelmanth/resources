Hi there! We’re the Levin Family: Basya, Mimi, Chaim and

Yossi—chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Note: These images are for illustrative purposes only and are not meant to be an accurate depiction of the holy faces of the Tzaddikim.

You know the candy man in shul? I think he’s a hidden tzaddik!

Yeah! He for sure has ruach hakodesh! He always gives me red lollipops—and I never even told him that it’s my favorite flavor!

I wish I was a tzaddekes, because then Hashem would love me a lot, and I’d know more about Him.

Mimi! I think you could use a chat with Moshe Rabbeinu’s mother, Yocheved. Yossi, can you bring Yocheved in?

Oh hello, dears! You all look so healthy and beautiful, baruch Hashem. Can I help you with something?

Yes, please! Could you tell Mimi the story from Parshas Shemos, about when you all went down to Mitzrayim?

Sure! There was a hunger, and we needed food. The only place to find food was in Mitzrayim.

Because of Yosef! I learned this in school! Yosef saved food!

Yes! So Grandfather Yaakov Avinu and all of his children went down to Mitzrayim. The Torah says that 70 neshamos entered Mitzrayim. These were the 70 original neshamos of all Yidden, which means that every Yiddishe neshama comes from one of those 70 souls.

Including yours, and including every Yid you know! These neshamos were the founders of our nation. Do you know who was part of this group of 70?

Yes, lots of tzaddikim, including Yaakov Avinu himself! And there was also a baby; a brand new baby named Yocheved—me! The important baby.

Yeah, you were counted as one of the 70 neshamos, just like Yaakov Avinu?! Even though you were just born?

Yes. We were all counted the same. We were all just as important to Hashem. The entire Yiddishe nation wouldn’t be complete even if just one baby was missing!

The Torah says that all Yidden are like Hashem’s children, and Hashem loves us all!

Yes, Hashem loves each and every Yid. But He has a special love for His young children, those who are under Bar and Bas Mitzvah.

Of course you are! In fact, when we finally left Golus Mitzrayim and crossed through the Yam Suf, the children were the leaders.

Yeah! The children were the first ones to say “Zeh keli veanvehu—This is my Hashem!”

The children were the first ones to recognize Hashem. Even before all of the parents and all the Tzaddikim!
