<h3>Forever Lifted</h3>

It’s Chaya’s first year as a teacher.

She is teaching second grade, and she loves her students. But her job is not what she expected!

She knew she’d teach them Chumash, Rashi, Halacha—even Tanya. She wasn’t prepared to teach them about life!

But one boy told her that someone at home wasn’t well, and another asked how to help out a friend with kriah, and a third whispered to her during recess that he wished he could stop fighting with his older brother.

She had to help.

She starts to sing faster and faster. As a teacher, she’s also a shlucha of the Rebbe! She thinks about how Hashem guides the Yidden, and how Moshiach is so close.

Chaya is thankful that she has parents and mashpiim to guide her—and in dealing with her little boys, her brother Mendel too. But sometimes, there is so much to think about that she needs to find a quiet place to sing.

Even if things are challenging now, Hashem will take care of His nation and lift them up— always and forever!

"Hoshiah es amecha..." Chaya

begins slowly and softly.
