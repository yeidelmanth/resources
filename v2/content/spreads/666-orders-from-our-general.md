<p class="lead">After 120 years of building, Noach and his family entered the teiva with all their belongings. Water covered the entire world, but inside, they were safe.</p>

The world nowadays is also flooded, but not with water. The world is covered with ideas that go against Hashem's Torah.

So how do we stay safe? The Baal Shem Tov explains that just like Noach, Hashem gives every Yid the protection of a "teiva”.

the words of Torah and of davening." Every Yid can enter their own "teiva”.

How do we go into our “teiva” today?

By ignoring the world and staying quiet.

By entering the holy words of Torah and tefilla.

Did you ever open a sefer and learn out loud, with lots of lebedikeit? You might have felt like you were in a different world. That's because you were not just saying or thinking words; you were actually going inside the Torah! That's how the words can surround you completely, like a safe teiva.

<span class="mc">Every day when we daven, the same thing can happen. We can turn to Hashem with all of our heart. We can sing the tefillos with so much feeling that we get wrapped up inside of them.</span>

When we enter the words of Torah and tefilla, Hashem protects us from any trouble that might go on around us. None of the ideas of the world affect us.

But if we just enter the teiva by ourselves, what will happen to our families and friends? We must invite them to enter the teiva, too! Together, we’ll let the “stormy” Golus pass, and then go out to make the whole world a beautiful home for Hashem.

What is your favorite part of davening to sing out loud?

Can you think of a way to include others in your limmud Torah?
