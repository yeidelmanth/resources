I remember it clearly. It was the night of Tes-Vov Nissan. The night when Yitzchak Avinu would be giving the brachos. The night that would determine the future of the Yidden and, through them, the future of the entire world.

Many years earlier, while she was still expecting Yaakov and Eisav, Rivka had received a nevu’a that she didn’t share with anyone— not even Yitzchak.

You see, these were no ordinary brachos. They were specially made for the next leader after Yitzchak—the one chosen to carry on the holy shlichus of Avraham Avinu.

The nevu’a revealed that “the older will serve the younger.”

Yitzchak was now 123 years old, and it was time for him to pass the brachos along.
