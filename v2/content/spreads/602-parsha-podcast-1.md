chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Look everyone! Our cousin Shmuel made a Brachos Machine!

A Brachos Machine? It looks awesome, but what does it do?

You just take a picture of any food and it tells you what bracha it is.

Oh wow! That is really helpful for anyone who is still learning their brachos.

Yup, and he said we should try it out on our podcast today, and let him know what we think.

Yes! Now, what are we going to tell Shmuel? He’ll be here any minute.

We’ll say that we’re sorry, and we’ll be more careful next time.

And we’ll say a lot of nice brachos to make up for it. Maybe we can even help him build a new one.

We’re so sorry, Shmuel. We’ll be more careful next time.

Your Brachos Machine. We weren’t being very careful with it, so it broke.

We’re really, really sorry, and we’re going to say a lot of nice brachos to make up for it.

But you could’ve just hidden it away and pretended that it never happened! Or, you could’ve said that you found it like that.

But Shmuel, that would be silly! That’s what my Yetzer Hara said to do.

Yeah, mine too. The Yetzer Hara is a scaredy-cat. He can’t admit it when he does something wrong.

And anyway, what would be the point? Hashem sees and hears everything! Hashem knows that we broke it, and He wouldn’t be very happy to see us lie about it.

It’s actually in this week’s parsha. Hashem says "Stay far away from lies!"

I’d rather do what Hashem says way more than any silly advice I may get from my Yetzer Hara.

Wow. That makes all of my efforts worth it. I’m so happy that my experiment worked! You followed exactly what the Rebbe said about this week’s parsha.

I made the Brachos Machine to be very breakable, because I wanted to see how you all do what the Rebbe said. The Rebbe said that Yiddishe children know that Hashem is always watching, so of course they would never even think of lying! You kids told me the truth right away, because you know that Hashem is always watching over us. He sees everything that we do, and is very happy when we stay far far away from lies!

Oh, that’s why you were being really careful with it before! Could we still help you fix it? I want to see how it works.
