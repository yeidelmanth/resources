Harvesting Reb Avraham’s large orchard was a weeks-long affair. Reb Avraham, Reb Azriel, their entire families, Baruch, and a dozen others worked from dawn to dusk picking the fruits, packing them into pails, and loading them onto wagons to be sold in the marketplace.
