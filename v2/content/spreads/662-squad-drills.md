<span class="mc" style="color:#fecd88">Vehalachta Bidrachav Introduction</span>

<span class="mc">HASHEM’S ARMY DIVISIONS IN SHAMAYIM</span>

<span class="mc">Now how did the Yidden know to put those specific symbols on the division flags? Amazingly enough, they saw them on the malachim’s ruchniyus’dik flags during Matan Torah. Yup, malachim have flags too! When the Yidden saw the malachim’s flags, they copied the designs for their own division flags down here.</span>

<span class="mc">Many years later, Yechezkel Hanavi also saw the divisions of malachim, and he tells us what he saw: on each side of the Kisei Hakavod is a symbol that matches the flags in the midbar—a man, a lion, an ox, and an eagle. Four sides, four symbols.</span>

<span class="mc">Our general, the Rebbe, describes it one step further. He tells us that besides for malachim, there are also two special tzaddikim stationed on each side of the Kisei Hakavod. These tzaddikim are the division heads of Tzivos Hashem: Avraham and Sara are in the south, Yitzchak and Rivka in</span>

<span class="mc">THE BATTLE PLAN FOR TZIVOS HASHEM</span>

<span class="mc">All of these details are super cool, but what do the divisions in the midbar and Hashem’s divisions up in Shamayim have to do with you? Well…everything! Because you, too, are among Hashem’s divisions! As soldiers in Tzivos Hashem, we are all part of the most powerful army; an army with divisions, division heads, and a battle plan from our general, the Rebbe.</span>

<span class="mc">The battle plan, as you know, is to conquer the world through Torah and mitzvos. The Rebbe tells us that in order to be successful in our mission, we must follow the lead of our division heads: Avraham &amp; Sara, Yitzchak &amp; Rivka, Yaakov &amp; Leah, and Dovid &amp; Rochel. Each pair of these holy tzaddikim lived with—and therefore, teach us—a certain path in Avodas Hashem. And when you, proud chayol in Tzivos Hashem, march into battle the way our division heads have taught, we will conquer all four directions of the world:</span>
