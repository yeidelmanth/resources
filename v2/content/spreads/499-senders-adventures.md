It’s Reb Sender again. I won’t take too much of your time. On erev Yom Kippur, there isn’t a second

to waste! But I have a little Aseres Yemei Teshuva story for you...

It all started when I was busy frying kreplach. I felt a buzz in my pocket. A young chayol was on the line. “It’s Eli from England,” he said. “We’ve got a crisis. Can you come?”

I hopped onto my kfitza-board, and in a flash I arrived on site in Eli’s classroom. It was empty (well, besides for us).

During Aseres Yemei Teshuva, a time when chayolim should be learning Torah, no one was around. Where could they be?

I turned on my WePhone’s CLD (Chayol Light Detector). You see, when a chayol is doing their job, they leave a trail of light behind them. So if you have a CLD, like I do, chayolim are easy to find.

Satellites showed that the classroom was brightly lit. Eli confirmed that indeed, his classmates learned a lot of Torah

“Come on,” I exclaimed, pointing to the lights. “Let’s find them!”

Eli led the way to the nearby grocery store, where he and his friends go on mivtzoim every Friday. No chayolim there! So we followed the map to the ice cream shop, the next glistening spot on the map. Eli explained that the week before, a four- year-old girl had dropped her ice cream and an older chayol had replaced it.

We checked out a few more pins, including the shul and a nursing home where chayolim frequently visited. But in every place that the blazing trail led us, we still could not find any of his classmates!

Eli looked up at me, his forehead creased in worry. “It must be that our chayolim are not doing their job right!"
