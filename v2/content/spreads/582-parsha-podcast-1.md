Hey guys, who are we calling for this week’s podcast?

Could we bring Yehoshua please? I’m kinda nervous about being in the Parsha Podcast group … I feel like I’m too little for such a big mission! Maybe I can’t do it.

Yehoshua would probably have good advice. He had to take over from Moshe Rabbeinu!

I’m all for it! This week’s parsha says to ask the nevi’im and chachamim when we have questions like this. That would include Yehoshua!

Hello, hello! I guess technology has finally figured out how to reach people from another time and place! Wonderful!

Yes! Welcome Reb Yehoshua! This is the Parsha Podcast, and chayolim around the world are listening. Basya has a question for you.

Yeah, um, I … I was getting nervous about being on the Parsha Podcast, you know, teaching chayolim about the parsha. There are so many people in the world. Do you really think I could make a difference? I mean, did you ever feel hopeless when you had the huge mission to lead the Yidden?

No way! Because I was at Matan Torah. You know what happened at Matan Torah?

Exactly. There are so many other nations in the world; some way bigger and more powerful than us. But Hashem decided that davka the Yidden are the right ones for His special mission.

The mission to spread Hashem’s Torah to the whole world?

You chayolim know your stuff! Yes, and when Hashem chose us to do it, He gave us His special kochos to do it successfully.

And no one can mess with Hashem! We just said that on Rosh Hashana— that Hashem is the King of all kings. That means there’s nothing more powerful than Him!

Yes, and we say it with a simcha, because we know it means that the Yetzer Hara has no power over us!

Umm … but I don’t feel like my Yetzer Hara has no power over me. He seems pretty strong sometimes.

Yeah, like mine almost got me to give up on the Parsha Podcast. Already.

So here’s a tip: Whenever your Yetzer Hara dares to start speaking up, first tell him that his powers were taken away when Hashem chose the Yidden to be His people. Then, go right on with your mission, and he’ll just have to sit there and watch helplessly.

Sounds like a good trick to me! Does it work for you?

It works for all Yidden! In Parshas Ha’azinu, it says, “וה' ּבָדָד יַנְח ֶנּ—Hashem guided the Yidden alone”, and then “ל נֵכָר-ֵוְאֵין עִּמֹו א—there were no enemy powers with them.”

That means that the Yetzer Hara had no power over the Yidden then?
