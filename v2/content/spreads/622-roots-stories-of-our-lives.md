“Ah, Devorah Leah’le. All of Hashem’s creations are divided into different categories, each with a different general nature. People, too, are divided into two general groups: men and women. This difference is not just physical—that a woman looks different than a man—but also spiritual. A man’s nature tends to be more firm, and a woman’s nature tends to be more gentle.

firm and being gentle, have advantages. Being more firm and strict means being strong in one’s opinion and not giving up. Being gentle is also good. It could mean, for example, being understanding of other people’s views and not getting upset easily. Both traits are important, but in certain situations, one can be more useful than the other. When it comes to learning, being firm has an advantage over being gentle. That is why Rabi Eliezer says that it is better to teach Torah only to boys, and not to girls.
