After Shabbos, Zeidy told Mariela and her husband, “Mrs. Kadosh wants to see you.”

They went back to her house, and she greeted them with a warm smile. “Come inside,” she said. “I want to tell you a story about the Rebbe that I think is really special for you.”

Once they were seated comfortably, Mrs. Kadosh began her story. “One time, I was standing in line to receive a dollar from the Rebbe. When it was my turn, the Rebbe gave me two dollars that were stuck together. When I realized what had happened, I thought it was a mistake.

“I went back and told Rabbi Groner that I had gotten a second dollar by mistake, because they were stuck together.

Mrs. Kadosh looked at them kindly. “When you left my house after the meal, I thought of this story that happened to me so many years ago. You just got married and are starting your life together, so I decided to give you these two dollars that came together.”

It was already after Gimmel Tammuz. Did they miss their chance to get a dollar from the Rebbe?

Our Bubby and Zeidy are shluchim at the El Lazo in Argentina. Over the years, they’ve helped hundreds of Yidden learn about Torah and mitzvos.

One special couple they helped was Mrs. Mariela Preter and her husband Marcelo, who became frum through the Chabad House after Gimmel Tammuz.

Shortly after Mariela and her husband got married, Zeidy brought them to Crown Heights to visit 770 and the Ohel. When Shabbos came, he arranged for them to eat the seudahs in the home of Mrs. Malka Kadosh, who knew Spanish.

Baruch Hashem, they had a wonderful time at her home. They were amazed to hear that before Gimmel Tammuz, Mrs. Kadosh would go every Sunday to get dollars and brachos from the Rebbe. She had plenty of inspiring stories to share, and Mariela and her husband were excited to hear them.

But at the same time, they felt a little sad. They had only known about the Rebbe after Gimmel Tammuz, when they couldn’t see the Rebbe in person. How they wished that they could have gotten a dollar from the Rebbe, too!
