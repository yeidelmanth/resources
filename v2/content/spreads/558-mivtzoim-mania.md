“We checked the whole house, until we finally found them in the playroom. The bigger kids must have taken them out when they were playing, and hadn’t put them back.

“We immediately returned the Chitas and pushka to their proper place in the baby’s room, and guess what? He slept normally again!”

Mommy was amazed to hear this story—not just because it’s an incredible miracle story, but because she and Tatty were having a similar problem with me! During the past few nights, I had been waking up a lot and wasn’t getting proper sleep. Mommy and Tatty didn’t know what the problem was, and they were starting to get worried. Hearing this story, however, gave Mommy an idea.

As soon as she got home, she checked my room to see if my Chitas and Pushka were there. Sure enough, the Chitas wasn’t in its place! You see, my older brothers and sisters have a beautiful leather siddur that they daven from everyday. I wanted my siddur—my Chitas— to be with theirs, on the living room shelf.

Mommy explained to me that the Rebbe wants each of us to have a Cheder Tzivos Hashem, not just a plain old bedroom. Part of that includes always keeping a Chitas in the room.

Of course, I happily brought the Chitas back, and what do you know—Baruch Hashem, I’m back to sleeping through the night.
