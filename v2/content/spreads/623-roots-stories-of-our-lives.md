“Mamme, I’m scared.” Young Rochel whimpered as she toddled into her parents’ bedroom. She was dressed in a nightgown, her eyes bleary and puffy.

Dina\* opened her arms, and Rochel ran right into them, burying her head in her mother’s lap. Rochel covered her small ears, no doubt trying to drown out the terrible noises coming from the apartment above. The ceiling trembled and creaked, and high-pitched screams and drunken laughter mixed with the sound of stomping feet.

But the sounds only got louder as the night went on. Dina and Rochel slept uneasily, dozing off here and there when sleep overcame them, only to be woken up again by the noises a few minutes later. It was almost dawn by the time it was finally quiet.

Dina left her home just after sunrise. On her way out, she met Elka\* the widow, who lived on the bottom floor.

“Good morning, Elka. I was wondering—did you also have trouble sleeping last night?”

Dina nodded. “Yes, Rochel and I were up all night.”
