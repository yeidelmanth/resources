She had asked for a bracha for her sister, not for herself!

She replayed the Rebbe’s words in her mind again and again, trying to figure out what they meant. Somehow, she felt from the Rebbe’s bracha that first she would be bentched, and then her sister's bracha would come.

Sure enough, almost exactly nine months later, our mother, Bubby's second daughter, was born.

But the story is not over: exactly one year after our mother’s birth, Bubby's sister had her own baby girl.

\*\*\* For many years, our family had been looking for the JEM video of that Sunday dollars, so we could see Bubby asking for the bracha and hear the Rebbe’s response.

To our disappointment, Bubby couldn't remember which day it had been. We tried looking for the video anyway, but weren’t able to find it.

When we wanted to publish this story in Hachayol Magazine, we decided to try to find the video once more so we could write the story exactly as it happened. We contacted JEM and asked them to search for it.

When Bubby asked for a bracha for her sister, the Rebbe’s answer took her by surprise. What did the Rebbe mean?

Bubby bounced her one-year-old baby on her knee, smiling with delight as he clapped and giggled along.

Bounce, bounce, bounce. Her baby giggled once more, but this time, Bubby just sighed. She was thinking of her older sister, who had been married for a few years without any children.

It’s so hard for her that she doesn’t have a baby yet, Bubby thought sadly. I wish there was something I could do to help.

Suddenly, she had an idea. There was a way she could help!

The next Sunday that she was in Crown Heights, Bubby woke up early and hurried to 770. There was a long line of people stretching down the block. Everyone had come to get a dollar and bracha from the Rebbe.

Bubby waited patiently as the line slowly inched forward. Before she knew it, she was standing in front of the Rebbe. The Rebbe gave her baby a dollar, and then gave one to her.

“A bracha for children,” Bubby said, telling the Rebbe her sister’s name.

The Rebbe gave her another dollar. “That dollar was for you, and this one is for your husband. You should hear good news.”
