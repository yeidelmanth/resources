Zeidy got right to work, and the mesivta began that first year with fourteen bachurim. Until a proper dorm was set up, the bachurim slept in Zeidy’s basement. His days became extra busy as he juggled his two big jobs.

Baruch Hashem, with the Rebbe’s brachos, the mesivta flourished. Today, almost thirty years later, Yeshivas Ohr Eliyahu Lubavitch Mesivta has grown into an incredible source of nachas for the Rebbe. What started with a group of fourteen students has grown into a beautiful yeshiva of 175, with hundreds of former students now on shlichus around the world.

When Zeidy was making a big decision, he turned to the Rebbe—and the Rebbe’s encouragement made it all

All of Zeidy’s worries and doubts vanished in an instant.

Our Zeidy Perlstein was the principal of the boys’ cheder in Chicago for many years. In 1997, when his oldest son finished eighth grade, it was time to continue on to mesivta. But where would he go? At the time, there was no Chabad mesivta in Chicago, and all the bochurim would leave town for school.

Zeidy realized that there was a big need for a mesivta in Chicago. He dreamed of opening one with a high level of learning in both niglah and chassidus, with a strong focus on hiskashrus and the Rebbe’s inyonim.

But Zeidy was very torn. On one hand, he thought that opening a mesivta in Chicago was the right thing to do. On the other hand, he was already the principal of the boys’ cheder.

How could he take on such a big responsibility when he was already so busy?

He thought about the idea for a while until, finally, he decided to go ahead with it.

The next time he went to the Ohel, Zeidy wrote about his plan to open the mesivta and asked for the Rebbe’s brachos.
