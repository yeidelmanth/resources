Army Strategy

Vehalachta Bidrachav Introduction

<span class="mc">FOCUSED STRATEGY</span>

Once in a while, an army may focus their manpower and weaponry in one direction. The purpose is to make an all- out attack on one specific enemy, and/or to strengthen one specific border.

In Hashem’s army, we too make focused attacks on specific aspects of our enemy, the Yetzer Hara, thereby strengthening one specific border.

IN THE REBBE’S OWN WORDS

<span class="mc" style="color:#b56a10">\[Because\] just as in every army, when a major attack is launched on one front, the other sides are not left open and vulnerable, chas v’sholom. Rather, every side is continuously guarded, even though the main attack is on one specific front.</span>

...We \[also\] see that in every army, the physical needs of the soldiers are supplied by the army, as directed by the commander-in-chief. The soldiers’ food and drink, clothes, and sleeping quarters are all taken care of.

In Tzivos Hashem as well, by fighting the war against the Yetzer Hara, Hashem will bless you and your parents that you will have everything you need. You will be healthy, happy and strong, and you will have food, drink, and a brightly-lit home, so that you are able to win the war even better and faster.”
