Hi there! We’re the Levin Family: Basya, Mimi, Chaim and

Yossi—chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Chaim, who’s our guest for today? No one told me who it’s gonna be.

Well, we were going to have this farmer call in, but Yossi said he had a better idea. He even got special permission from Mommy and Tatty.

See? I can do things responsibly. Anyway, look outside.

It’s not in the house. It’s in the backyard! And it’s just a calf. I tried, but Mommy didn’t let us bring the whole farm.

Awww, that little baby cow is really cute. Can I pet it?

I think we should record the podcast first and then you can go pet the calf.

Okay. Calf, what message do you have for us about the parsha?

Sorry, I don’t speak cow. Chaim, what does a farm have to do with our Parsha?

Because I was born first! In this week's Parsha Hashem says that a firstborn fruit, animal, or person, is a bechor. The bechor belongs to Hashem, and we should make it kadosh, by putting it aside for Him.

Wow! It sounds like a really special mitzva. I wish I was a bechor.

Exactly! That’s why I wanted to bring a farm! It’s not fair that Chaim gets to do the mitzva of bechor just cuz he was born first! I also want to do the mitzva.

Oh come on, Yossi! We don’t need to bring in a whole smelly farm for you to be like a bechor! Everything in Torah has a lesson for everyone! Even if you don’t own any animals.

Well the first born is all about being new. Did you know that you’re like a new person every morning?

It’s a new day, with a renewed neshamah! You’re like a newly-born person every morning.

Yeah, we say that in Modeh Ani every morning, right when we wake up!

Oh! A rooster! We should get a rooster to wake us up!

Because we don’t need a farm! Just like the bechor is kadosh for Hashem, everyone can make every new day special for Hashem. Starting with our first words, Modeh Ani, when we thank Hashem for a new day.

So we start our new day with something kadosh, and that’s how we do the mitzvah of bechor?

Yeah! Each day, the first thing we think and say is something kadosh. We start our day saying thank you to Hashem. Then we continue thanking Him the whole day.
