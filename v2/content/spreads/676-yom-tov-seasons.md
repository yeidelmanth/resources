Since the Rebbe's takana more than 40 years \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ .

Learn more: Likkutei Sichos, vol. 27, pp. 229–236; Sichas Shabbos

Mishneh Torah teaches mitzvos in a very thorough way,

While Sefer Hamitzvos is more concise, that’s clear.

Every man, woman, and child should learn their track each day,

And most tracks complete the cycle each and every \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ !

Very soon, Hashem's knowledge will fill the whole land,

Since it is now the Rambam’s yartzeit and just before this

year’s Siyum HaRambam, it’s a good time to look back at

In תשד״מ )1984(, the Rebbe called upon Yidden worldwide

to unite through studying all the halachos of the Torah together, at the same pace. When we all study the same part

of Torah each day, it creates a powerful sense of achdus

throughout Klal Yisroel. The only sefer that includes every halacha is the Rambam!

Through Mishneh Torah or Sefer Hamitzvos, men, women, and children can each take part in this special limmud. By learning all of Torah’s halachos together, we strengthen our achdus and bring Moshiach closer.
