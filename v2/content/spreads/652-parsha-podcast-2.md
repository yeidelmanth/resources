chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Do you guys ever get bored of trying to be, you know, better people?

Yes! I mean, of course I want to be the best Tzivos Hashem soldier I can be, but sometimes I feel like I’ve just been trying so hard for so long. I get tired.

I think I know what you mean. It’s a tough job to keep fighting the Yetzer Hara.

I can’t believe that you guys are worried about that. We have bigger problems on hand! Like the fact that our only guest option for this week is an old lady! I thought old people just sit around and rest all day.

What? No way! I love older people! They are so wise and helpful!

And don’t forget, Yossi, it’s important to respect elders. In fact, it’s in this week's Parsha: ,ם"ִמְִּפְֵנֵי ֵׂשׂיָבָה ָּתּקּו "ַרְּת ָ ּפ ְנֵי זָקֵן וְה ָד, we kids need to stand up and respect older people.

Ah, a sheineh yingeleh! Your parents must get lots of nachas from you! Vat vould you like me to tell you? You are a soldier of Hashem! You are also vise!

Hmm. But you’ve been living for a really long time, right? You must know what life is all about.

Yeah, you probably have worked very hard for so many years to become a real Tzivos Hashem soldier! How did you do it?

How did I do it? I didn’t do it. I am doing it. I vork very hard every day to be a good soldier! Dis doesn’t stop ven you are out from school. A Yid is alvays a soldier for Hashem! I love Hashem and I vant to do His mitzvos in de best vay!

Well, we were just saying that sometimes, it gets tiring to keep working on our avodah.

Yeah, and you’ve been alive for a lot longer than we have! How are you not bored yet?

Vat zeeseh kinderlach you are! But you have it backvards. It goes ze other vay around. “ר ֹוב ׁש ָנִים י ֹודִיעּו חָכְמָה— Many years give visdom.” As ve grow older, our visdom grows. Every year I live, I gain more experience and more chayus in my mission. Over my time on zis world, I have seen so many tings, and learned so much from dem, now I have a greater love for Hashem dan ever!

Wow, so I guess it’s time to come to terms with ourselves not being perfect.

Definitely! Serving Hashem means constantly growing— and constantly struggling.

Dat’s right! Dere’s noting boring about becoming a better person.

Just imagine: we’ll look back in fifty years from now and be so proud of how far we’ve come.

And then we can be the guest on some kids’ Parsha Podcast, and share our experience with them!

And now, let’s ask all de oder chayolim to tink about it: How will you make sure to keep growing?

…and that’s another fabulous episode of the Parsha Podcast. Thank you, Wise Bubby, for joining us! We’ll be sure to think the question over. See you all chayolim next week!
