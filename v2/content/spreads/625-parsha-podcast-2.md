chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Ooh cookies! Why do we have black and white cookies?

Yeah! Because Hashem created light and dark! See? These cookies have a light side, and a dark side!

Thanks Mimi and Basya! How about we save them for after we talk to our guest? That way we won’t have our mouths full while we’re recording.

Yeah, I don’t want my conversation with Adam HaRishon to be interrupted by a mouthful of cookie crumbs. I’ll call him now.

Hello to you all! I hope it’s not too noisy on my end. There are a lot of curious animals who want to join in on the call.

Wow. That does sound like a lot of animals. Did Hashem really make all of them from just … nothing?

Yup! Hashem made them all! ”“אֵת הַשָׁמַיִם וְאֵת הָאָרֶץ—Hashem made everything in the heavens, and everything on the earth.

But I made those cookies. With Mimi. All by ourselves. We made them for the parsha.

Ah, yes. You did make those cookies. You took the flour and sugar, eggs and oil, and all the rest of the ingredients which were created by Hashem. And then, with His help, you made cookies. In fact, by making those cookies, you’re doing your mission!

What mission? Making cookies isn’t one of the missions on my Tzivos Hashem checklist.

What I want to know is why Hashem made all of this in the first place. Why did Hashem make that giant tree in our backyard, and this phone, and those dark stormy clouds outside that are definitely going to bring thunder and lightning?

He made it all for you! For you and for the Torah. Hashem tells that to us in the first word of the Torah. If you split the word “תיִּבְרֵאׁש” in half, then you get ”תיִ“ּב' רֵאׁש which means “two firsts”.

Oh yeah! Hashem created the world for the two most important things— the Yidden, and the Torah!

Yup. He made everything so that you, a Yid, can use it in the Torah way! For example, you can sit in the shade of that tree and learn with friends.

And we can cut some of its branches for our sukkah every year! And we use this phone for our Parsha Podcast, and, and, and, when there’s thunder and lightning, we get to say two whole brachos!

Right! We just learned in school that we say a bracha on lighting for Hashem creating everything, and on thunder for Hashem being so powerful.

So, Adam Harishon, you’re saying that our mission is to use all of this stuff to serve Hashem?

Yes, and also much more than that! Your mission is to use everything for Hashem and to make sure that every single person knows that Hashem created the world, too!

Well, by using everything you see in the Torah way! That’s how you’ll show everyone what it was all created for. ”ר“וִיהִי אֹו! Light up the world! When you light up your surroundings with Torah and mitzvos, then everyone else can clearly see the truth of what it is and what it’s meant to be.
