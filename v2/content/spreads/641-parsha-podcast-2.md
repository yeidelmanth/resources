chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Yisro is really fascinating. Did you know that he travelled all around the world to find the truth? He wanted to know who really ran the world!

And did you know that he was super rich? He was the ruler of Midyan! He must’ve had a massive palace!

Yeah, but when he stopped worshipping idols, the people in Midyan weren’t very nice to him. So that probably wasn’t fun.

Good point. I wonder if it was really worth giving up all his fancy stuff to go wander in the desert with the Yidden.

Yisro, you left all your fancy mansions and expensive stuff to go join the Yidden in the desert. Was that really worth it?

Yeah, I can’t believe you did that! The desert is scary, with snakes and scorpions, and it’s boiling hot, and you can’t even bring your comfortable palace with you! You just have to live in a silly old tent.

No, no, no, children. I didn’t just randomly decide to run off into the desert! My son-in-law is Moshe Rabbeinu. I went to join him and accept the Torah from Hashem! Of course that is worth it! Without a question!

But still, that must’ve been a really difficult move! You left your whole big palace behind to wander in the desert. You’re amazing!

No, no, no, those kids are amazing. They were the first ones to notice all of the miracles that Hashem was doing! The first to realize that it was all a build up for Yetzias Mitzrayim and Matan Torah! Yes, I moved to the desert to join the Yidden, but the children were the ones who got excited right away! They’re the real deal!

Wouldn’t it make more sense for the older people or the talmidei chachamim to notice the build up to Matan Torah first?

Because it happened so differently than the way you’re imagining it. Of course, the talmidei chachamim were excited, but compared to those kids I saw, it was nothing!

The Yiddishe kids in the desert. Even the ones in Mitzrayim. They realized before anyone that all of the miracles were from Hashem. They knew that Hashem wanted to take them to Har Sinai and give them the Torah—and they were so excited! I’ve never seen such chayus anywhere else! They were counting down every moment until they could serve Hashem with Torah and mitzvos.

I never realized that the kids weren’t just on the sidelines, but actually had a central and important place in the lead-up to Matan Torah. That’s empowering for all of us.

Yeah! I wanna serve Hashem just like the first kids in Hashem’s Army did before Yetzias Mitzrayim! I’m gonna make a hachlata tova to learn with more chayus, and when we go to shul on Shabbos, I’ll concentrate on every word of kriah. That way, we’ll have the final Geulah, and Moshiach will come right away!

Okay guys, we’d better go and rally up our troops! This Parshas Yisro, all of us kids will be right there, listening to the Aseres Hadibros with a smile and a hachlata tova, which will bring Moshiach down here as fast as possible!

Yes! Chayus like the kids in the desert! And now, let’s ask all the other chayolim to think about it: What hachlata tova can you take on to show your excitement for Torah?

…and that’s another fabulous episode of the Parsha Podcast. Thank you, Yisro, for joining us! We’ll be sure to think the question over. See you all chayolim next week!
