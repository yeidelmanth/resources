No way. I don’t want to grow up. Grown- ups don’t get to play with LEGOs.

What in the world does this have to do with the parsha? Let’s get started with the podcast, guys. Yossi, who are we calling today?

Umm, any of the shevatim. Your pick. Actually—wait! Yehuda! I vote Yehuda!

Yehuda is really fascinating. All kings come from Yehuda.

We can hear you, but not so well. There’s a lot of noise in the background.

PRECISELY! LET ME STEP AWAY FOR A MINUTE. Ah, here we go. It should be much quieter now.

We’re doing expansions on a yeshiva. It’s really important for everyone to have a place to learn Torah.

Oh, you’re the one whom Yaakov sent to build a yeshiva in Mitzrayim!

Yup! Before my family came to Mitzrayim, my father told me to go ahead so that I could get the yeshiva ready.

But why did you set it up before anyone even got there? Couldn’t you wait until you at least had houses to live in?

Good question! It’s because we Yidden were moving to this new place, and we must make sure that before we move into a new place, it’s already a place of Torah.

Because Yidden always need to be learning Torah. We can’t exist without it!

Wow! That was probably the fastest we’ve ever learned a lesson on the Parsha Podcast! Yidden need Torah! Awesome!

Wait, not so fast. I’m still confused— Yehuda, why did you have to go build the yeshiva? Yosef was already there in Mitzrayim. He could’ve done it!

Aha! Good question. Let me tell you something about my brother Yosef. He’s a great tzaddik, and on a very high level. Yosef knows a lot of Torah, and makes time to learn every day. But he’s a busy man—second to Paroh himself. Most of the day, he’s busy running the country. Yosef really wants to spend all of his time learning Torah, but unfortunately, he can’t.

Oh, but you and your brothers and Yaakov Avinu are all shepherds. That’s not nearly as busy as being second-to-the-king!

You’re right. We can sit and learn Torah all day, with barely any distractions. That’s just about all we do, in fact. So really, you could say that our job is learning Torah.

So that’s why you got to build the yeshiva and not Yosef? Because the sheep won’t bother you?

You’re on the right track. It’s because a yeshiva is a place where talmidim can learn without being distracted, so it should be built by someone who does just that.
