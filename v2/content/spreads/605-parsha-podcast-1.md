So what are we going to learn from Parshas Ki Sisa?

I don’t know, but did anyone realize that we haven’t even brought Moshe Rabbeinu yet?!

Y’know Mimi, Moshe asked Hashem for something very interesting in this week’s parsha.

Okay, guys, so while you were all talking, I got the number to call Moshe Rabbeinu. Here goes!

Huh? I mean, thanks for the “yasher ko’ach”, but I don’t get it.

I asked Hashem that "אִם מָצָאתִי חֵן בְּעֵינֶיָך— if I have found favor in His eyes,” then, “וְנִפְלִינּו אֲנִי וְעַּמְָך מִּכָל הָעָם אֲׁשֶר עַל ּפְנֵי הָאֲדָמָה— I and the Yidden would be kept special and different from all the other people in the world.”

I’m guessing that Hashem said yes, because we’re pretty different.

Yeah. For one, I don’t see any goyim dressing like me!

And I think we’re the only ones who celebrate Shabbos each week!

That’s right! We Yidden are special in every detail of our lives. We make everything we do special for Hashem. Like you both said, even our clothes and weekly schedules are different from the rest of the world. We live our lives the Yiddishe way.

We say and know that Hashem made everything, “from the heavens to the earth!"

Yes. And so every detail—from tying our shoes and cutting our nails to the way we walk, talk, and eat! —we do for Hashem.

Once we were in the airport at mincha time, and everyone was staring at us while we davened. I felt so proud to be a Yid!

That’s wonderful! Chayolim, it is amazing how you remain different even now, while you are in golus and surrounded by goyim! And you do it with such pride and simcha! Just wonderful!

But there are a lot of Yidden nowadays who don’t know how to live their life like Hashem wants. They don’t even know that we are special and different in the first place!

And some Yidden are scared. They say that the goyim won’t like us if we act differently.

Well, it’s our shlichus to show all Yidden how special we are! We can act like Yidden, no matter what the rest of the world says or does!

Remember, I asked Hashem, "If I find favor in Your eyes, then we will be separate." Hashem agreed to help us be separate from all others, and when we are, then the rest of my request is agreed to as well: we’ll find favor in Hashem’s eyes.

And then the people all around us will respect us and help us to serve Hashem!
