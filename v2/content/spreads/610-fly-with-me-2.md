What happened in Mitzrayim and Har Sinai we recall.

A strong connection to children these events all share.

And looking back, we’ll see how it’s a lesson for us \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ .

The children in Mitzrayim kept their bitachon strong,

Despite the harsh decrees that were targeted at them.

They learned and kept the mitzvos; they knew it wouldn’t be long!

And they were first, by Kri’as Yam Suf, to point and see \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ .

“ּבָנֵינּו עֲרֵבִים ּבַעֲדֵנּו!” the Yidden said,

Today, towards our own Geulah we look straight ahead,

When counting Sefiras Haomer, we say “Day one, day two,”—

Holding on to what was done, and adding more and more.

Building on the levels that we’ve already reached \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ . 4
