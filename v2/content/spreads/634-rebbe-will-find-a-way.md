Now, more than ten years later, Tante Rivkah is completely healthy, Baruch Hashem, with no side effects at all.

When everyone was so worried and afraid, the Rebbe helped them to strengthen their bitachon, saving Tante Rivkah’s life!

We feel so lucky to have a Rebbe who we can turn to in times of need. We know that he is with us!

Our Mommy’s sister Tante Rivkah had been having headaches for a few days. But she was so busy taking care of her baby, who also wasn’t feeling well, that she didn’t pay any attention to it.

One Friday, she put her baby in the stroller and opened the door, ready to go to work. Right then, she felt a massive pain in her head.

It felt like the world was spinning. She quickly laid down on the floor and called out for help.

A passerby called Hatzalah, and she was whisked away to the hospital. There, the doctors found that she had a very serious aneurysm that was causing bleeding near her brain.

At this point, Tante Rivkah couldn’t move the left side of her body. The doctors didn’t think she would live, and if she did, they said that she would never be fully healthy again.

Everyone was very worried. Yidden around the world davened for her.

At the time, Mommy and her sister Pessel were away in high school. They wrote a letter to the Rebbe, describing how serious the situation was. The doctors think that Rivkah

won’t survive, they wrote. Please bentch her with a refuah shleima.

Afterwards, they opened up Igros Kodesh and read the letter that was on the page.

The Rebbe’s words were: “Doctors are just tools in the hands of Hashem. Strengthen your bitachon in Hashem, the true Healer, and everything will be okay.”

Mommy and Pessel were relieved to see such a clear answer from the Rebbe. They were sure that Tante Rivkah would have a miracle. That Shabbos, knowing that “simcha poretz geder,” they gathered their friends to dance!

On Motzei Shabbos, the doctors decided to do a very serious surgery. It could cause Tante Rivkah to be unwell for the rest of her life, but it was also the only solution they could think of.
