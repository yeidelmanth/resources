This week we are ready to fight! We plan our attack by learning a different way from Rivka Imeinu.

We tell our Yetzer Hara that all Yidden have a neshama inside. Our job is not to judge the other person’s chitzoniyus appearance or behavior.

Rivka teaches us to be value-seeking, which means

<span class="mc" style="color:#cf8715">The way we attack our enemy, the Yetzer Hara, is by putting positive and true thoughts into our heads. We tell our Yetzer</span>

<span class="mc" style="color:#cf8715">Hara that Hashem is the one who takes care of all our needs, and the best way to take care of myself is by ONLY doing what Hashem wants. Those thoughts grow into good feelings that will lead into even better actions.</span>

looking for the good in others. Even if someone appears like an “Eisav,” we can remember that deep down, they’re really a “Yaakov” like us—a precious Yid with a precious neshama. When we see our fellow Yidden for who they truly are, we can find rich value in every Yid.
