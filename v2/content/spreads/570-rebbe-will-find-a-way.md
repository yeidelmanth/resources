Rebbe handed him another dollar and said, “For the children that need to be born.” Zeidy was so surprised! After all, he hadn’t even mentioned that Bubby was expecting.

When Bubby’s mother, Mrs. Miriam Popack, heard what the Rebbe had said, she called Bubby in Eretz Yisroel and asked, “Are you having twins? The Rebbe said ‘children’, in plural!”

But Bubby wasn’t expecting twins. What did the Rebbe mean?

Bubby soon gave birth to a healthy baby boy and continued wondering about the Rebbe’s bracha.

Over the next few years, until she was well into her forties, Bubby gave

birth to four more healthy children— until her family reached a grand total of fifteen children ka”h!

Today, the five children born from the Rebbe’s bracha feel the special z’chus to have a dollar from the Rebbe, even though most of them were born after Gimmel Tammuz.

The Rebbe’s words are exact, and no matter when or how, they will be fulfilled. This means that when the Rebbe said that our generation is the last generation of Golus and the first of Geulah, we can be sure that it’s true!

Bubby wasn’t expecting twins. What did the Rebbe mean when he said “children” in plural?

In תשל”ו (1976), Crown Heights was abuzz with excitement. The Rebbe had announced that he would be sending a group of shluchim to Tzfas and Yerushalayim!

Bubby and Zeidy eagerly submitted their names to the Rebbe, hoping that they would be chosen for this special shlichus.

Imagine their excitement when out of hundreds of other chassidim, they were one of the few selected!

The Rebbe sent Bubby and Zeidy to Tzfas for the first two years of their shlichus. Afterwards, the Rebbe sent them to Migdal Ha’emek, where Zeidy became the shliach and rav.

At the time, the Rebbe was encouraging couples to have as many children as possible, saying, “Every child brings brachos to the family and brings Moshiach sooner!”

Baruch Hashem, Hashem bentched Bubby and Zeidy with a large family. In תנש”א (1991), they were expecting their eleventh child. At that point, Bubby was already almost forty years old.

That Shavuos, when Zeidy was in Crown Heights to spend Yom Tov with the Rebbe, he went by the Rebbe for dollars. Zeidy didn’t know it then, but it would be the last time he’d get dollars before Gimmel Tammuz.
