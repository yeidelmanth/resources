The Story Lives On: That night, on Shmini Atzeres (1977) ”the Rebbe had suffered a serious heart-attack. For the next few weeks, the Rebbe's room in 770 was set up as a mini-hospital, and doctors cared for the Rebbe from there. Every Chossid turned over heaven and earth to ensure the Rebbe's full recovery. Imagine the joy, then, when the Rebbe was able to go home on Rosh Chodesh Kislev. This day became a yom tov for us as Chassidim, and each year we make a seudas hoda’ah to thank Hashem for giving the Rebbe kochos to continue his holy avoda.
