chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Last year, at the seder, I stayed up until eleven o’clock!

I learned about it, too. He was at a seder in Bnei Brak with Rabi Eliezer, Rabi Yehoshua, Rabi Elazar ben Azarya, and Rabi Tarfon. They were all great tzaddikim.

Exactly, Mimi. They were up all night talking about the miracles of Yetzi’as Mitzrayim, and then suddenly their students came in and said that it’s already time to daven shacharis!

Wow. This story is making me yawn. They must have been so tired!

Hey! Maybe Rabi Akiva could come on our podcast and give us some tips on how to stay up the whole seder night!

Or maybe he could come and tell us why we even need to talk about Yetzi’as Mitzrayim in the first place.

Yes, of course, and also no. By night, I mean Golus. Golus is the night.

Yeah, we’re still in Golus, but we’re really almost out. We’ve done so much to bring Moshiach, he’s going to be here any moment now!

Wonderful! So we must talk about the miracles of Yetzi’as Mitzrayim! All through the night, we talk about them.

Ohhh, I get it! When the Haggadah says that you talk about the miracles of Yetzi’as Mitzrayim “all night long,” it means all through Golus!

Yes. All through this big long Golus, we must talk about the miracles of Yetzi’as Mitzrayim.

But why? I mean, it’s an awesome story and I love frogs, but what does it have to do with this Golus?

Yetzi’as Mitzrayim started the preparation for the final Geulah … so it does have to do with us now.

Yes, it did. As the navi Micha says, “ּכִימֵי ”צֵאתְָך מֵאֶרֶץ מִצְרָיִם—this Geulah will be like Yetzi’as Mitzrayim. Only greater!

There will certainly be wondrous miracles, Yossi. When we talk about Yetzi’as Mitzrayim all through Golus, we can get happy and excited. We know that Hashem will take us out of this Golus as well, just as He promised!

During the seder we are also very happy when we talk about it. The more we talk about it, the happier we get!

…all the way up until the end of the Haggadah, when we get up to Hallel! Then we sing to Hashem about how we’re so happy that He will take us out of Golus, and into Geulah!

Okay, this year I’m going to stay up to say Hallel. I want to be super happy about the Geulah, too!

So, chayolim, think about it: How can you remember that Geulah is coming during the long Golus?
