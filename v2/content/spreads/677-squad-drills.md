As soldiers in Hashem’s Army, we know that every Yid has a precious and loveable neshama inside. But the Yetzer Hara tries to trick us by saying that “what we see is what we get”: the way someone looks and acts is exactly who they are. The sneaky enemy tells us that if a Yid doesn’t do good things, then he’s not good (or at least, he’s not as good as us). The Yetzer Hara wants us to focus on the “חיצוניות — outer layer” and be judgmental (which means “judging negatively”), instead of looking for the Yid’s “פנימיות — inner layer”, their neshama, and judging positively.

<span class="mc" style="color:#d01d30">Recon Mission</span>
