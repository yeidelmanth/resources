Benny ran outside for recess, but stopped on the stairs. He had noticed the new boy, Aizik, standing alone.

He never joins in our games, Benny thought. Maybe it’s because he doesn’t know much English yet. He just arrived here after the war with other refugee boys. He must miss his family back in Europe so much.

“Hey, Aizik,” he called out. “Can you believe it’s almost Chanukah? I already went to the store to choose the new bike I’m getting with my Chanukah gelt! What are you doing with your gelt?”

“Oh, I wish I’d get some,” Aizik said sadly. “I just got a letter that my little sister Golda is very krank and needs penicillin\*

to get better. But my parents can’t afford the medicine.”

During afternoon classes, Benny just couldn’t concentrate. The minute the last bell rang, he gathered his friends and repeated the conversation he’d had with Aizik. “We have to do something!”

“You know how much I wanted that bike, but I think I’m going to give my gelt to Aizik, and if we all chip in…”

Everyone readily agreed. That night, Benny wrote a letter.

I put a blue bicycle on hold in your back room so that I could purchase it with my Chanukah allowance. However, my friends and I have decided to pool our money for a better purpose, so that our classmate Aizik can send medicine to an ill family member in Europe. Please sell the bike to another customer.

Chanukah came. All the boys gathered around Aizik during recess. “This is a present for Golda,” Benny said shyly, stepping forward and handing him a package. Aizik tore it open.

“Penicillin!” Aizik cried out, tears welling in his eyes. “How can I ever thank you?”

“It’s nothing,” the other boys mumbled. They, too, were crying.

That evening, after lighting the menorah, Benny’s father said, “A letter came for you in the mail today.”

Benny opened the letter from Best Brooklyn Bicycles nervously. Were they asking him why he didn't pick up his bike? Had the letter with his instructions about the bicycle gotten lost?

Thank you for your letter in which you told us why you wouldn’t be buying a bike from us. Benny, we think that you and your friends did a remarkable thing and we have decided to keep that bike in the back room—until you can come to the store and claim it as a gift.

P.S. Tell your friends to come with you. We have a present for each one of them. And one for Aizik, too.

“Oh!” Benny exclaimed. “This is the best Chanukah ever!”

\*Nowadays you can easily get this antibiotic from your doctor, but in Aizik and Benny’s time, it was rare and expensive.
