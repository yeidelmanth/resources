Hi there! We’re the Levin Family: Basya, Mimi, Chaim and

Yossi—chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Do you know what my friend said to me in school today? He said that he can eat whatever hechsher he wants, because in Golus it’s too hard to be so careful.

Yeah! At recess, my friend said that Hashem doesn't expect her to be tznius, because we’re not in Eretz Yisroel with the Beis Hamikdash.

I was so confused! He also said that if Hashem wanted us to do Torah and mitzvos, he wouldn’t have put us in Golus.

Oh no! That’s horrible! It seems like the Yetzer Hara came up with a really good trick to stop Tzivos Hashem soldiers from fighting him.

Hmm. I think Yosef Hatzaddik might just be the perfect one to help us out with this.

Everyone is nodding their heads in agreement, so I guess I’ll make the call right away! Just hold on a sec … dialing his number…

Wow! Father told me about the Parsha Podcast! Really amazing work you are all doing!

Yosef Hatzaddik! Is it true that in Golus we don’t have to do mitzvos that are too hard? Only when we’re in Eretz Yisroel with the Beis Hamikdash?

Oy vey! You tell me, is Hashem only in Eretz Yisroel?

No, that’s silly! Hashem created the whole world. Hashem is everywhere!

So it’s also silly to say that Hashem only cares about what we do in Eretz Yisroel! Hashem is watching us and helping us to keep Torah and mitzvos everywhere.

Yeah, like you! You were in golus in Mitzrayim, away from all of your family—and surrounded by goyim. But you didn’t let your Yetzer Hara trick you into doing an aveira!

So what should we tell our Yetzer Hara if he says these silly things?

Tell him about Parshas Mikeitz. “Mikeitz” means “the end”. It talks about the end of my suffering in Mitzrayim. I was sold as a slave into golus, away from my family and in prison for a long time. But then, the end of my suffering came.

When you were called to translate Paroh’s dream! Then you ended up being the second to the king, and ruler of all Mitzrayim!

Exactly. And just like the end of my golus came, the end of yours is coming, too. Very soon you will all be princes and rulers just like I was!

But how can we make the end of Golus come already? I don’t know how to translate dreams.

My geulah didn’t come because I translated Paroh’s dreams. It came because I kept all the mitzvos, even in golus. When Hashem sees you working hard to keep all the mitzvos, then He’ll bring your geulah right away!
