chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

You don’t look too happy about it, though. What’s the matter?

Well, I thought that if I’m going on mivtzoim, I might as well go somewhere nice! Like a fancy business building filled with smart and rich people who I could talk about deep ideas with!

An old age home! What am I going to do there? I mean, old people are really great, but what would I do with them for an hour? I’m not the type to sing and dance for entertainment! This is just so not me. I’m really not looking forward to it.

But Chaim, I’m sure they’ll love you! You’re really kind and patient with me, even though it’s hard for me to understand your big ideas.

I agree. And anyway, let’s get started with the podcast. We can’t just sit and complain all day!

Can we bring in Yaakov Avinu? I feel like he’d understand my problem.

For sure! Especially if he could help you feel better.

Hello, chayolim! Aren’t you smart kinderlach. Indeed, I am watching the sheep.

Zeidy Yaakov Avinu, I don’t understand. Don’t you love learning Torah all day? Watching sheep instead probably makes you feel so sad.

Of course, I love learning Torah. But right now, Hashem wants me to watch sheep, so that’s what I’m doing—and very happily so!

Are you happy because you have so many sheep and that makes you rich?

But … how are you serving Hashem? I thought you were watching sheep!

Kinderlach, tell me. What’s the first passuk in this week’s parsha?

Oooh, I know! We’re learning this parsha in Chumash class! “וַּיֵׁשֶב יַעֲקֹב ּבְאֶרֶץ מְגּורֵי אָבִיו ּבְאֶרֶץ ּכְנָעַן—And Yaakov settled in the land of his fathers, in the land of Cana’an.”

Amazing! Your morah must be proud. But let’s look at the deeper meaning: I settled here so that I can elevate gashmiyus things for my Father in Heaven, Hashem!

Aha. So, you’re serving Hashem by elevating the sparks of kedusha in the gashmiyus things, like sheep.

But, why you? You’re one of the Avos! You love to learn Torah and do holy things! Couldn’t it be someone else’s job to elevate the sparks in things as simple as sheep?

Great question, Chaim. It’s because I am a soldier in Hashem’s Army. I do what Hashem wants me to do. If Hashem sent me to work with sheep, then that’s what I do. And I do it with simcha because this is my special mission from Hashem.

But, do you ever feel silly being a shepherd when you’re such a special person? Does it make you feel less important?
