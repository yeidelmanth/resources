As they thanked the owner of the store, she mentioned that her aging mother was in a nursing

home. “I visit her from time to time, but I wish there was more I could do so she’ll feel less lonely.”

husband promised to help care for her. From that day on, they began visiting the elderly woman often. They would tell her and her Jewish friends stories of the weekly parsha and other uplifting messages, inspiring them to become closer to Hashem.

Not only that, but they also arranged for bochurim to visit her at the old age home every Erev Shabbos. They would bring special gifts before Yomim Tovim, like honey cake for Rosh Hashana, menorahs for Chanukah, and so much more.

The old woman, who had once felt so lonely, now felt deeply cared for. She had a newfound appreciation for her Yiddishkeit, and was able to do many more mitzvos than she had ever done before!

It was clear that the dollar had to be lost for a few days so that this elderly lady and her friends could learn more about Yiddishkeit. Not only did the Rebbe dollar uplift Mariela and her husband, but it continued to uplift the elderly lady and many of her friends.

Mariela and her husband were so happy to once again have both of their Rebbe dollars, until one day…

After Mariela and her husband got dollars from the Rebbe, the impact continued on.

Back in Argentina, Mariela kept her dollar safe in a wallet deep inside her closet. Her husband kept his dollar in his pocket siddur, so he could take it with him wherever he went.

But Mariela felt uncomfortable with his choice. Her husband would take the bus quite often to the courthouse, where he worked as a lawyer, and she was afraid that it might get lost.

“A dollar from the Rebbe is precious,” she would tell him often. “It carries the Rebbe’s brachos with it. Please keep it safe!”

“Don’t worry,” he would reassure her. “It’s safe in my pocket. I already see that by bringing it with me to the courthouse, I’m having much more hatzlacha and winning more court cases!”

But one day, Mariela’s fears came true. “The dollar is gone,” her husband said tearfully. “It’s not in my pocket. I’ve searched my entire office, but it’s nowhere to be found. I’m afraid that it fell out of my pocket on the bus on my way to work…”

Oh no! How would they ever find the dollar? It seemed that it would be lost forever.

A few days later, Mariela’s grandfather called her. “Did you lose a small siddur?” he asked. “Someone called to say that they found it.”

Mariela’s face lit up. “Yes! It was a very important siddur!”

Her grandfather gave her the person’s number, which she dialed right away.

It turned out that the siddur was found by a non-Jewish lady who worked at a leather coat store, which was owned by a Jewish woman. When the worker saw that there were Hebrew words on the siddur, she brought it to her Jewish boss.

Inside the siddur, they found the Rebbe dollar, along with a small notepad with a list of important numbers. She called the number of Mariela’s grandfather, who immediately got in touch with Mariela.

Mariela and her husband went to the coat store right away, where they were reunited with the precious Rebbe dollar.
