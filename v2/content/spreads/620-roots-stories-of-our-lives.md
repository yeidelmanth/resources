Reb Shneur Zalman, Baruch’s father, came to get him, as he did every day. First, they would go to the shul to daven mincha and ma’ariv. Then, they would head home to eat dinner with his mother, Rochel, and his sister, Devorah Leah.

Reb Shneur Zalman’s pale face brightened and his stooped frame seemed to straighten a little as he watched his son lower himself down from his perch.

Once Baruch was on the ground, Reb Shneur Zalman placed his hand on his son’s shoulder, and the two began to walk together along the riverbank.
