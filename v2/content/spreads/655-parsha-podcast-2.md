chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Now, we’ll paint the stackable boxes for the aron, and it will be finished!

I’m so excited! My Mishkan project will be amazing! Thanks for helping me, Chaim! I wish could spend all day building my model Mishkan. The Yidden in the midbar were so lucky; they had the real thing.

Hmm … I have someone named Naava from Shevet Dan. It says that she gives tours in the midbar. Mimi, you do the calling today.

Hi, Naava! What was it like traveling in the midbar? Were you close to the Mishkan? Did you see it everyday?

Such good questions! Traveling in the midbar was quite special because Hashem took care of everything for us. We were all camped in the shape of a square with the Mishkan in the middle, because Hashem said, “סָבִיב לְאֹהֶל מֹועֵד יַחֲנּו”. So, three shevatim camped on each side, creating a big box. Inside that box was Shevet Levi, camped around in a smaller square…

…and inside the Mishkan was the Kodesh Hakadashim, and inside the Kodesh Hakadashim was the aron, and inside the aron—in the innermost box—were the luchos!

Hmm, it’s almost like the luchos were given layers and layers of protection.

It was a desert, remember? There were probably snakes, scorpions, and big scary spiders!

Exactly! There are many dangers in a desert. Our mission as Yidden was to protect the Torah.

Basya, when Hashem told Bnei Yisroel to build the Mishkan, He didn’t say, “Build me a Mishkan so that I can rest in it.” Hashem said “וְׁשָכַנְּתִי ּבְתֹוכָם—so that I can rest in them.” Hashem is always with us, and His presence rests in every one of us, even today!

That’s right! Don’t be sad, Basya. We all have our own ruchniyusdik Beis Hamikdash.

Hey, neat! So we can guard the luchos and the Torah? Umm … but what are we protecting it from?

Well, like the desert, Golus is also full of dangers. C’mon Basya, think of the Yetzer Hara! It’s scary!

He’s right. So when we finish recording this podcast and I study for my Chumash test instead of pulling out the new board game, I’m protecting my Mikdash?

You got it, Mimi! But there’s more. Did you kids know that your house is also called a Mikdash?

It sure is! When you protect the Yiddishkeit of your home, Hashem responds by filling it with brachos.

So maybe it’s time to get rid of those questionable CDs we found in the attic.

And throw away the candy with a not-so-great hechsher that I accidentally bought yesterday.

Perfect! By guarding the kedusha of your heart and your home, you’ll reach the same place that the Yidden in the desert reached: Eretz Yisroel!

Yes! All Yidden will leave Golus and greet Moshiach in the gashmiyus Beis Hamikdash!
