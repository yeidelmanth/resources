Hi there! We’re the Levin Family: Basya, Mimi, Chaim and

Yossi—chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Let’s interview Yaakov this time. I want to ask him for tricks on dealing with enemies like Eisav.

It looks like everyone agrees with that one.So nu, let’s call him!

Uh, sorry, I think we called the wrong number. We meant to call Yaakov Avinu, because we wanted to ask him about dealing with Eisav.

Um, the clothes in your profile picture look quite ancient. Are you from the times of the Beis Hamikdash? And why are youholding that funny shaped stick?

I’m from the days when the Bais Hamikdash was destroyed. And this isn’t a stick, it’s my michtavin! I use it in cheder to point along when we learn Torah. But it’s only for us little boys. When we turn Bar Mitzvah, we don’t use it anymore. Now what were you saying about Yaakov and Eisav? I might be able to help you.

Wait, I wanna hear stories about the days of the destruction of the Beis Hamikdash! Were you scared?

Are you kidding me? Why would I be scared? When we heard that evil armies were marching towards Yerushalayim, my friends and I all shouted ”“הַקֹל קֹול יַעֲקֹב וְהַּיָדַיִם יְדֵי עֵׂשָו !

Yeah, and that’s how it’s always been—Eisav’s descendants fight with their hands as a weapon, and Yaakov’s children use their voices for Torah as protection!

Yeah, so it didn’t matter to us that they had a big huge army, because that’s not our weapon! Our weapon is Torah, and me and my friends were learning plenty of Torah all day!

We’d wave our michtavin pointers at them. We knew that the power of our Torah learning would defeat them.

You know, you’re not who we meant to call on our podcast, but I think you answered just what Yossi wanted to know.

Yeah! When my Yetzer Hara, who’s like Eisav, tries to bother me, I’ll fight him with my Torah learning!

And when anyone in the world tries to pull us away from Hashem, we’ll just use our “kol Yaakov” to fight them off!

And then, instead of fighting you, the world will respect you for the Torah you learn, and even help you to serve Hashem!

And of course that means we’ll march proudly out of Galus with great honor, right away!

So, chayolim, think about it: How can you, too, not be afraid?

…and that’s another fabulous episode of the Parsha Podcast. Efraim, thank you for joining us! We’ll be sure to think the question over. See you all chayolim next week!
