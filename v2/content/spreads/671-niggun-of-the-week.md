<h3>Breakthrough Sound</h3>

Chaya can feel boundaries bursting as she films her family while Yochi leans over her shoulder.

Tatty is on the guitar, Tzvi plays his piccolo, and Dovid totally works the drums. Chaya is filming the whole thing as a promotion for their upcoming concert.

more people now benefit from seeing our videos..."

“Learning to do this sure was a challenge!” Chaya comments, giving her shiny computer lid a pat.

And they began the niggun again. This time without her camera or computer, Chaya just listens. She hears the niggun going wider and deeper, bursting through boundaries and pushing even further. Yes, this is a Moshiachdik niggun! She can’t explain it, but she feels it. Ever farther, ever closer to Hashem, forever!
