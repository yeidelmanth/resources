<h3>Esther Miriam’s Special Harp</h3>

Eleven-year-old Esther Miriam Shir skips to the corner of the garage where the family tools are kept. Her handy brother Mendel joins her for their first lesson in how to use the drill. Nearby: wire strings, pliers, and the wooden hanger she will use as her base. She is going to build a harp.

answers the poor,

It will have an exquisite sound. Unlike a usual harp with seven strings, this will be made with one string more.

Merciful One Who answers the

Like the harp of Moshiach, Esther Miriam thinks. Moshiach will take us way above the natural world, because he will introduce a note beyond the seven we already know.

Her thoughts race to the tempo of her playing: Because even though the niggun is about “poor” Yidden in golus, we know we won’t stay here any longer. We’re happy because Hashem is answering our tefillos. He’s in the field, listening, and Moshiach is almost here!

But her fingers can’t stop themselves and they move faster and faster. Like a rocket taking off, the niggun reaches the high part and turns into something completely different, happy and upbeat—
