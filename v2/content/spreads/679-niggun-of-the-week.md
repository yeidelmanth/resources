<h3>Right Now!</h3>

The entire Shir family has been davening for their neighbor Leah. Leah has not been well for so long that the doctors don't know what to do. But the Shirs are not giving up hope. They help Leah’s family in any way they can, until one day they get the news...

"Leah is better! She’s really better!" Sara is beaming as she tells her family.

"Wonderful! How wonderful!" says Bubby, who is visiting her eineklach.

"The happiness we are feeling now for Leah will be felt by all Yidden very soon, when the Rebbe leads us from Golus to Geula!" Bubby says happily.

"Right now!" Dovid’s hands are a blur. "Mamash, right now!"
