Under Reb Moshe’s guidance, Gedalia Moshe moved in with a Jewish family in Rome. Living in a Jewish environment, combined with his steady meetings with Reb Moshe, built up his commitment to Yiddishkeit bit by bit.

And the stronger Gedalia Moshe’s ties to Reb Moshe, the more vocal he became about defending the Jewish religion whenever the topic came up in class. And the more that happened, the colder his professors became

towards him. In the past, they had favored Gedalia Moshe, doing constant favors for him and meeting with him whenever he asked. Now that they saw their prize student slipping out of their grasp, they stopped the special treatment. At the end of the semester, Gedalia Moshe graduated with honors from his science and math courses. But his religion professor gave him a failing grade.
