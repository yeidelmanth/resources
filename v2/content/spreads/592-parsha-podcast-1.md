Hi there! We’re the Levin Family: Basya, Mimi, Chaim and

Yossi—chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

The moon?! Why the moon? Let’s bring Dovid HaMelech!

How about we all just agree on someone and go for it? Anyway, Chaim knows a lot of Torah, so if he’s saying Dovid HaMelech, he probably has a secret reason why.

Well, looks like I’m out-numbered! Yossi, could you bring in Dovid HaMelech?

Wow. A call from the Ikvisa D’Mishicha, the time right before Geula!

Um, er, uh … Dovid HaMelech, uh, it’s kind of scary to talk to a king, but … um, what should we learn from Parshas Vayishlach?

Yeah, I sort of wanted to ask the moon, and Mimi wanted to ask Yaakov Avinu, but then Chaim thought that you’d be the right person.

Yaakov Avinu, the moon, and I. Do you know what we all have in common?

That too. In this week’s parsha, Yaakov Avinu is called ‘katan’, the little one. The moon and I are also called little. And guess who else is described that way?

The Yidden! Hashem calls us by Yaakov’s name, and calls us a little nation.

Yes! We’re called little because we know that on our own, we’re really nothing. Any greatness and power we have comes from Hashem.

Yes! The moon told us that! But it also taught us that with Hashem’s power, we can do great big things. Like our mission to fill the whole entire world with Hashem’s Torah and Mitzvos!

Just like the moon, who can’t do anything without the help of the sun. But with the sun, it can fulfill its big mission to light up the big world.

Yes, yes. We always have to remember these two opposite things. We are small, because we can’t do anything on our own. Whatever we accomplish is only because Hashem has helped us very much.

Yeah, like us in the Parsha Podcast. We really couldn’t do such great things without the ko’ach that Hashem gives us!

Right. But we also must remember the opposite: We can do big things! We need to do big things! We have a mission to light up the world, and we can do it because we have kochos from Hashem!

Of course! And Moshiach will come from me—who is called small—to the whole big world!

So, chayolim, think about it: What great things would you like to accomplish with ko’ach from Hashem?

…and that’s another fabulous episode of the Parsha Podcast. Dovid HaMelech, thank you for joining us! We’ll be sure to think the question over. See you all chayolim next week!
