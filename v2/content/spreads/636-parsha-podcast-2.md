I read a story about this week’s parsha, and I don’t get it.

When the Tzemach Tzedek was a little boy, his teacher taught the posuk “וַיְחִי יַעֲקֹב ּבְאֶרֶץ מִצְרַיִם ׁשְבַע עֶׂשְרֵה ׁשָנָה —and Yaakov lived in Mitzrayim for seventeen years.” The teacher explained that these seventeen years were the best years of Yaakov’s life.

That’s exactly what the young Tzemach Tzedek wanted to know. So when he got home, he asked his zeidy, the Alter Rebbe, “Wasn’t Mitzrayim a low place? How could his best years have been there?!”

Yup! Yaakov sent Yehuda ahead to build a yeshiva. So, when they got to Mitzrayim, they learned Torah all day. That made Mitzrayim into a place filled with good things, which made Yaakov very happy.

But I don’t get it. They also learned Torah all day when they were still living in Cana’an.

That’s Eretz Yisroel! The holiest place in the world!

Exactly! Why did the learning in Mitzrayim make Yaakov happier than the learning in Eretz Yisroel?

We just had Yaakov Avinu as a guest three weeks ago, but I’m sure he’ll be happy to talk again.

My children! It’s so nice to hear from you again. What’s on your minds today?

Hi, Zeidy Yaakov Avinu! Why were you happiest in Mitzrayim?

Ah, a fine question. For a long time before going to Mitzrayim, I was very sad. I didn’t know if my son Yosef was still a tzaddik—or if he was even alive!

Mm-hmm. Then, I suddenly found out that he was alive, and second-to- the-king in Mitzrayim! I was very happy to hear that he was alive, but then, I felt terrified. Mitzrayim? That’s a land full of aveiros and tuma! Was it possible that Yosef was still keeping Torah and mitzvos?

Yes! I found that out when I arrived. I felt extremely happy! All of my sons are tzaddikim, properly serving Hashem! I was happy before Yosef went to Mitzrayim. But this time, the joy came after being very sad and scared, so I appreciated it much more.

Like when you’re in the dark for a long time, and then you turn on the light— and you go, “Whoa! I can see!” Suddenly, you appreciate the light more than you ever did before! Because you can only know how good the light is when you know how bad the dark is.

Exactly! After being in the dark for so long and then seeing that my Yosef was still a tzaddik, I was suddenly surrounded by light! But there’s another great thing about light.

That it makes the dark go away? Or, even turns it into light?

Yes! The darkness became light! The sadness became joy! You see, Mitzrayim was a place full of tuma, which brought me a lot of fear before I got there. But then, there I was: learning Torah with all my children in Mitzrayim!

You turned Mitzrayim—the sad place— into a happy place, full of kedusha!

So the yeshiva turned all the sad times into happy ones! No wonder it’s the happiest years of your life!
