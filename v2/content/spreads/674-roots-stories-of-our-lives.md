Baruch also heard the story of how Zevulun Binyamin saved the entire town of Dobromysl. It started with a terrible epidemic that raged through the small town, especially affecting young children. Zevulun Binyamin, with his knowledge of farm animals, was the only one who realized the source of the illness: poisoned milk, as a result of the cows and goats eating contaminated grasses. And he knew not just the source of the problem, but the exact herbs and plants that would cure the poison. Incredibly, within just a couple of days, all of the sick were healed, thanks to Zevulun
