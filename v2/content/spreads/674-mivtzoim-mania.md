Zeidy’s Special Mission Operation Bayis Malei Sefarim

That all helped adults learn the Rebbe’s teachings. But what about kids like you and me? The sichos were long and sometimes complicated. How could we learn what the Rebbe said?

One day, Reb Yerachmiel Benjaminson, the executive director of Tzivos Hashem, came over to Zeidy with an amazing idea.

“The Rebbe spoke directly to children at more than one hundred rallies, saying over three hundred sichos just for them,” he said. “But those sichos are spread out in many places. I want to collect and publish them into a set of sefarim for children. Can you help with the writing?”

Zeidy imagined children opening their own sefer and learning the Rebbe’s important messages meant especially for Tzivos Hashem soldiers.

And that was the beginning of Der Rebbe Redt Tzu Kinder—The Rebbe Speaks to Children.

Rabbi Benjaminson asked Reb Sholom Ber Baumgarten to lead the project and make sure everything ran smoothly. But before Zeidy could start writing, the sichos would need to be gathered and sorted. Reb Aaron Leib Raskin took on that enormous job.

In those days, you couldn’t type “Tzivos Hashem” or “Chanukah” on a computer and instantly find every sicha on that topic. Rabbi Raskin had to turn page after page, searching through

fifteen years of children’s rallies to find all the times that the Rebbe spoke to children!

Soon, the task was complete. Rabbi Raskin put together tens of folders, each one clearly labeled with topics like “Tzivos Hashem,” “Chanukah,” “Pesach,” “Twelve Pesukim,” “Camp,” and “Special Missions.”

He then gave the folders to Zeidy, and the writing began. Day after day, Zeidy sat surrounded by piles of papers, folders, and sefarim. He read through each rally, marked the important parts, and rewrote the sichos in a way that children could easily understand.

Sometimes the Rebbe explained the same idea at more than one rally. Zeidy put the matching parts together like puzzle pieces to create one clear sicha.

When many of the sichos were ready, Zeidy began the next step: dividing them into sefarim.

Zeidy organized the newly written sichos by topic, giving each sefer a clear theme. The first two volumes were about Yomim Tovim, followed by two volumes connected to the parshiyos. The fifth sefer was all about Tzivos Hashem.

After each sefer was put together, Reb Heishke Gansbourg and Reb Refoel Wilschanski carefully reviewed it.
