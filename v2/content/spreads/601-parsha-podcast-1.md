Yup! I’m sure we all have the same question to ask him.

Ahh! The Parsha Podcast! I guess that means you want to ask me about this week’s parsha?

Yes. This is the parsha where the Yidden finally get the Torah. It’s the most important thing that ever happened to us! So the parsha should be named for that. Why is it called by your name, “Yisro”?

Oh, I had a different question. Why did Hashem only give the Torah after you came?

Brilliant questions you have! Why is it called Parshas Yisro and not Parshas Matan Torah? And why did this most wondrous event happen after I joined Am Yisroel? It’s because I, Yisro, have a very important lesson to share about the giving of the Torah.

Yeah! You weren’t part of the Yiddishe nation. In fact, you lived far far away from them! But then, one day, you heard about the miracles that Hashem made, so you traveled to the wilderness where the Yidden were camping out.

Then you declared that Hashem is the Master of the Universe! You decided to become a Yid and keep all the mitzvos.

And, like I said, only after you became a Yid, did Hashem give the Torah to the Yidden.

Yes, that’s my story! And now the lesson for you: Did you know that each of you have your own “Yisro”? Your guf and nefesh habehamis are just like me. I started off as a regular person; all I wanted was food and fun.

Just like my nefesh habehamis! It always wants to eat chocolate and play games! But my neshama isn’t into those things. My neshama wants to learn Torah and do mitzvos all day.

That’s right. Your “Yisro”, or nefesh habehamis just wants gashmiyus, and your guf often tags along. But your neshama wants kedusha. So you need to do what I did: Get your “Yisro” to stop chasing useless gashmiyus. Instead, make your “Yisro” love kedusha as much as your neshama does!

So let me get this straight. I need to make my body love Torah and mitzvos. And when it does, it will be just like you, Yisro, when you became a Yid!

You got it! And then, once you bring your guf and nefesh habehamis on board, you can receive the Torah!

It all makes sense now! Because Torah is so special, we need to use something special in order to understand its lessons. We need our “Yisro” to help us!

Ohhh! So it’s called Parshas Yisro because we need our “Yisro” to accept and want Hashem, and then we can get the Torah!

Exactly! Starting with —“אָנֹכִי ה' אֱֹלקיָך accepting Hashem as the only Power.

And of course, thanks to you, Yisro, we all have the kochos to do this. Because when the Torah teaches us a lesson, it also gives us the ability to make it happen!

So, chayolim, think about it: How can you help your body love Torah and mitzvos?

…and that’s another fabulous episode of the Parsha Podcast. Yisro, thank you for joining us! We’ll be sure to think the question over. See you all chayolim next week!
