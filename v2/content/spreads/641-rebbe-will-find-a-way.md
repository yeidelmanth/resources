Our Elter Zeidy received a letter from the Rebbe … fifty years after it was written!

It was two weeks before slichos in the year תשפ”ב (2022). Our Elter Zeidy had just celebrated his 85th birthday when his friend, Rabbi Yitzchok Wilhelm, came hurrying towards him.

“Rabbi Dalfin, you won’t believe this! I found a letter from the Rebbe with your name on it! It was hiding in a corner of the Rebbe’s library!”

“Yes!” said Rabbi Wilhelm. “It says ‘'Rabbi A. Dalfin’ right on the envelope, but there’s no address on it. There’s just a small scrap of paper attached with a clip that says, ‘'check address.’ It looks like the Rebbe’s secretaries set it aside until they would find your address … and then somehow, the letter got lost!”

Zeidy could hardly believe it. A letter from the Rebbe, written just for him! And now, right after his birthday and right before Rosh Hashana, it had finally been found. What better time to receive a bracha from the Rebbe!

The next morning, before shacharis in 770, Rabbi Wilhelm brought the letter to Zeidy.
