Hi there! We’re the Levin Family: Basya, Mimi, Chaim and

Yossi—chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

YUM! Popcorn! Hey, why are you all looking at me funny?

Oh! Oops! I can’t believe I just did that! I’ve been so good at saying brachos lately, I always say them loud and clear. How did I forget, just like that?

I know who would have the answer! Someone right in this week’s parsha. Could you please call in one of the Yidden who came out of Mitzrayim?

Basya? Mimi? Do we all agree on this one? Okay, set up for an ancient Yid that came out of Mitzrayim... Just gotta press some buttons and set the dials and....

Whoah! I guess it’s my turn to speak on the Parsha Podcast. How can I help you kids?

Ah, those fools. Paroh thought he could keep chasing us! When we left Mitzrayim he sent secret agents to tag along.

Well, Moshe and Aharon had told Paroh that we were just going on a three day trip to serve Hashem in the midbar. Paroh wanted to make sure that we would do just that, and then come back.

He wanted you back? After all those makos? After you were already gone for three days?

Oh, you obviously don’t understand Paroh! Quite foolishly, he never gave up on us being his slaves. No matter what, he kept trying to get us back. Even after we were far, far away from him.

Well, when the three days were up, they tried telling us it was time to go back to Mitzrayim. We pretty much just laughed at them! Of course we wouldn’t go back. We only go where Hashem tells us to go, not Paroh.

So the agents just went back all alone? They were probably so embarrassed!

Yup! And we Yidden followed Moshe, who first took us backward a bit, where we rested close to Mitzrayim. Then we headed toward Har Sinai to get the Torah!

Um, can I ask why you first went backward? Paroh probably thought you were going back!

Moshe wanted to show Paroh that we weren’t afraid of him. He could chase us if he dares! We were just resting up so we’d be ready to head to Har Sinai.

You know, Paroh is just like my Yetzer Hara. He also never gives up! Even when I’m so good and I ignore him for so long. He just pops in my head, as if he’s a mighty king, and says I should listen to him. Then he tells me to quickly put food in my mouth— without thinking of saying a bracha.

Or without making sure the food is a good hechsher, or without saying thank you.

Yeah! Mine too! The Yetzer Hara never stops bothering us kids.

Aha... So you must do what we did. Laugh at him, don’t give him any attention. Only follow Hashem’s commands. Then run far away from him, until there’s not a trace of Yetzer Hara left. What can we do when the Yetzer Hara pops back up?

…and that’s another fabulous episode of the Parsha Podcast. Ancient Yid, thank you for joining us again! We’ll be sure to think the question over. See you all chayolim next week!
