Weeks passed, and the Zukins couldn’t fulfill their favorite mitzvah. They needed help!

Our family friends, the Zukins, love the mitzvah of hachnasas orchim. Every Shabbos, they host large meals for many guests, with delicious food and plenty of divrei Torah.

As you can imagine, making food for so many people is a big job. On top of that, Mrs. Zukin works on Fridays, so she can’t spend so much time in the kitchen. Like most families in Brazil whose parents work full-time, she relies on her housekeeper to do most of the cooking.

But one day, her housekeeper suddenly stopped working for her, and Mrs. Zukin couldn’t find a new one. This made her very sad. Without someone to help with the Shabbos cooking, she couldn’t invite as many guests anymore.

On a family trip to Eretz Yisroel, Mrs. Zukin davened at the Kosel: “Hashem, please help me find a housekeeper so I can continue having hachnasas orchim.”

Sure enough, she soon found a housekeeper named Vanda.

Vanda loved working for the Zukins. She started saying “Baruch Hashem,” brought some of the fresh challah home for her husband, and even started checking the vegetables in her own house for bugs.

For fourteen years, the Zukins hosted many guests on Shabbos, thanks to Vanda’s help. Everyone was welcome—people from the neighborhood, Yidden who weren’t frum, and tourists from all over the world.

One day, Vanda called Mrs. Zukin crying. She was very sick, and the doctor told her to stop working so that she could rest. Mrs. Zukin thought it would be for just a few weeks, but six months passed, and Vanda was still in a lot of pain.

Submitted by Leib Ortiz, 6 Beit Menachem, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil
