chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

I wonder what it feels like to have a king. A real king, with a jeweled crown and a satin cloak...

I know it’s not as exotic, but we do have a president who wears suits and ties. I’m guessing that you’re thinking about the Alter Rebbe’s mashal of melech basadeh, the king in the field, right?

Ooh, I know that mashal! The king lives in his glamorous palace with all of his guards, and it’s almost impossible to arrange an appointment with him.

Right. Then, he goes out into the field where anybody can meet him. He greets his people with a big smile and listens to their requests.

If only we had a human-king, we would be able to understand Hashem even better.

Yesterday, Tatty was listening to a podcast, and the host decided to call up a chossid who had been at the Rebbe’s farbrengens.

Well, what if we could have a podcast too … and call up someone who has seen a king with their own eyes…

Yeah! Let’s make a podcast! And share it with all of our friends, so they can share it with their friends. It’ll be amazing!

I’ll get the family phone—Yossi, you can record it on that little recording device you won at school.

Oh, I know just the person! I read about her in a book.

Wait—can you call someone who lives in another time and place?

Call going through … what’s taking so long? Oh, you didn’t tell me she lives 600 years ago. Well, it worked! Guest: Sara, age 10, from the kingdom of Feyold. Hobby: tree-climbing.

Hello Sara from Feyold, this is the Levin Kids Podcast! Can you please tell us about the time you saw the king?

Oh! Um, hi Levin kids! Well, yeah— where do I begin? Seeing the king was the most exciting day of my life!

I’m sure it was! How did you know the king was coming? Did you get an invitation?

Well, I was sitting at the top of a tree in my family’s strawberry field when I suddenly heard music and saw people running out of their houses. That’s when I noticed writing in the sky! It said, “Baruch Haba, King Souvrain.” I scrambled down from the tree and ran home.

Well, I wanted to look presentable. My sister Rivka quickly braided my hair and dusted off my skirt. I was so excited, I love the king! But I was scared, too. When I got to the king, he had such a big smile, his face was glowing, and he listened to everyone’s requests—including mine!

The most powerful man in your whole entire country took the time to meet you!

Thank you Sara for telling us all about seeing your king!

Basya, we have the exact same opportunity during Elul—but even better! We can ask Hashem anything! Like King Souvrain, Hashem is listening to us and smiling!
