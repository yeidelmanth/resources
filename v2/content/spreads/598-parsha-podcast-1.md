Hi there! We’re the Levin Family: Basya, Mimi, Chaim and

Yossi—chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Hey, calm down. I know it sounds nuts, because Paroh is an evil man. But think about it... we’re sort of controlling Paroh... I can decide when to call and when to…

Hey! I see what you’re getting at! Brilliant! Let’s do it!

What? I’m so confused! Are we calling Paroh just so that we can hang up?

Basya, the first war the Yidden ever fought was against Paroh. He wanted us to be his slaves. But Hashem fought him “b’yad chazaka uvizroya n’tuya”.

With a strong and outstretched arm! Hashem really whacked him hard! Then He made tons of miracles for the Yidden.

Okay, so why would that be a reason to bring Paroh on our podcast?! I don’t want to listen to the mean-y Paroh!

EXACTLY! Now, lemme dial this number, press these buttons, and.....

Yeah! We only listen to Hashem! You can’t tell us what to do!

There is a little bit of Paroh inside of all of us, trying to make us his slaves.

You mean the Yetzer Hara? The Yetzer Hara is Paroh?

Yep! We’re fighting Paroh inside of us. He wants us to be his slaves. He tells us not to do the right thing.

But no, we fight him like Hashem did, b’yad chazakah! Hey, Paroh, I’m gonna put this coin in the pushka!

No! Don’t do that! I command you to obey my orders and give me all your money! Or use it to buy toys that your mother doesn’t like!

Well, sorry, Paroh, but I think you have some makos to return to...

Bye-bye, Paroh! Chaim is gonn hang up now, just like we’re going to hang up on our Yetzer Hara! Gone— and never to be heard from again!

Ah! He’s gone! I’ve always wanted to do that. Now we’re all free. Let’s drown our Yetzer Hara with mitzvos, and never hear from him again.

Then we can serve Hashem without anything bothering us!

And Hashem will see what we did, and bring the geulah!

Yes! “ּכִימֵי צֵאתְָך מֵאֶרֶץ מִצְרַיִם"—Hashem will bring us to geulah with miracles even greater than the Yidden saw back then!
