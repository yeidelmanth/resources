Curious students began to approach Gedalia Moshe, asking how a star student like him could intentionally ruin his grades. Intrigued by the answers that Gedalia Moshe gave them, these students began to meet regularly with him and Reb Moshe to discuss faith and religion.

Most of the students who attended came from completely assimilated Jewish families. For example, there was Lars Aschberg\*, a medical student from Sweden. His parents were both Jewish, but on the verge of converting to Christianity. They themselves didn’t have the heart to do it, but they sent Lars to study at the Vatican in the hopes that

he would do it for them. Another example was Antoni Yasinovsky, a science student from Warsaw. He was brought up even more distant from Yiddishkeit than Lars; he was never even given a bris milah.

Students like Lars and Antoni were raised to believe that all religious Jews were ignorant and stuck in the past. Seeing Reb Moshe and Gedalia Moshe speak about Judaism in such an educated manner made a deep impression on them.
