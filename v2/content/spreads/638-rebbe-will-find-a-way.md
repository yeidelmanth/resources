But after the first day of camp, she still didn’t know what to do.

A week later, in honor of Gimmel Tammuz, all the counselors went on a special trip to visit the Ohel.

During the long bus ride there, Chaya began writing a pan. She poured out her heart to the Rebbe, saying how confused she was about how long to stay as a counselor. Rebbe, I know you always answer, she wrote. Please give me a clear sign so I’ll know what to do.

After davening at the Ohel, the counselors went to watch the Rebbe video. Soon, the Rebbe started talking about Jewish children.

Chaya’s ears perked up. Maybe the Rebbe had a message for her! She listened closely to the Rebbe’s words:

effect on Jewish children, you should stay as long as you can, because you are helping the future generation of Yidden.”

Chaya stood there in shock. This was her answer from the Rebbe!

She immediately turned to the head counselor and said, “I’m staying for five weeks!”

Chaya returned to camp with a new sense of purpose. She had a shlichus

When Chaya didn’t know what to do, she turned to the Rebbe. The Rebbe answered quickly and clearly!

Our sister Chaya was so excited for the summer! She was in eighth grade, and she had big plans.

For the first part of the summer, she was going to be a junior counselor in a day camp. Then, she’d be going to overnight camp with her friends. After that, she would be starting high school away from home!

If she would do it for just three weeks, then she would have enough time to go home, pack for overnight camp, and get ready for high school.

But if she stayed for five weeks, she would have to go straight to overnight camp. And when camp ended, there would be only a few days left to get ready for high school.

Summer was coming closer, and the camp director kept asking how long she would stay. But Chaya just couldn’t decide. Every time she thought about it, her head felt dizzy and her stomach felt tight. “I’ll decide once camp starts,” Chaya said.

Submitted by Moshe, 10 Zalman, 7 Yossi Rothstein, 5 MyShliach, Nashville, TN
