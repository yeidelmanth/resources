Because Hashem tells us we have to be kadosh and keep the Torah and mitzvos very, very well. Even though we’re only tiny little kids from a tiny little nation in a big massive world. How are we supposed to do that? “Kadosh” means “holy,” and being holy is a big deal!

Yetzer Hara alert! Yetzer Hara alert! Only the Yetzer Hara would say that we can’t do a mitzvah from Hashem!

Mimi’s right. Let’s strike him with the kochos of Rivka Imeinu! Rivka was very kadosh, and she was the only good person in a huge city of evil people! Rivka will have valuable advice for us.

Baruch Hashem, not feeling great at the moment. The Yetzer Hara is trying to attack us! Can you tell us how we’re supposed to stay kadosh in galus? When we’re tiny little kids surrounded by a big massive world of people who don’t keep Torah and mitzvos?

That's a very good question. You can find the answer right where the mitzvah is.

In Parshas Kedoshim? Where Hashem says, ”ׁש אֲנִיוֹדָקְדׁשִים ּתִהְיּו ּכִי ק”?

Exactly! Hashem tells us, “You should be kadosh, because I am.”

So it's easier for us to be kadosh because Hashem is?

Yes! Hashem is the one who created the world and everything in it. "םּבְרֵאשִׁית ּבָרָא אֱֹלקִי". So of course, nothing can stop Hashem when He wants something! If Hashem wants you to be kadosh, then even all the other people in the world can’t stop you.

Because Hashem is the greatest Commander-In-Chief of them all!

Oh, and that's another thing to make it easier for you. Remember, Hashem is not only the greatest Commander-In- Chief—he also acts very differently than any other commander-in-chief.

Oh I know! In a regular army, the commander-in-chief tells his soldiers what to do, and then he sits back in his fancy desk chair and sends the soldiers off to battle without him. But Hashem isn't lounging around in a fancy desk chair!

Exactly. Because Hashem comes to battle with us. Hashem comes down here and stands right over every Yid! He’s watching us and looking into our hearts to see that we're serving Him in the best way!

Well then my Yetzer Hara is being silly. If Hashem, Who created this whole big massive world, is standing right with me, then of course I can stay kadosh! Nothing can stop even little me!

Ah! But remember, it's not “little you.” It's “big Hashem”! Because it's Hashem who gives you the mitzvah to be kadosh. The Creator of the universe is helping you! ”קְדׁשִים ּתִהְיּו”! So, chayolim, think about it: What can you think about to help yourself remain kadosh?

…and that’s another fabulous episode of the Parsha Podcast. Rivka Imeinu, thank you for joining us! We’ll be sure to think the question over. See you all chayolim next week!
