chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Remember last week? When the Yid from the Mishkan made us so happy to serve Hashem?

The one that fell on his face? Yeah, I remember! He was dancing with joy!

Well, I wish we could get all Yidden to be that happy—not just us.

Yeah, me too. But we need to take care of Parshas Tazria now.

Hmm. In Tazria, we learn about the mitzvah of doing a bris milah at eight days.

Who would we ask about that? Eight-day-old babies can’t talk.

The first Yid to ever have a bris at eight days was Yitzchak Avinu. Plus, Yitzchak Avinu’s name is all about simcha. So maybe Yitzchak Avinu can also help, Basya!

Yeah! When Sara Imeinu had Yitzchak, she said, “Everyone who hears will be happy with me!” So let’s call Yitzchak Avinu!

Hello! It’s Parshas Tazria, so I assume you want to know some secrets about bris milah. Am I right?

Because I was the first Yid to ever have a bris milah at eight days, of course! So the bris is very shayach to my name.

To your name? Your name is about simcha! Yay! Please tell us about simcha!

Ahhh simcha! A bris milah is done with great simcha! We are always very happy and excited to bring a new Yid into the bris of my father, Avraham Avinu!

That’s why we do the bris as soon as we can. From when the baby is born we’re already excited and counting down the days until the bris!

Ahhh, and do you know what else is done with great simcha?

All Torah and mitzvos! That’s what the Yid from last week told us!

Exactly! Every mitzvah is done with great simcha, just like a bris milah! Can you figure out why?

Because every mitzvah is part of the bris with Hashem?

Yes! Yes! Every mitzvah is a connection with Hashem. We do every mitzvah with joy, because we want to do it. We love being connected to Hashem!

But I wish everybody did. Some people don’t look too happy.
