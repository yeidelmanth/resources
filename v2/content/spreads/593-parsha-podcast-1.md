Hi there! We’re the Levin Family: Basya, Mimi, Chaim and

Yossi—chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

It’s so not fair. The other shvatim only get called by their names, and Yosef gets to be called Yosef Hatzaddik!

Yeah, what about Levi Hatzaddik? Or Yissachar Hatzaddik? I’m sure they were tzaddikim too! I wonder how Reuven felt. He was the oldest brother, but he didn’t get called “Hatzaddik” like Yosef!

Okay, I’ll call up Reuven! Just gotta press these buttons, dial his number, and…

Hey kiddos! How’re you all doing? Making Tatty proud?

We’re trying! But we wanted to ask you how you feel about the whole tzaddik thing…

Why do we only call Yosef ‘Hatzaddik’, and not the rest of you shvatim?

Ah, I hear your question. The reason is because Yosef isn’t like the rest of us. All of us brothers are tzaddikim, it’s true. But Yosef—he’s a whole lot more of a tzaddik. A different type of tzaddik. He deserves it to be in his name.

Yes, but I’ll get to that reason soon. First, take a look at his name, Yosef. It means to add. Yosef was always adding in his avodah. He never thought, “Hmm, what a great tzaddik I was today!” It was always, “Hmm, what can I add in my day so that I can serve Hashem even better?”

Oh man. I could learn from Yosef. Yesterday I was thinking about how good I’ve been lately. But I know that really, I could’ve spent more time learning Torah.

Me too! I could’ve given more tzedakah, but I wanted to buy a soda instead.

Yep. So Yosef is really a greater tzaddik than the rest of us. Plus, like your sister suggested, there’s another reason he deserves the title. He was in Mitzrayim from when he was just a teen, and lived there for 93 years. Do you know what he was doing all that time?

He wasn’t hiding in a cave and learning Torah. Yosef Hatzaddik had to deal with the Mitzriyim all day long! And they did a lot of really yucky aveiros.

Yep. But Yosef kept being Yosef Hatzaddik. Just like when he was in Eretz Yisroel with the rest of us. He kept growing and doing more for Hashem, never getting influenced by the Mitzriyim who were around him all day.

Okay, so Yosef has major reasons to be called “Hatzaddik”, even more than the rest of the brothers. And we have a lot to learn from him.

Yeah! Because we’re also in Golus, and we’re surrounded by people who aren’t doing what Hashem wants. Sometimes it’s really hard to not do things like them.

Uh huh. Especially when what they're doing looks so much easier, or when I think I’m being good enough anyway.

So the trick is to be like Yosef! When the Yetzer Hara tries to fool us into being like the goyim, we can just ignore him!

Not only that—we remember Yosef’s name, and make sure we’re doing more everyday to serve Hashem! Because I can always be better in my avodas Hashem!
