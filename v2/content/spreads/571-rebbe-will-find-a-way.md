Hearing the size Tatty was looking for, Mr. Stryken exclaimed, “So small? You need to aim for bigger and better! Listen, I used to buy and sell homes. Let me see if I can help.”

Tatty reflects on the miraculous chain of events that began with his letter to the Rebbe: “Originally, we were looking for a short-term solution. But when we left the decision to the Rebbe, a much bigger and better opportunity became possible— far beyond our dreams!”

After leaving the decision to the Rebbe, Tatty and Mommy achieved something which they never dreamed was possible.

When Tatty and Mommy first moved out on shlichus to Alaska, all of their Chabad House activities took place in the house that they were renting.

After two successful years, they realized it was time to move on. “This house is too small for our events,” Mommy said. “Let’s look for a bigger house until we can afford a proper Chabad House building.”

And so, the search began. For almost an entire year, Tatty and Mommy looked for a house, but nothing worked out. All the houses were either too small, too expensive, or too far away.

Soon, there was even more pressure to find a new home. Our rental agreement was about to expire, and the landlord needed us to decide whether or not we were staying.

Tatty and Mommy didn’t know what to do. After Rosh Hashana, Tatty wrote a letter to the Rebbe describing their dilemma: Should we buy one of the houses that we aren’t fully happy with, or should we continue living in our rental home?

Soon, our family prepared to travel to New York for Simchas Torah. We couldn’t wait to go to the Ohel and experience Yom Tov near the Rebbe.

Just before boarding the plane to New York, Tatty saw a text message from Mrs. Hardy. I spoke with the Strykens, she wrote. We’ll be happy to join your family for a Friday night meal on the first weekend of October.

Tatty and Mommy were thrilled. They’d invited the Hardys and the Strykens for a Shabbos meal many times, but they had never been able to come. Now, they’d be joining us for a meal on Shabbos Bereishis!

After an uplifting Simchas Torah by the Rebbe, we flew back to Alaska and enjoyed a beautiful Shabbos meal with the two families.
