When Mommy called to ask how the baby was doing, the father said, “We really need a miracle.”

Although he wasn’t familiar with writing to the Rebbe, Mommy encouraged him to write a letter and showed him exactly how.

Two weeks later, he called back, amazed. “Our baby suddenly got better! The doctor said that he would never believe she had ever been sick at all. We just brought her home, completely healthy!”

Seeing the miracles that happened, first for our family and then for our neighbors’ family, show me how the Rebbe’s brachos reach every Yid. No matter who they are, the Rebbe cares for them.

I am proud to be the brother of a miracle, and proud to be a Chossid of the Rebbe, who finds a way to help every Yid.

The doctors didn’t know what was wrong. Would our baby sister be okay?

In the summer of תשס”ט (2009), everyone was very excited. Mommy was having twins!

But during one of her checkups, the doctor’s smile faded. “Something’s not right,” he said. “The second twin isn’t growing properly.”

Mommy and Tatty were very worried. They davened a lot and decided that they would name that baby Chana, after the Rebbe’s mother, as a z’chus for good health.

When my twin sisters were born, the second baby was totally fine. But the first twin suddenly began shaking every thirty seconds. The doctors ran many tests but couldn’t find the cause. “We don’t know if it will stop,” they said. “And if it does, it may take a whole year.”

The happiness of meeting the twins quickly turned into worry. Mommy and Tatty were scared for the first baby and didn’t know what would happen.

Although Mommy had originally planned to name the second baby Chana, now she felt unsure. “Maybe the first baby should be the one named Chana,” she told Tatty.

On Friday, Tatty went to the Ohel to ask the Rebbe for a bracha. On Friday night, Mommy decided for sure: the first baby would be named Chana. The next morning in shul, Tatty gave her the name. The second baby was named Yael.

Right after davening, Mommy and Tatty went to the hospital to see the twins. The head nurse hurried over. “Something strange happened,” she said. “This morning, the baby suddenly stopped shaking! Everything looks normal!”

Tatty and Mommy were overjoyed to hear the good news. “What time did it stop?” they asked.

Tatty looked at Mommy in disbelief. “That’s exactly when I named her Chana!”

From that minute on, Baby Chana never shook again. She grew up healthy and strong, and today, you’d never believe anything had ever been wrong.

A few years later, Mommy saw another clear bracha. A Litvish family who lives nearby had a newborn who suddenly became very sick.

Submitted by Yoel Silkes, 12 Anash Kinder, Brooklyn, NY
