Something from this week’s parsha has been bothering me all week.

Our guest is Reb Shloime. He was a chossid of the Alter Rebbe, so I’m sure he’ll be able to help.

Baruch Hashem, good, but I’m really struggling with an idea in this week’s parsha. Could you help? As a chossid of the Alter Rebbe, I’m sure you know a lot.

But it’s hashgacha pratis that you’re our guest for this week. So you must be able to help us!

In this week's parsha, Chassidus explains that Avraham Avinu represents the neshama and Sara Imeinu represents the guf. But last week Hashem told Avraham Avinu “ּכֹל אֲשֶׁר תֹּאמַר אֵלֶיָך שָׂרָה שְׁמַע בְּקֹלָּה—Whatever Sara tells you, listen to her voice.” That just doesn’t make sense together.

Yeah! Why should the neshama listen to the body? It should be the opposite!

Beautiful question! Once, when the Alter Rebbe was davening on Rosh Hashana or Yom Kippur, and he got to the words, “…ּובְכֵן ּתֵן פַּחְדְָך—And so, Hashem, put a fear of You on everything You made,” he was unable to get the words out, and he began rolling on the floor.

Because the Rebbe was so overcome by the meaning of the tefillah, that he felt it in his whole body.

I get it. Sometimes we feel something so strongly that it affects our whole body. For the Alter Rebbe, it was his connection to Hashem.

That is exactly right! Our Rebbeim always showed us that when we do mitzvos, it does change our whole body. And so, the best way to do a mitzva is by showing how it affects your guf.

Sometimes I get really excited about doing a mitzvah and I jump up and down. Like when we got to help our neighbor build his sukkah.

And sometimes when Mommy is davening, she sways back and forth and cries. Does that mean she’s davening with her whole body?

Yes! These examples show that the body feels its ruchniyus connection to Hashem.

But that’s still the guf listening to the neshama. They’re connected, but it’s still Sara listening to Avraham. Hashem told Avraham to listen to Sara.

Well, now that we’re discussing this, I realize that things were probably different for Avraham and Sara because they’re tzaddikim.

A kluger kop! Tzaddikim live with the reality of Geulah. And what will be of the guf then?

Right again! In a way, the guf is greater than the neshama. While we are in Golus, though, we don’t see it. Our bodies seem gashmiyus and grub. But in Moshiach tzeiten, the greatness of the Yiddishe body will finally be obvious. We’ll recognize that it’s from a higher place, and constantly use it for Hashem!

But even now, we can show how amazing our guf is by doing mitzvos with our whole self. This gives us a taste of Moshiach!

Oh, yeah! And when we live with a Moshiach mindset by doing mitzvos with our full body, then Moshiach will come in a fully revealed way!

Amen! Exactly! And now let’s ask all the other chayolim to think about it: How will you show the ma’alah of your guf and use it for Hashem?
