When Reb Avraham went to Vitebsk that week, he spent most of the time carting his fruits to the market, making deals with the fruit sellers, and selling baskets at his stall. But in between all of

his business dealings, he asked as many people as he could—in shul, at his hotel, and random acquaintances he met in the street—one simple question: “Do you happen to know an orphan boy named Baruch?”

On the next market day, Reb Avraham let Reb Shimon travel to Vitebsk, while he headed to Beshenkovitch. It was usually a two days’ journey from Liozna to Beshenkovitch; with Reb Avraham riding well into the night, he did it in a day and a half.
