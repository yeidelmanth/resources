Tatty and Mommy were ready for their own shlichus. But how would they know where to go?

For two years, Tatty and Mommy joined our uncle and aunt on shlichus at the University of Maryland.

They loved it! They met Jewish students, helped them learn Torah and do mitzvos, and showed them the warmth of Yiddishkeit.

Time flew by quickly, and before they knew it, the two years were almost over. It was time to find their own permanent shlichus.

So Tatty and Mommy began asking around and speaking to different people. They soon heard about four different campuses that needed shluchim. One of them was the University of South Florida—called “USF”—in Tampa, Florida.

USF sounded like a great place for them to go on shlichus. But Tatty and Mommy still weren’t completely sure.

Around Chof-Beis Shvat, תשס”ח (2008), Tatty went to the Ohel. He wrote to the Rebbe and asked for guidance: Which campus shlichus should we choose?

After leaving the Ohel, Tatty went into the room where the Rebbe video was playing.

It was a video from the Kinus Hashluchos in תנש”א (1991). The Rebbe was saying a sicha to the shluchos in the big shul of 770.

Tatty watched quietly. Then, suddenly, he saw something that made his eyes grow wide.

There on the screen was Mommy, standing in front of the crowd!

Of course, she wasn’t Mommy yet. She was a little girl then, visiting the Rebbe with her mother, Mrs. Ruth Cohen. They had come from Leeds, England, where their family was on shlichus.

Tatty stared in shock. Wait a minute, he thought. Mommy told me that she only came to the Rebbe once as a little girl. It was for the Kinus Hashluchos in תנש”א.

After the sicha, the Rebbe began giving dollars to the shluchos. One shlucha after another passed by, but JEM had put background music, so Tatty couldn’t hear what they were saying.

Then the fifth shlucha came onto the screen. It was Mrs. Sulha Dubrowski, a shlucha from Tampa, Florida.

Just then, the background music became quieter, and Tatty could hear her words clearly. “A bracha for the institutions in Tampa, Florida,” Mrs. Dubrowski said to the Rebbe.

Then the Rebbe gave her another two dollars and said, “For the entire institution. Much success.”

Tatty stood frozen in his spot. Tampa, Florida! The very place that they were thinking about!

He understood that this was the Rebbe’s answer. The Rebbe was showing them where to go and giving a bracha for their shlichus.

And so, that summer, Tatty and Mommy moved to Tampa and started our Chabad House at the University of South Florida.

From the very beginning, we knew that our shlichus came with the Rebbe’s clear guidance and bracha. And until today, we feel the Rebbe’s brachos constantly, in every step of our shlichus.

Sometimes, we also have questions. We don’t always know which choice is right. But this story reminds us that a Chossid can always turn to the Rebbe and ask for guidance. The Rebbe is with us, and he will find a way to answer!
