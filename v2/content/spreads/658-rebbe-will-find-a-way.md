He spoke to the doctors, and they told him that the surgery would be on Monday.

That Shabbos, Zeidy stayed home with his father, our elter zeidy. When he came to the hospital to visit Elter Bubby after Shabbos, she had amazing news.

“The doctor said he is canceling the surgery,” she said. “He sees that my shoulder is healing by itself!”

Just to be sure, they went to another doctor for a second opinion, just like the Rebbe had encouraged many people to do. This doctor agreed completely.

“There’s no need for surgery,” he said. “It will heal well on its own.”

Baruch Hashem, Elter Bubby continued getting stronger without any more operations.

Everyone was so grateful. We saw clearly that the Rebbe’s bracha was fulfilled!

The doctors said that Elter Bubby would need another surgery. Was there anything we could do?

Last year, our elter bubby fell very badly and was rushed to the hospital.

The doctors checked her carefully and said that she had broken her hip and her shoulder.

“The hip needs surgery right away,” the doctors said. “It’s an emergency.”

Baruch Hashem, the surgery went well. Afterward, Elter Bubby slowly began walking again and getting stronger. Everyone was so relieved to see her smiling and moving.

“The shoulder will need surgery too,” the doctors explained. “We’ll do it in a week or two, once she feels better from the first operation.”

But Zeidy was worried. One surgery was already so much for Elter Bubby. And now she would need another one?

Around that time, Zeidy traveled to New York for the Kinus. On Friday, he went to the Ohel. In his letter, he asked for a bracha that Elter Bubby would not need the second surgery, and that her shoulder should heal on its own.

The next week, Zeidy flew to England to be with Elter Bubby and help take care of her.
