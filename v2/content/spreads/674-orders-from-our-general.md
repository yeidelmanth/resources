<p class="lead">An incredible thing happens at the beginning of Parshas Mikeitz: Yosef is was Yosef’s job that Paroh said, “No one in the land can do anything without your permission!”</p>

Despite being in a place as low as Mitzrayim, Yosef continued living as a proud Yid. When other people saw how Yosef kept Torah and mitzvos, they respected and honored him. Plus, as we just said, he became so powerful that Paroh made him the mishneh lamelech! Yosef was in control.

In what way does Hashem put every Yid in charge?

By fitting in with everyone around him.

By following Torah and mitzvos, even if it means being different from others.

And Paroh said to Yosef... “Without your permission, no one may lift his hand or foot in the entire land of Mitzrayim.”

that even now, at the end of Golus, each of us can be like Yosef, the mishneh lamelech.

“We are soldiers in Hashem’s Army!” we tell the Yetzer

Hara. “We follow Hashem’s Torah!”

How can we stay strong? It’s when we remember that for anything connected to Yiddishkeit, we are in control. We are the mishneh lamelech! Instead of acting like the surrounding nations, we proudly follow Torah and mitzvos.

And just like Yosef who influenced the entire Mitzrayim, every Jewish child can influence others to behave correctly.
