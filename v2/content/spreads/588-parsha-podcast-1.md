Hi there! We’re the Levin Family: Basya, Mimi, Chaim and

Yossi—chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Mommy just gave us 4 lollipops. I get the blue one.

Forget the lollies. It’s Parshas Vayeira, and I want to bring in Avraham Avinu again. I have a question to ask him!

About what? I hope it’s something that will make them stop fighting.

Well, the Midrash tells us that Hashem came to see Avraham Avinu while he was sitting outside his tent. So of course Avraham wanted to stand up! But Hashem didn’t let him, because he was old. So Hashem stood, while Avraham sat.

Well, Hashem said he was happy to stand while Avraham was sitting, and that He’ll also happily stand over Avraham Avinu’s children when we’re sitting!

Exactly! And I get why Hashem is happy to stand while Avraham is sitting. Avraham was a tzaddik who did so many mitzvos, and now he was old, so it makes sense. But we’re little, so why is Hashem happy to stand over us?

Okay, okay, you’re making me curious, too. I’m calling Avraham Avinu right now…

Chaim wants to know what it means that Hashem is standing over us.

Oh, I would be delighted to stand over you as well! My precious children! Even when you’re as little as three or four, you go to shul and sit, learn, and daven with such kavanah. It’s wonderful! Hashem loves it! So He stands over you, just like He was happy to stand over me after I did so many mitzvos.

He guards and protects you. Hashem stands over you with all His power! This gives you the strength to fight over any Yetzer Hara voice that might try to stop you from doing the right thing.

Like if my Yetzer Hara tells me to take the better lollipop for myself?

Yes. Anytime the Yetzer Hara is bothering you, just remember that Hashem is standing right over you, and giving you strength to ignore it.

That’s the passuk ”“אֱֹלקִים נִצָב ּבַעֲדַת קֵל. With strength from Hashem, we could just ignore that Yetzer Hara until it disappears. Then we can serve Hashem with no bothers at all!

Even when the Yetzer Hara is sneaky and says that what I want matters more than what Mimi wants. Mimi, you can have the blue lollipop. I’ll take a different one.

No, you have it Yossi! I won’t let my Yetzer Hara make me do anything wrong, or stop me from doing anything good. Hashem is helping me to do the right thing.

And happily so! So, chayolim, how can you remind yourself that Hashem is always standing over you?

Well, that’s another fabulous episode of the Parsha Podcast. Zeidy Avraham Avinu, thank you for joining us once again! See you all chayolim next week!
