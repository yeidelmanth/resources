The chazan’s name, Baruch learned, was Reb Zalman Fishel, Der Ben Yachid.18 He was called a ben yachid even though he had eight sisters and brothers, and was already a grandfather. His nickname came from the fact that he’d always been treated like an only child due to

his delicate health. Even into adulthood, Reb Zalman Fishel never worked at a craft or business in order to preserve his strength. The one job he enjoyed—and tried to get at every opportunity—was being chazan.
