“That very same day, I called her with your new offer. When she heard that a Lubavitcher family—shluchim of the Rebbe—wanted to buy the house, she knew right away that this was her answer from the Rebbe! She lowered the price much more than she had ever planned, and agreed to sell the house to you.”

Tatty and Mommy were amazed at the incredible hashgacha pratis. Now we would finally have a big enough space for our family and our shlichus! The final price for the house ended in “770”, reminding everyone of the Rebbe’s brachos that helped us purchase our new home.

And guess what? Soon afterwards, the owner got engaged and remarried! Her personal tefillos were answered as well.

The owner had refused to lower her price. Why did she suddenly change her mind?

Seven years into our shlichus, our family had outgrown our small apartment. It was time to move to a bigger house.

One day, Tatty saw a great house for sale online. "It looks perfect!" he exclaimed, showing Mommy the pictures. "It’s big enough for our family and for all the Yidden who come to our Chabad House!"

“The location is amazing, too!” Mommy added. “But look how expensive it is,” she frowned. “We can’t afford that price.”

“I’ll offer a lower amount. Maybe they’ll agree to sell it for less.”

But when Tatty called the real estate agent (who is a frum Yid), his hopes were dashed.

“The owner won’t lower her prices for anyone,” the agent told Tatty. “I’ll tell her your offer, but don’t get excited.”

Sure enough, a few days later, he called back. “I’m sorry, but the owner is firm on her price. She won’t go lower.”

Tatty and Mommy were disappointed. They continued looking for other houses, but nothing was a good fit. And week after week, they saw that this house was still for sale. It felt like it was waiting for them!

But they still couldn’t pay the full amount, and the owner wouldn’t lower her price.

"We’ll offer a little more than before," they said.

"That’s still much less than what she’s asking," the agent replied. "I’ll ask her, but I don’t think she’ll say yes."

Tatty and Mommy wrote a letter to the Rebbe, asking for revealed brachos that they should get the house.

The next day, the real estate agent called back with exciting news. “You won’t believe this!” he said. “The owner agreed to sell it to you!”

Tatty and Mommy were thrilled. “But what changed?” they asked. “Why did she agree if our offer is still much lower than what she wanted?”
