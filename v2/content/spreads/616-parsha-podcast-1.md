Of course! Yossi is a very good owner! He takes good care of his things. He needs to know how many pieces he has, and make sure that none ever go missing!

Not quite, my friend. But Hashem does have something that He loves so very much. Hashem has counted it many times. Nine times to be exact.

It’s you! You and every single Yid! In Parshas Bamidbar, Hashem counted all the Yidden in the desert. And as the nasi of Shevet Yehuda, I was called up to help!

Because He loves us! Why else? Hashem is showing us how much He loves us by counting us! Even though Hashem obviously knows how many Yidden there are.

That’s right! And when we know how much Hashem loves us, it gives us lots of strength to do whatever He wants!

Because if Hashem loves us so much, He'll help us through any challenge that Golus can bring.

Like speaking nicely to our siblings instead of arguing.

And being careful with the hechsher on our food even when we’re not home!

And saying the truth if you broke something by mistake.

Right on! You know, this great love Hashem has for Yidden gets revealed even more every day. More and more until Moshiach comes. Then, we’ll get to see this huge love when Hashem counts the Yidden for the tenth time!
