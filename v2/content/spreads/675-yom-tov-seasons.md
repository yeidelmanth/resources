But the Yetzer brings “Bavel” (“confusion”) to make us unsure.

So we limit the use of gashmiyus to our hands and our feet,

While our hearts and our minds remain separate and \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.

5 Disconnected from Torah and mitzvos, some Yidden may seem,

But we hurry to bring them close and unite our nation.

Hashem transforms fast days to the opposite extreme:

“Jumping” from sadness to days of great \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_!

By making a change for the good and behaving this way,

We'll transform the fast days to Yomim Tovim \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_! With davening and learning, each day we begin,

And only then work with the world, the confusing Bavel.

And Yerushalayim spreads out everywhere; to Bavel, as \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.

On Asara B’Teves, we remember when Yerushalayim was surrounded by the army of Bavel. This eventually led to the churban. We also think

When we start our day with kedusha, keep our Yiras Shamayim strong,

and use the everyday things we do (like eating, playing, and learning) for Hashem, we bring more holiness into the world. Every time we jump to help another Yid do a mitzvah, Hashem joins us, and “jumps” to turn these sad times into times of joy.
