and be led forth

the hills will burst into song before you,

of the field will clap their hands.

all out of Golus to Eretz Yisroel, with Moshiach, of course. That’s what the Rebbe told us at a Tzivos Hashem rally...”

“I go to rallies,” says Tzvi. “So with hachlatos tovos and enough joy, Moshiach could lead us to Eretz Yisroel right now!”

He’s playing his piccolo now with great happiness—just the happiness we need to step into Eretz Yisroel with Moshiach!
