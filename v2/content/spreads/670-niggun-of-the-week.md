<h3>Homebound</h3>

Tatty becomes quiet and Esther Miriam feels she has made a good point. But then Tatty’s lips curve into a smile. "That’s exactly what the niggun is telling us! It’s a mashal: instead of spending our time ‘traveling’ and feeling unsettled in golus, we’re supposed to be resting at the ‘inn,’ the place where the Rebbe is. When we’re connected to him, we can enjoy some ‘l'chayim,’ which is the spiritual nourishment he gives.
