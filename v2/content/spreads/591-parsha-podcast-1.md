Hi there! We’re the Levin Family: Basya, Mimi, Chaim and

Yossi—chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

I think it’s pretty obvious who we should bring this time!

Yaakov Avinu! Parshas Vayeitzei is all about Yaakov and his family.

Yep. I’m calling already! Let’s hope we get through this time…

Ah! My children! How are the Parsha Podcasters doing? Are you using the bracha that Hashem gave me in this week’s parsha?

”“ּופָרַצְּתָ יָמָה וָקֵדְמָה וְצָפֹנָה וָנֶגְּבָה—That I should grow strong and spread out to the west, east, north, and south!

Well … um, actually, we’ve kinda just been staying here in our secret headquarters...

Wait—is that the bracha that teaches us how to attack the Yetzer Hara like a real army does? With four special divisions?

Yes, exactly. Every army has divisions, and each division has its specialty, something that it is best at doing.

Like an air force for sky attacks, the navy for the ocean, cavalry for the tanks and trucks, and infantry on their feet!

Hey, I think I’m remembering something about this. Tzivos Hashem also has four divisions—the east, north, west, and south!

Right on! “Yama vakeidma v’tzafona v’negba!” Each division also has leaders: My grandparents Avraham and Sara lead the south. My father, Yitzchak, and mother, Rivka, lead the north. Leah and I, Yaakov, lead the east. And Dovid and Rochel lead the west.

We each lead different attacks on the Yetzer Hara. Just like the navy is used when the army needs to attack from the sea, Avraham and Sara’s division— the south—is used when the Yetzer Hara needs to be attacked with mitzvos of tzedakah and chessed.

Oh, that’s because we learn about mitzvos of chessed from Avraham and Sara, so we look to them to lead us in those things. What if the Yetzer Hara is trying to stop Yidden from learning Torah? Who leads then?

That would be Leah and I from the east. Tell the Yetzer Hara that you’re following in Yaakov’s lead! I would sit and learn Torah, unlike Eisav who would go out to hunt.

So basically, whichever division the Yetzer Hara is attacking, that one has to be the strongest and lead the fight!

But what if the Yetzer Hara figures that out? Like, what if when we’re busy fighting hard and learning a lot of Torah, he’ll attack Avraham and Sara’s division of the south? He’ll see that they’re not fighting because we’re too busy with Torah, so he’ll easily win and get us to stop giving tzedakah!

Good point, Chaim. That’s why we’ve got to make sure that every division is always on guard. North, east, west, and south. When one side is leading the fight, the others must stay on guard and make sure that the Yetzer Hara doesn’t get through to them, either.

Just because we’re learning all day, doesn’t mean we can’t help Mommy prepare for Shabbos!
