“I believe the Rebbe had your twins in mind when he gave you those dollars,” our great- grandfather, Rabbi Sholom Mendel Simpson— one of the Rebbe’s mazkirim—told Mommy when he heard the story.

Mommy became very emotional. These dollars really do belong to the twins, she thought. When the time is right, I’ll give the dollars to them.

Years later, the twins saw an ad in the Hachayol magazine: “Send in your Rebbe story to be featured.” Because our family loves reading the Rebbe story each week, they decided to send in the special story of their birth.

A short while later, our parents got an email from Hachayol, thanking them for the story and asking for more details: Have you given your children their Rebbe dollars yet? If yes, was it during a birthday farbrengen? How do they feel about owning them?

Tatty was amazed. The day that the email was sent was Yud-Daled Menachem Av, the twins’ birthday!

“What hashgacha pratis!” he exclaimed. “I think it’s time to give the twins this special gift.”

Mommy and Tatty framed the Rebbe dollars and hung them in the twins’ room. Each time the twins see the dollars, they remember that the Rebbe’s brachos were with them when they were born, helping them grow to be healthy and strong. They are proud chassidim of the Rebbe, doing all they can to make him proud!

Mommy was being rushed into an emergency operation. Would the dollars get to the hospital in time?

When Mommy was expecting twins, she went with Tatty for a regular checkup in Long Island. At the time, she was only seven months pregnant.

When the doctor saw her test results, he became very serious. “Things aren’t looking good,” he said grimly. “You’ll need to have an emergency C-section right now so that the babies can be born healthy.”

Tatty and Mommy were shocked. They had prepared for a checkup, not for an emergency birth! They immediately wrote to the Rebbe for a bracha, davening that the twins would be born healthy.

Then, Tatty called his father, our Zeidy Levin. “Can you please bring one of your Rebbe dollars to the hospital for the operation?”

“I wish I could, but I’m not home,” Zeidy Levin said, his voice filled with worry. “I’m in Crown Heights.”

“So can you please get one from my in- laws, the Simpsons?” Tatty asked. Our Zeidy Simpson has a collection of over 100 dollars from the Rebbe that he, Bubby Simpson, and their children had received over the years.

Zeidy Simpson was ready to help. He got the envelope of Rebbe dollars and put his hand inside to pick a random dollar. Imagine his surprise when out came two dollars that were stuck together!

Even more amazing, both of the dollars had Mommy’s name written on them. Zeidy was stunned by the tremendous hashgacha pratis. Out of all the dollars belonging to Mommy’s parents and many siblings, hers were the onces that had been chosen!

It felt like a clear sign from the Rebbe that he was giving Mommy a bracha for the twins that were about to be born.
