Twelve silver bowls, twelve silver washing basins, and twelve gold spoons. That's a lot of silver and gold!

No, I wish! It's in today's chitas. That's the total of all the dishes brought by different nesi’im to the Mishkan.

Why were the nesi’im bringing dishes to the Mishkan?

Each nasi brought a special karban for the Mishkan from his shevet. For twelve days, they all came one by one and brought their special gift to Hashem.

Hey, why don't we bring in one of the nesi’im and ask him about it?

Sure. Nachshon ben Aminadav was the first, but we spoke to him last week. I’ll call up a nasi from a different shevet this time. Guess which one!

Uh, Nasi of Shevet Asher, can you tell us what it was like to bring a special karban from your shevet to the Mishkan?

Of course! First of all, my name is Pag'iel ben Achran. And ahhhh, what a z’chus it was! But let me tell you about the best part.

Before we started bringing our own karbanos, all of us shevatim came together and brought a joint gift—one from all of the Yidden—to the Mishkan.

The wagons and oxen to carry the Mishkan! That was from all of the Yidden together!

You got it. When we all came together to bring a gift, we showed that we are all “Am Echad”! All twelve shevatim are one united nation.

That shows that all of us Yidden need to work together to build the Mikdash Hashlishi!

Yes. We need to remember that we are united and working together. Only after that can we each do our own special missions.

Like my mission as a girl to light Shabbos and Yom Tov candles?

And mine to prepare to put on tefillin at my Bar Mitzvah?

Yes, yes, and yes. When you do your special mission, make sure to remember that we are Am Echad.

So everything good that we do brings goodness to all Yidden all over the world!

In Eretz Yisroel, in New York, and even in Bangkok—wherever that is. Our mitzvos bring light and kedusha not just to us, but to all Yidden!

Yes! The Rambam tells us that every good thing we do, think, or say brings geulah to all Yidden!
