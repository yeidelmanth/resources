<p class="lead">One thing matters to a soldier more than anything else. Can you guess?</p>

Not their comfort. Not their lives. They just want the war to be won!

Exactly. Their mission is the most important.

But they are soldiers! I guess I don't feel like a soldier. Sometimes I’m afraid to do the right thing. Like I am worried about losing out.

Yeah! Like when my cousins slept over for Shabbos and my mother asked me to give up my bed. I didn’t want to say yes!

It’s hard to give up something we want for what Hashem wants.

But that fear? It’s the Yetzer Hara lying to you.

<span class="mc" style="color:#cf8715">The Yetzer Hora is telling you that if you give up something of yours then you’ll be left with NOTHING. You’ll be a NOBODY.</span>

What an evil lie! Of course that’s not true.

Right, because Hashem wants us to be SOMEBODY.

Well, He wouldn’t have chosen us as His soldiers if we were nobodies.

Maybe it’s just a matter of listening to my true self.

The Yetzer Tov! The voice in my head that feels calm, happy, and confident.

Right on! Because by dedicating ourselves to Hashem we aren’t losing anything. Instead we are doing what WE really want deep down.

And then not only do we survive— but we thrive!!!
