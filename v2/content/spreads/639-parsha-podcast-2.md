chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

I wish I had been alive during Yetzias Mitzrayim! It must have been so much fun with all the blood and frogs and wild animals! And then the Yam Suf splitting … oh, how I wish!

Eww, Yossi! Blood everywhere? That’s gross! Plus, they were slaves in Mitzrayim, and that’s not fun.

If it wasn’t fun, then why is our guest this week called a “Happy Little Girl”?

Hi, Yael! We were just talking about all the stuff that had to happen before Yetzias Mitzrayim. You know, like the makkos and the mitzvos we got. Was it really cool and fun or just scary?

It was awesome! We got to bring a Karban Pesach. Isn’t that exciting? Hashem said it was really important, and if we did it, He’d jump over our houses and save us during Makkas Bechoros. It was my first real mitzvah! I was so so excited!

Amazing! Did you have to shecht the lamb yourself, though?

My tatty shechted it, and then I got to eat it! Everyone in the house, even my youngest sister, had to eat the karban for the mitzvah to be done correctly. It was one of my favorite days ever! The first time I got to do a real, live mitzvah! Ooh … I’m jumping up and down, just remembering it!

Wow! I’m so excited for you that I also want to jump up and down! But what were you saying about Hashem saving you from Makkas Bechoros?

Yes! Yes! Hashem said that if we do the mitzvah, then He will jump over our houses. But He didn’t mean that He’ll just jump over our houses.

Oh, right! I learned that for everyone who did the mitzvah, Hashem jumped over anything that was stopping them from leaving Mitzrayim.

Okay, but why did Hashem make kids eat the Karban Pesach? Was it really so important for you to join in that mitzvah?

Well, we kids were really excited about Hashem, Torah, and mitzvos! Even though we were born in Mitzrayim, we were even more excited than the adults.

Right! That’s why, when the entire Yam Suf split, the kids were the first ones to recognize Hashem! They pointed at Him and said "זֶה קֵלִי—This is my Hashem.”

Oh my, I was just sooo excited! Woohoo! Hashem is the best!

Ooh, I get it. The kids got a part in the mitzvah of Karban Pesach because it was a preparation for Yetzias Mitzrayim, which they were so excited about.

Well, I’m super excited to leave this golus! With super-duper miracles, too! So, what can I do to make it happen sooner?

The Rebbe told us already. We soldiers in Tzivos Hashem should do exactly what the first Jewish kids did. The kids had a big part in preparing for Yetzias Mitzrayim, to the point that they recognized Hashem first. Today, we need to have a major part in preparing for the Geulah, too.

And now, let’s ask all the other chayolim to think about it: How can you have a major part in preparing for Geula?

…and that’s another fabulous episode of the Parsha Podcast. Thank you, Yael, for joining us! We’ll be sure to think the question over. See you all chayolim next week!
