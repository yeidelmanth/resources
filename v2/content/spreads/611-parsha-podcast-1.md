chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

I wonder what it felt like when the Yidden put up the Mishkan for the first time.

It must have been really exciting! I heard there was a big fire that came from Hashem!

Yup, there was! And then they all fell on their faces!

I don’t know. The Chumash says that they fell on their faces.

I think we’d better ask them. Or at least one of them.

Calling up! A Yid who fell on his face when the Mishkan was set up for the first time…

Wow, the Parsha Podcast! This is amazing! Baruch Hashem!

He means to ask you what it was like when the Mishkan was set up for the first time.

Oh it was wonderful! I can’t describe it! We had worked so hard to build the entire Mishkan, and then to bring up karbanos for Hashem … Oh, just remembering it makes me shake with joy! The kohanim brought up a karban olah and then the chalavim—the first karbanos that Hashem asked for in the Mishkan. And then—then suddenly a big fire came from Hashem and burnt the karbanos! Aaaahhhhh!

Because, Hashem! Hashem!! Hashem came and fulfilled the brachos of Moshe and Aharon! That means that Hashem is now always with us! With joy and delight! When we saw that happen, we all fell on our faces.

Of course we did! To thank Hashem! We bowed with our faces to the ground and praised Hashem! It was the happiest moment of my life. Ah! Just thinking of it makes me dance!

But what about me? I want to be as happy as you! I also want to see Hashem with me!

What do you mean? You also can! Don’t you get it? Moshe and Aharon’s bracha! Hashem is right over us. You can see it whenever you want!

“וְהִּנֵה ה׳ נִּצַב עָלָיו … ּובֹוחֵן ּכְלָיֹות וְלֵב”

Yes! Yes! This is the happiest thing ever! Just think about it: Hashem is standing over you and watching you serve Him!

Okay, now I’m getting happy just like you! Hashem is watching us and gave us so many mitzvos, so that we can be with Him!

Nu, so sing a niggun! If you feel happy, then do what we did and praise Hashem!

"יִשְׂמַח יִשְׂרָאֵל בְעֹושָׁיו—We rejoice with Hashem, Who made us." Hashem is happy that His world is a "דִירָה "ּבְתַחְּתֹונִים, and so we’re happy, too. This is the real happiness!

So, chayolim, think about it: How can you feel true happiness?

…and that’s another fabulous episode of the Parsha Podcast. Reb Yid, thank you for joining us! We’ll be sure to think the question over. See you all chayolim next week!
