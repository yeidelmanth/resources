<h3>Deeper and Brighter</h3>

Usually, on Chanukah, the Shir family brings their music everywhere: concerts, menorah parades, nursing homes and more. But this year, some performances were cancelled and now they’re snowed in!

“Maybe we can get out through the window,” suggests Tzvi.

Then the lights go out.

“The power lines!” says Tatty Shir. “No city lights... but that means our menorahs will be brighter than ever!”

Tatty, Mendel, Dovid, Tzvi, and little Yossi and Shneur begin to light their menorahs. As they sing Haneiros Halalu, Mommy Shir’s tambourine, Sara’s viola, and all the other girls’ instruments join in.

The little flames seem to dance along, deeper, brighter, and stronger than usual in the complete darkness.

Then Tatty says, “The very end of Golus is the darkest darkness, but it will turn into the first moment of Geula! Then, the same way we

Tzvi pipes in, “And after we watch Aharon Hakohen light the menorah, we’ll sing this niggun with the entire Am Yisrael!”

can point to these

Chanukah lights, we’ll

be able to point out

how Hashem is clearly everywhere.”
