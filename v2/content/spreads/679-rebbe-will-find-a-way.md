A few weeks later, Bubby started feeling unwell. She went to the doctor to make sure that everything was okay.

The doctor examined her and smiled. “It’s normal for you to be feeling this way,” he told Bubby. “You’re expecting a baby!”

Soon after our bubby and zeidy got married, Bubby needed surgery.

Baruch Hashem, she recovered. But afterward, the doctors explained that the surgery had caused permanent damage. It would now be very hard for her to have children.

For the next three years, Bubby and Zeidy never gave up hope.

They davened and tried everything the doctors suggested. They wrote to the Rebbe often, asking for a bracha.

But time kept passing, and they still didn’t have children.

At the time, Bubby and Zeidy were on shlichus in Argentina. Sometimes, when they traveled, they would stop in New York and go to the Ohel.

But it was always just a stopover along the way. It was never a special trip of its own.

As Yud Shvat, תשנ”ח (1998) came closer, Bubby and Zeidy had an idea.

“Let’s travel to the Rebbe,” they decided. “We won’t visit anywhere else. We’ll go only to ask for the bracha that we want so much.”

On Vov Shvat, they landed in New York and went straight to the Ohel.

Bubby and Zeidy stayed in Crown Heights for a few weeks, returning to the Ohel at every possible opportunity.

“Rebbe, please bentch us with children," they davened.

When they returned to Argentina, Bubby and Zeidy began preparing for a new medical treatment that was suggested to them.
