Now that a few years had passed, Mommy and Tatty felt it was the right time for me to make the switch, so I could learn Chassidus properly in a cheder that taught al taharas hakodesh.

But even after making this decision, they still wondered if it was really the best thing for me. Switching schools is not simple or easy. And now, Mommy felt she had received a clear bracha from the Rebbe. It felt like the Rebbe was telling her: I know what’s on your mind. You are doing the right thing.

Mommy walked out of the Ohel feeling calm and confident. She knew she had the Rebbe’s brachos for her test, for my chinuch, and for our whole family.

After davening at the Ohel, Mommy went to watch the Living Torah video. The Rebbe’s message surprised her.

Mommy is a doctor, but it took her a long time to become one. For years and years, she spent tons of time studying and taking tests.

Since we live close to the Ohel, Mommy went there before every big test to ask the Rebbe for a bracha for hatzlacha. She always davened that she should pass, so she could become a doctor and help other Yidden, especially frum women.

The day before the test, Mommy went to the Ohel, like she always did. She wrote a pan, asking for a bracha for our family and for hatzlacha with the test.

After davening and saying Tehillim, she went to watch the Rebbe video.

Right when she came into the room with the screen, Mommy gasped. The Rebbe was talking about someone “who has devoted many years to studying medicine.”

Mommy couldn’t believe it. She had studied for so many years, and now she was about to take her very last test. Hearing the Rebbe

say those exact words felt like a personal bracha. She listened carefully, trying not to miss a single word.

The Rebbe continued speaking. He said that just like parents want their child to go to a doctor who studied medicine for many years, they should also choose the best chinuch, with teachers who are true experts in Torah and Chassidus.
