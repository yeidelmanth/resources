When our family’s security was threatened, Tatty turned to the Rebbe. Through a letter in the Igros Kodesh, the Rebbe guided us to safety!

In the early years of our shlichus, our family didn’t have a big Chabad House. Instead, we used part of our own house as a shul for our shlichus activities.

One night in 5776 (תשע״ו), between Yom Kippur and Sukkos, Tatty and Mommy were up very late preparing for Yom Tov.

They had just put up security cameras around the house, and Mommy wondered, “Do you think anyone ever tries coming inside our property?”

“I don’t know,” Tatty replied. “Let’s go look at the camera recordings.”

Tatty and Mommy went to the computer to check the recordings. “Oh no!” they gasped in alarm. Around our house is a big grassy area, surrounded by a

gate with four entrances. And at that very moment, a man was trying to break into one of the entrances in the gate!

Tatty dialed 911 with trembling hands. Within moments, the police came with flashing lights and sirens to make sure that everyone was safe.

Tatty and Mommy were very shaken. The next day, Tatty wrote a letter to the Rebbe about the man who tried to come inside our property the night before, asking for a bracha to keep our family safe.
