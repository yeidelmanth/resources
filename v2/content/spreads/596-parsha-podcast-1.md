Hi there! We’re the Levin Family: Basya, Mimi, Chaim and

Yossi—chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

I’ll never be as smart as Rebbi Rosen. He knows so much.

Chaim, you can become a talmid chacham too! Just keep learning!

I’m pretty sure Rebbi Rosen knows the whole Torah… I barely even know Chumash Bereishis!

Hello, tree, welcome to our podcast. Is there something you’d like to share with us today?

Well, for starters, I can help you out with Parshas Vayechi.

The last Parsha of Chumash Bereishis! Where Yaakov Avinu bentches his children.

You really know your stuff! Now, do you remember when I was planted?

Yeah! Tatty planted this tiny little seed one day, and then slowly it turned into a whole big tree—you!

Yeah, so in Parshas Vayechi the Torah calls you all seeds. By “you all,” I mean all of Yaakov Avinu’s children. You Yidden.

Okay, so I’m a little seed. What’s to learn from that?

Yes, but if you plant it in good soil, water it, let it get sunlight, then with lots of brachos from Hashem—

It slowly becomes a big tree! Like you! Or like that big oak tree over there!

Yup. I came from a tiny seed. Now I’m a big tree, and I even grew apples—with seeds of their own!

So we Yidden are called seeds because we can turn into trees?

Yes. You start off as a simple neshama— you don’t know much Torah, and haven't done many mitzvos yet. But then you learn more and more Torah, grow strong yiras Shamayim, get plenty of Chassidishe warmth—

—brachos from Hashem, and bam! We’re a nice big tree!

Exactly. A tree full of Torah and mitzvos that adds so much goodness to the world, with delicious fruits that have more seeds to make even more trees of Torah and mitzvos!

And of course, the more Torah and mitzvos we do, the bigger and better trees we become!

Wow. That was really helpful. Now every time I go outside and see trees, it will remind me how big and fruitful I can become when I learn Torah and do more mitzvos. Thanks, Tree!
