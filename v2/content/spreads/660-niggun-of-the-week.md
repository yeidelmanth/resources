<h3>Nothing Stops the Shirs</h3>

As the summer comes to an end, the Shir family is excited for the new year ahead.

Everyone wants it to be perfect by Elul time, when they will be having a performance for family and friends: a boys’ concert and a girls’ one…

They practice till the very walls sing. Twelve sounds combine in perfect harmony for the sweetest, happiest Elul concerts ever, with Tzvi’s piccolo right on key!

"With each note … with every word... " Tatty sings to the niggun’s high notes, "we are wishing each person in this whole world … to be inscribed and sealed … for a sweet new year!”

“With Moshiach!” adds Tzvi.

“Now,” Tatty smiles, “with our hearts and minds filled with this message, let's play!"
