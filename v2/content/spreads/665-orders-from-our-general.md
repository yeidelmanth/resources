<p class="lead">Take a moment to look around: the sun, moon, clouds, and sky; the birds, animals, plants, and trees—everything was created by Hashem.</p>

Now, think for a second: What’s the purpose of it all?

The first word of the Torah gives us a firsts,” meaning, two things that are known as “firsts” because they’re so important. What are these two “firsts”? They are the Yidden and the Torah.

Rashi explains that the entire world was created for us.

Yes, the entire world, and everything in it, was created for us Yidden to keep Torah and mitzvos. It’s all here for us to serve Hashem! When we think about what that means, how could we not do what He wants?

What is the world really for?

To be enjoyed just the way it is, without rules.

For Yidden to learn Torah and do mitzvos.

In the beginning, Hashem created the heavens and the earth.
