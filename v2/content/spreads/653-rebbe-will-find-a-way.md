Three years later, Zeidy was having a hard time. There were big bills to pay, and he didn’t know how he would manage.

One afternoon, Zeidy sat in his office and wrote a letter to the Rebbe online. Tears filled his eyes as he said Tehillim, asking for a bracha to continue his shlichus.

Just two minutes after sending the letter, the phone rang. It was Jason!

“Rabbi, please come to New York as soon as you can,” he said.

Zeidy caught the first bus the next morning. When he walked into Jason’s office, Jason smiled and handed him a thick envelope filled with money.

For a moment, Zeidy could only stare at the envelope, completely stunned. Jason laughed. “Don’t you remember? I told the Rebbe I’d

helped Jason when his job felt impossible. And now, the Rebbe had helped Zeidy right when

He felt so lucky to be a Chossid of the Rebbe, who cares for every Yid!

Jason’s job wasn’t going well. Who could he turn to for help?

As the shliach to the University of Delaware, Zeidy helped many young Jewish students connect to Hashem. Even after they graduated and moved on, Zeidy stayed in touch. One of those students was Jason.

Jason worked in a big company in Manhattan. Every once in a while, he’d call Zeidy just to say hello. So when the phone rang one morning, Zeidy thought it was a regular call.

“Rabbi,” he cried, “business is terrible. I didn’t get even one new client for the company all year. I might lose my job. How will I support my family?”

Zeidy couldn’t offer business advice, but he did know where to turn for help.

“Let’s go to the Rebbe’s Ohel together,” he suggested.

The very next day, Zeidy drove two hours to Manhattan and picked Jason up. Then, they drove to the Ohel. After going to the mikvah,

Jason asked the Rebbe for a bracha, and he made a promise too: When my business becomes successful, I’ll help support Chabad at the University of Delaware.

That very week, Jason got a new client! Then another … and another! Before long, his business was doing great! By the end of the year, Jason even won an award for getting the most clients in the whole company!

He told his entire company the secret to his success. When Zeidy once came to visit, he couldn’t believe his eyes. Many of Jason’s coworkers had pictures of the Rebbe in their offices!

But Jason never mentioned his promise to help support Zeidy’s Chabad House. Zeidy didn’t feel comfortable reminding him, so he kept quiet.

Avtalyon, 9 Menachem Witkes, 7 Oholei Torah, Crown Heights
