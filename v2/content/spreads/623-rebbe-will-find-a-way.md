“Nu, are you still coming to California?” Rabbi Resnick asked.

“I just wrote to the Rebbe about our moving date and asked for a bracha!” Tatty said happily. He then shared what was holding us back from moving, and Rabbi Resnick offered to help.

Rabbi Resnick also told Tatty more about the school. He even had a daughter who would be in Chana’s class! Now Tatty was even more certain that we had the Rebbe’s brachos.

Feeling inspired, he decided to share these two incredible stories—of finding the kuntres and meeting Rabbi Resnick—with Shloimy Greenwald.

Shloimy was so amazed that he decided to give another big donation spread out over time. “Text me on the first day of every secular month, and I’ll send a payment.”

The first time Tatty texted him was on July 1st. That year, July 1st was Chof-Ches Sivan, the very same day that Tatty had received the kuntres from the Rebbe!

Soon afterwards, Tatty and Mommy moved on shlichus, feeling empowered by the Rebbe’s brachos.

When they had felt stuck and unsure of how to begin their shlichus, they turned to the Rebbe. And the Rebbe’s clear brachos gave them the ko’ach to move forward.

As chassidim, we know that when we turn to the Rebbe, the Rebbe helps and guides us!

When Tatty and Mommy felt stuck, the Rebbe’s brachos helped them move forward.

Tatty and Mommy were so excited: They were going on shlichus to Concord, California! They began planning their move, but things kept getting in the way. Almost a year passed, and they still weren’t able to go.

Tatty decided to go to the Ohel to ask the Rebbe for a bracha to move on shlichus.

On the way home, Tatty got a message from his cousin, Sruly Duchman.

“I found a kuntres from the Rebbe with your name on it. Is it yours?”

He sent a picture of the first page. Right away, Tatty recognized the handwriting—it was Bubby’s! This was the kuntres Tatty had gotten as a baby from the Rebbe, on Chof- Ches Sivan, תנש”א (1991).

That night, Tatty picked it up and brought it home. He started to learn from it, and realized something amazing.

Chof-Ches Sivan is the day that the Rebbe came to America. The kuntres was speaking about spreading Chassidus to “חֲצִי ּכַּדּור הַּתַחְּתֹון—the lower half of the world,” including America!

“Wait a minute,” Tatty thought. “That’s exactly what our shlichus is—spreading chassidus in America!”

Tatty and Mommy felt that the Rebbe was telling them, “You will go on shlichus and succeed!” They were so happy to get such a clear bracha.

They went back to the Ohel to thank the Rebbe. Even though many things still weren’t ready, they picked a date for their move: Yud- Gimmel Menachem Av.

In his letter, Tatty also asked for a bracha that our sister Chana should have an easy time switching to her new school in California.

He also wrote about a generous ba’al tzedakah, Reb Shloimy Greenwald, who had given a big donation to help with their shlichus.
