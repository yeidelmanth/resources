chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

How are you bored? I have, like, a million things to do!

That’s cuz you’re always playing sports. Basya, didn’t Mommy suggest that you learn Tanya in your free time?

Yeah! But I’m already doing that in school! I don’t need to learn more Tanya.

Hmm. I think our guest will be able to help us with this one.

And if not, I’ll help you figure something out after the podcast. Okay, Basya?

Okay! We shall now call … Mrs. Shifra Rosenbaum, mother of 12, and shlucha to the University of Wor–Workest– Work-es-teer! Or, something like that.

Perfect timing! I just finished learning with my Tanya chavrusa!

Wow, that’s amazing! You learn Tanya with a girl from the university?

Actually, I give a Tanya shiur for the college girls on Mondays. This was a chavrusa that I have with a friend. We learn the daily Tanya together.

Well, think of something that you really love doing.

Yeah, we know. Whenever you have even two minutes of free time, you get your mitt and ball and run to the park.

Well, there you go. If you really love something and are so excited about it, then you make sure to fill every free moment with that thing.

Exactly! Even though my days are so busy, I know that I have exactly fifteen minutes from when my kids go to school until I need to start working on other things. So, in those fifteen minutes, I call my friend and we learn the Tanya of the day.

Wow! You probably love learning Torah more than anyone I know! Besides maybe Chaim. He’s always learning.

Actually, I think the Yidden in this week’s parsha were also very excited.

Oh, right! Moshe said “ּתַעַבְדּון אֶת הָאֱֹלקִים עַל הָהָר הַזֶה—you will serve Hashem on this mountain,” and they felt so excited to get the Torah! They were literally counting the days until it happened.

But the Yidden were in the worst golus! Even when they stopped working as slaves, wouldn’t they be too tired for Torah?

Wow. I think those Yidden loved Torah even more than I love playing baseball.

Mm-hhm. I think so, too. But remember, getting excited about learning Torah isn’t just for the Yidden of the past. We can do the same!

And we can show how excited we are by filling all our free time with learning!
