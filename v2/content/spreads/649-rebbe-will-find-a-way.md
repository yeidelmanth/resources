Tatty and Mommy were so excited. They knew that with the Rebbe’s bracha, everything would work out.

Within weeks, the lease was signed, and we moved into our beautiful new home. It had plenty of space for our family, the preschool, and for all the Yidden who come to learn and daven. Now, our shlichus could grow even bigger and better than before!

We feel so lucky to have seen such a clear bracha from the Rebbe. The Rebbe is with us in our shlichus, helping us light up El Cerrito with the light of Torah and mitzvos!

Tatty and Mommy searched for months, but nothing worked out. Would they ever find the right home?

Just two-and-a-half years after moving on shlichus to El Cerrito, California, our Chabad House was already so busy! Many Yidden came for programs, our new preschool was full, and our family was growing, too.

But, there was one big problem: our house was way too small. The preschool was squeezed into the living room, and the programs felt crowded. We needed a bigger place to grow our shlichus.

In Adar, Tatty and Mommy began searching for a bigger home. But in El Cerrito, most houses are small, very expensive, and not built in a way that would work for a Chabad House.

Month after month, they looked and looked. After six months, they had already visited more than twenty- five houses! Each time, they hoped that it would be the right one. But, they just couldn’t find the right match.

Tishrei came and went, and we were still in our tiny house. Tatty and Mommy realized that something had to change. They needed a bracha from the Rebbe.

Right after Simchas Torah, Tatty flew to New York with a mekurav to visit the Ohel. Tatty wrote a letter to the Rebbe and poured out his heart, explaining why we needed to move and how hard the search was.

“Please, Rebbe,” he asked, “give us a bracha to find the right home quickly.”

After leaving the Ohel, Tatty went to Crown Heights. On his way there, his phone rang. It was Mommy, and she sounded excited!

“You won’t believe this!” she said. “A new house was just listed on Craigslist while you were at the Ohel! I just went to see it, and it's perfect!"
