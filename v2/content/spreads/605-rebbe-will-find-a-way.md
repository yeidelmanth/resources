Sometimes, we can find ourselves thinking: If only we still had the Rebbe here with us physically, everything would be so different! We would be able to get brachos and answers to all of our questions.

But the Rebbe finds a way to show us that he is right here with us, even though we can't see him with our own eyes. And any minute now, we will see the Rebbe with our physical eyes as we march towards the Geulah!

Our aunt gave Mommy a Rebbe dollar by accident —but it wasn’t a mistake.

Do you recognize our names from last week’s story? Yup, it’s us Wilansky kids again, with another incredible story involving our mother and a Rebbe dollar. The story goes like this:

Last summer, our family flew to New York for a chasunah and stayed for a few weeks.

The entire time, Mommy was very nervous about our flight back home. There was an issue with her passport, and she was worried that it would cause problems at the airport security. She kept those feelings to herself, though, and tried to push the worrisome thoughts out of her mind.

On our last day in Crown Heights, our aunt and uncle, who were visiting from Seattle, came to say goodbye. As they were leaving, our aunt handed Mommy some money as shliach mitzvah gelt. “We’re going to the Ohel now,” she said. “It’s my last chance to visit before my birthday.”

After they left, Mommy looked at the bills in her hand and was shocked to see small words

written on one of them. It was a dollar from the Rebbe!

A few minutes later, our aunt returned. “What happened?” she asked.

“You gave me one of your Rebbe dollars by mistake! Baruch Hashem, I noticed in time!”

Shaken, our aunt switched it for a regular dollar. “I can’t believe it,” she later told Mommy. “I got this dollar from

the Rebbe thirty-three years ago, before my birthday. And now, the Rebbe found a way to give it to me again before my birthday, just as I was on my way to the Ohel. What a z’chus!”
