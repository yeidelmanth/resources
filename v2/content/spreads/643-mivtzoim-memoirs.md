Mivtzoim—they feel like they’ve been around forever! Can you even imagine life without them? Yet, unbelievably, before the Rebbe introduced each of the mitzvah campaigns that we know so well, they simply didn’t exist.

Join us as we explore imaginary old diaries that could have belonged to kids just like you—kids who might have lived during the time when the Rebbe first announced many of these mivtzoim.

Hadassah was just five years old when Mivtza Purim began, and while

her handwriting became smaller and neater over the years, the mivtza grew

bigger and more dramatic. Just make sure not to read her diary upside down!

My siblings and I just spent the whole day pretending to be airplanes. We jumped off the couches—dropping papers on each other's heads—and then ran to catch as many as we could.

Don’t think we spend every Sunday doing this! After a Purim like this one, though, it’s all we can think about!

You must have heard about it, Diary! On Purim morning, Chassidim flew an airplane over a huge area in Eretz Yisroel, dropping flyers onto people’s heads! The flyers had a Purim sicha about being like Mordechai—not bending, not bowing, and doing mitzvos with Jewish pride—and people were running in all directions to catch them.

It’s been ten years since Mivtza Purim began, and it just grows every year. This Purim, Chassidim in Eretz Yisroel (some of them in their soldier uniforms!), flew in an army airplane to bring the mitzvos of Purim to faraway army bases.

They brought tens of thousands of Mishlo’ach Manos, and also Megillos to lein with. The soldiers were so moved! Each Mishlo’ach Manos had a picture of the Rebbe in it, and many soldiers stuck it next to their beds.

This year, the Rebbe wanted the mivtzoim with soldiers to be even bigger and better. He explained that the Purim activities with bereaved families (families who are sad because someone passed away) should be done with a k’nak (extra excitement)!

I also heard that the Rebbe didn’t start the Purim farbrengen until he knew that the Chassidim who had flown to the Sinai desert for mivtzoim with soldiers— and got stuck there—were able to listen to the farbrengen through the live hookup.

Just when we thought Mivtza Purim couldn’t get any bigger, the Rebbe said we must include the prisons. Wow! That sounds complicated.

B’ezras Hashem, in the z’chus of all of these mitzvos, Klal Yisroel will be released from the prison of Golus, and Moshiach will come right now!

Rabbi Dovid Raskin just made an announcement after davening that everyone who goes on Purim mivtzoim this year will get a dollar from the Rebbe! That doesn’t happen often, and it shows how special Purim mivtzoim are to the Rebbe!

The Rebbe just held an unusual weekday farbrengen last night, encouraging us to go on Purim mivtzoim. Why on a weekday? So it can be broadcasted for everyone to hear the words straight from the Rebbe!

The Rebbe said that since Mishlo’ach Manos must be given from man to man and woman to woman, we women and girls are super important for this mitzvah.

The Rebbe also emphasized that Mivtza Purim isn’t just for Lubavitchers. It’s for all of Klal Yisroel!

Today, Tatty came into the kitchen as I was putting a wafer and a tangerine into my Mishlo’ach Manos bags. “Hadassah, I’m so glad that you are making your own Mishlo’ach Manos!” he said. “You’re so big now, already five years old!”

“Of course, Tatty. It’s my favorite mitzvah! And look,” I told him, pointing to four pennies on the counter. “I have Matanos L’evyonim ready, too!”

“It’s because of the letter from the Rebbe,” I explained.

“That’s right!” Mommy piped in. “As soon as I heard about the Rebbe’s letter that young children should do Mishlo’ach Manos and Matanos L’evyonim by themselves, we jumped into action and went straight to the grocery store!”
