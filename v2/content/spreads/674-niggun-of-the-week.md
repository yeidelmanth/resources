<h3>The Cousins are Coming</h3>

“Kids, there’s been a fire in Aunt Leora and Uncle Michoel’s house, but boruch Hashem, no one was hurt! And guess what? The whole family will be coming to stay with us.”

The Shir children love their cousins, though they can be a handful. They get ready to make them feel at home.

“Oh! Tomorrow is Hei Teves!” exclaims Aunt Leora, tearing up. “This has been such an enormous challenge, but now that we’re singing and dancing, I am certain we’ll break through the hardship—and like the Rebbe’s library, we’ll all have a geula!”

“They must feel as lost as the Rebbe’s seforim before Hei Teves,” says Tzvi.

“But all their books got burned,” Freidel says sadly. She loves to read.

Mommy Shir shoos them off to clean their rooms.

“It’s good to see you all!” says Uncle Michoel as he walks through the door some time later.
