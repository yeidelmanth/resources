chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Yossi, please can you tell me? Pleeeeeease? I’ll be your best sister!

Hey! That's not fair, Basya! Anyway, Yossi, what’s the secret? What aren’t you telling her?

Nu-uh! Every day after school, Yossi disappears for like an hour. He goes somewhere or does something—and he won’t tell me what he’s doing! I asked Mommy, and she said it’s something very special, but Yossi doesn’t want anyone to know.

Aww, now I’m curious, too! What is it, Yossi? C’mon, you can tell us. We won’t tell anyone else.

Mommy said it’s something special? Hmm … Is it a mitzvah? Yossi, why can’t you tell us?

I’m not saying. How about we all just do what we have to do—

Can you get Yossi to tell us what mitzvah he keeps running away to do?

Basya, Mar Ukva is like the last person who would do that for us. Do you know who he is?

Well, yes. But he also did something special that we can all learn from. Mar Ukva, can you tell us the story about how you hid in the oven?

No, no. My wife and I were hiding from a poor man. We had just secretly dropped off some money at his house, like we did every evening, and I guess the man was curious about who kept giving him money.

So he tried to sneak up on you? And find out who you were?

Exactly! That’s why we jumped into the nearest hiding spot, which was a huge oven.

Well, if the poor man saw us, he would have been embarrassed. We didn’t want that to happen!

So, you jumped into a super hot oven to save one poor man from being a little bit embarrassed. Wow!

Wait. I don’t get what this has to do with this week’s parsha.

The end of Parshas Tezaveh speaks about the mizbei’ach hazahav on which the ketores was brought. Since this was the most important avodah, it's only mentioned after all of the other keilim and the garments of the kohanim. Do you know how the kohen would bring the ketores?

Yeah. Every day, a kohen would go into the Kodesh. He would go all alone; nobody was allowed to be there with him. It was just him and Hashem.

Exactly. And that is also where the Shechina came down. Guess what we learn from this?

Oh, I know! That we should do mitzvos quietly, and not boast or make a big deal about them.

Yes. The best kind of mitzvos are ones kept between just you and Hashem. Especially the mitzvah of tzedaka.
