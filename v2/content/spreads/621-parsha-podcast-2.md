chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Rosh Hashana is coming up, and we’re about to start a new year as Tzivos Hashem soldiers. We need to call someone to help us prepare.

We are! We learned in school that the parsha always matches up with the time of year. So we’re gonna call the twins, Tova and Tuvia. They’ll also help us prepare for Rosh Hashana.

It’s so exciting to talk to the kids who will bring Moshiach!

And we are so excited to talk to kids who were actually there when Moshe gave his final instructions to us. Please can you tell us how we can prepare for Rosh Hashana?

As you said, Moshe Rabbeinu has been talking to us every day and giving us final instructions before we go into Eretz Yisroel.

When Moshe Rabbeinu started talking today, he said: ”םֶ“אַּתֶם נ ִּצָבִים הַּיֹום ּכֻּלְכֶם לִפְנֵי ה׳ אֱֹלקֵיכ

“Today, you are all standing here together before Hashem.”

It means that all of the Yidden were standing together in front of Hashem. “םֶּכֻּלְכ—all of you,” united with Ahavas Yisroel.

Close. We were united because us Yidden are always all working for one King. And not just any king: the greatest and awesomest King ever!

Sometimes we call really famous and amazing people for our podcast, like Avraham Avinu, and even though they’re really really nice, it can still be kind of scary. Like, I’m not scared of them, but it makes me want to behave my absolute best. But they’re still just people! I mean, as amazing and powerful as they are, Hashem is waaay greater than them.

I know what you mean, Basya. I’ve also felt honored and nervous—and of course, very happy!—to be talking to such famous and important people!

Now do you understand what made all of us so united?

I think so! You were about to make a bris with Hashem. You were promising to be His soldiers. It’s a really big deal to be in front of Hashem and serving Him in that way.

That must’ve been what made everyone feel united! Because everyone felt how amazing and close Hashem is, and sharing such a strong feeling brought everyone together!

Exactly! We realized that we were standing in front of Hashem. And we were all so proud to be there, and so ready to do whatever He wanted, that it made us into one unit. We all stood before Hashem together. As one!

And now we’re going to renew that promise! On Rosh Hashana, we’re going to crown Hashem as our King, and promise to do everything He wants!

We’ll feel proud to be Hashem’s soldiers and be on our best behavior. And we’ll do it all with simcha, because we are so lucky to be in Hashem’s army!

And with Ahavas Yisroel, of course. Completely united, like your generation was, when you made the bris for the first time.

I’m sure that Hashem, the Commander-in-Chief, is very proud and happy to have you in His army!

You will crown Him as King, and He will send Moshiach and the final Geulah for all of His soldiers!
