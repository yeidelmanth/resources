He looked closely at the two dollars— the one his wife had received that day, and his undated dollar—and sure enough, the serial numbers were exactly one after the other!

Our uncle couldn’t believe it. All these years, he had two Rebbe dollars from that day, but never knew it! One had been hiding the whole time, waiting to be discovered.

Just hours earlier, he had asked the Rebbe for a sign. And now, in the middle of the night, the Rebbe found a way to answer him: Regards from the Rebbe. Don’t worry about giving away the dollar. I’ve given you another one.

Our uncle felt calm again. He knew the Rebbe was with him, encouraging him to keep going.

He worked hard to raise the rest of the money, and Baruch Hashem, he succeeded. His Chabad House grew, and he was able to help many more Yidden grow in their Yiddishkeit.

And that surprise dollar? He still has it, safe in his collection.

in our hands. We just have to look a little closer.

In the middle of the night, our uncle got an email with a surprising message.

Our uncle is a shliach in Argentina. He helps all types of Yidden, including many children who come from poor families.

About 15 years ago, he needed a bigger Chabad House for his programs. He tried hard to raise money, but it wasn’t going well.

Week after week went by, and our uncle felt more stuck than ever. How will we ever get a bigger Chabad House? he worried.

Feeling desperate, he promised a certain Yid that he would give him a Rebbe dollar if he would give a donation. The man agreed, and our uncle gave him one of his Rebbe dollars.

At first, he felt relieved. Baruch Hashem, we got the donation! But he still needed to raise a lot more money, and as the days went by, he regretted what he had done.

Did I do the right thing? he thought. I still don’t have enough money … and now I gave away a Rebbe dollar, too!

Night after night, the same thoughts kept him awake. Maybe I shouldn’t have done that. Maybe there was another way.

One day, he was feeling especially sad. “Rebbe,” he cried, “please show me that you’re with me and that I’m doing the right thing.”

Late that night, as he was getting ready for bed, our uncle heard a “ding” from his phone.

He went to check, and saw it was from his friend. Then, as he read the subject line, his eyes grew wide. It said, in Hebrew, “דַ״ׁש מֵהָרַּבִי— Regards from the Rebbe.”

He clicked on the email, his heart racing. Inside was an old picture of him getting a dollar from the Rebbe—a time when he and his wife had gone together.

Our uncle was about to close the email, but something made him stop. There was something strange about the picture.

He leaned closer … and froze. The Rebbe was giving him two dollar bills!

Two!? he thought. That can’t be right. I only remember getting one!

Suddenly, he realized: The dollar he had given away to the man was from the day of that picture!
