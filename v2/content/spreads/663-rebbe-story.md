<p class="lead">How Can Sukkos</p>

Make Us One?

in one sukkah. Orders from our General

When Moshiach comes, Hashem will make a very special sukkah for the Yidden. Chazal explain that it will be made from the skin of the Livyasan fish, and all Yidden will sit together inside. Hashem Himself will be there, too. Just imagine: every Jew together in one sukkah, with Hashem right there with us!

The happiness that we’ll feel will be even greater than the joy of Rosh Hashana. Sitting together in the sukkah of the Livyasan will be a time of pure closeness, unity, and simcha between Hashem and the Yidden.

But here’s something amazing: Even now, while we are still in Galus, we already have a chance to feel that even nowadays! And by fulfilling this mitzvah in Galus, we will be zocheh to sit in the special sukkah of the Geulah.

The same is true of the great achdus that we’ll feel. When you sit in the sukkah nowadays with your family, friends, and guests, you show how all Yidden truly belong together. Differences don’t matter when it comes to sitting in a sukkah. What matters is that we’re sitting together.

<span class="mc">Redt Tzu Kinder,</span>

All Yidden are fit to sit together

When you celebrate Sukkos, remember that your actions now prepare the world for what’s coming next. Be sure to fill your sukkah time with simcha, achdus, and caring for other Yidden. Because with that, you bring closer the day when we’ll all sit together with Hashem in the sukkah of the Livyasan!
