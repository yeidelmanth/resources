“Yes!” Mommy quickly typed back. And just like that, she helped me sign up! We had been trying for months, and right after asking the Rebbe for help, everything went quickly and smoothly.

I was so excited!. I began filling out my missions every day, and I immediately saw that they were helping me to stay focused on serving Hashem.

I worked on saying brachos carefully, davening with more kavana, and doing more mitzvos during the day. Sure enough, I started earning points and moving up in rank like a real soldier. It felt so good to be part of this powerful army of kids from all over the world; all working together on the same mission: bringing Moshiach.

And the best part of all? I feel like the General of the army himself picked me to be his soldier. I’ll do everything I can to make him proud!

We kept trying to enroll in Tzivos Hashem, but nothing seemed to work.

I'll never forget the long journey that I went through in order to join Tzivos Hashem. I have wanted to enroll for, like, forever! It's the most amazing program that helps kids connect with Hashem and bring Moshiach, and I really, really wanted to be a part of it.

But, boy, was it hard! My school doesn’t have a Tzivos Hashem base, so we had to register on our own. But when Mommy tried signing me up, she kept running into one challenge after another. First, she looked online and found a few numbers to call. But when she tried calling, she couldn’t get through to the right person.
