And now, thirty years after Zeidy had given away this Rebbe dollar, it returned at the exact time when Mommy had gone to the Ohel asking for clarity about her engagement!

Mommy felt the Rebbe’s message clearly. The dollar was marked with a gimmel, and the bochur whom Mommy was about to get engaged to had the last name Gelman, which also begins with a gimmel!

There was one more detail that made it even more special. The dollar was from the year תשמ"ח (1988), a Shnas Hakhel. Mommy’s engagement and chassuna also took place in a Shnas Hakhel!

This Rebbe dollar had traveled across distant countries and for many years, only to arrive right on time. It was just the bracha that Mommy had asked for, showing her that the Rebbe was with her every step of the way.

Right when Mommy asked the Rebbe for a bracha, Zeidy got surprising news.

Before Tatty and Mommy got engaged, Mommy went to daven at the Ohel. She asked the Rebbe for a bracha that her engagement should happen at the right time, and that she should feel sure about her decision.

“I have exciting news,” he said. “You already got a bracha!”

Mommy couldn’t understand. “A Rebbe dollar? How could that be?”

There’s a rabbi in Brazil named Rabbi Battat who makes a big sefarim sale every year. When people come to buy, they sometimes pay with American dollars. One day, a man bought some sefarim and handed Rabbi Battat a few bills as payment.

Right away, Rabbi Battat noticed that one of them had Hebrew words on it. “Here, take it back!” he exclaimed. “It’s a dollar from the Rebbe!”

But the man just waved his hand and said, “It’s okay, you can keep it.”
