Have you seen how exciting Hachayol Magazine looks every week? That’s thanks to our aunt Nechama! She brainstorms ideas, chooses designs, and makes every issue so much fun to read.

Nechama is from Brazil, but she has lived in New York for many years. The government gave her special papers that let her stay in America for work, plus permission to travel out of the country for family visits. But after some time, the permit expired.

This past year, Nechama really wanted to spend Pesach with her family in Brazil. She tried to renew her travel permit, but each time, her application got sent back— unopened.

There’s no chance that I’ll get a new permit before Pesach, she realized sadly. Instead, she booked tickets to Los Angeles to spend Pesach with a friend.

Little did she know, something amazing was about to happen. While she was

there, her friend’s family thought of a shidduch for her—with their son!

Baruch Hashem, a short while later, Nechama and their son (our uncle) got engaged! Now she understood why she hadn’t gotten her travel permit in time. She needed to be in Los Angeles to meet her chosson!

Feeling so grateful to Hashem, Nechama knew exactly where she wanted the chasuna to be: in Brazil, with her community. She davened, Please, Hashem, help me get my travel permit in time so I can get married in Brazil. There aren’t many frum chasunas in my community, and I want to show everyone what a Yiddishe and Chassidishe chasuna looks like.

Nechama applied for an emergency permit, sure that Hashem would help. But the application was rejected.

Nechama didn't give up. She wrote a letter to the Rebbe, asking for a bracha.
