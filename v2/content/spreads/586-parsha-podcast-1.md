Hi there! We’re the Levin Family: Basya, Mimi, Chaim and

Yossi—chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Note: These images are for illustrative purposes only and are not meant to be an accurate depiction of the holy faces of the Tzaddikim.

Do you ever feel like there’s no point in us doing this?

Not at all. We’re finding out such amazing lessons from every Parsha. We’re doing amazing stuff here!

But what’s the point? We’re just four kids in a massive world. No one else cares about the Torah. They for sure won’t listen to us when we try to tell them about Hashem.

Oh no, Yossi! It sounds like your Yetzer Hara is talking. You’re a soldier in Tzivos Hashem—don’t listen!

Okay enough, guys. We’ve got a mission to do. Parshas Noach; makes sense we bring Noach this time. Yossi?

Oh, hello! Thank you for inviting me to your Parsha Podcast, but I’m sure you all know the story of the mabul already.

But we never heard it from you, and how you felt about it.

Wait! Why didn’t I think of asking you? Noach, did you ever want to give up?

Yeah. In your days the whole world was doing the wrong thing! How did you keep following Hashem? Didn’t everyone make fun of you?

You’re right. Everyone else in the world was doing the exact opposite of me. I served Hashem and did mitzvos, and they did, well, the opposite. When I built the teiva, they all teased me and said I was nuts.

Why? Because it’s what Hashem wanted, of course! I didn’t care what everyone else was doing, I just did what I knew was right.

Yossi, you can tell your Yetzer Hara that you’re like Noach! Even if everyone is doing the wrong thing, you can still do Torah and mitzvos!

But nowadays it’s so much harder. This yucky Galus is hiding Hashem from us.

Hey, you’re the one who’s part of a massive army of tens of thousands of Tzivos Hashem soldiers all over the world! Talk about easier. Imagine if I had an army like that with me!

Ooh, take that, Yetzer Hara! No more fighting us now! With all of us teamed up, we can for sure win over the world with Torah and mitzvos. Tell him Yossi; tell your Yetzer Hara that you’re not afraid!

You’re right. Thanks, Noach! I’ve got no reason to be afraid of anyone. Not the rest of the big world, and not you, stinky Yetzer Hara. I’m part of a huge army of Tzivos Hashem, and together we can team up to bring Moshiach!

And when we’re all teamed up doing mitzvos, Hashem will help us and destroy our Yetzer Hara forever. Like the mabul destroyed the not-good people in your times, Noach.

And just like you were able to build a whole new world after the mabul, our huge army will bring the new world of Moshiach! Then we’ll enjoy learning Torah and doing mitzvos all day, like it says in davening, ּ"“ּכִי הֵם חַּיֵינּו, וְאֹורְֶך יָמֵינו— they, the Torah and mitzvos, are our life!
