chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Mommy said that we all need to eat before starting the podcast today.

Wow, Basya, you got plates, cups, cutlery … and you set the table so well!

Yum! My favorite! Peanut butter and pickle sandwich!

Thanks, Basya! You set everything up so nicely. This reminds me of a Rashi in this week’s parsha. םיְּׁׂפָטִים אֲשֶׁר ּתָשִ ״וְאֵלֶה הַמִש םֶלִפְנֵיה —These are the laws you shall place before them.” Rashi explains that Hashem is telling Moshe to “place \[the halachos\] before them” like a set table, where everything is ready and easy to take.

Yup! This is how the Torah needs to be delivered to Tzivos Hashem soldiers: nicely and clearly, so that we can get straight to the point.

And, just like food makes our bodies nice and strong, Torah keeps our neshamos strong and ready to do our Tzivos Hashem missions!

Also, just like a set table is clear and neat, when we fight against the Yetzer Hara, we make it clear that we are Yidden, because we behave like Yidden!

By the way, you know how sometimes Mommy says that we should call someone for the podcast, but she doesn’t explain why?

Yeah. Those are some of the coolest and strangest guests we’ve had.

Well, this time it's someone whom Mommy met at the bank. His name is Mr. Smith, and he's Great-Aunt Devorah’s neighbor.

No it’s not! And we’re soldiers in Hashem’s army. Who are you?

Interesting. How do I know for certain that you’re in Hashem’s army?

Well, for one thing, we have Jewish names. Like Yossi.

And you can’t see it, but we’re also dressed in our uniforms. Tznius clothes for the girls, and yarmulka and tzitzis for the boys.

I guess those are good signs. But I still don’t believe you.

What? Why don’t you believe us? Hey, we know the twelve pesukim by heart to win the fight against the Yetzer Hara!

Yeah, like my favorite one: '“שְׁמַע יִשְׂרָאֵל ה אֱלֹוקֵינּו ה' אֶחָד—Listen up, all Jews, Hashem is our G-d, Hashem is One!”

Huh. You said that verse like you actually live those words. But now, I have another question: Do you really think you can win against that Yetzer Hara? The enemy who makes people forget that G-d runs the world?

Yeah, because we know that “ּבְרֵאשִׁית ּבָרָא !אֱֹלקִים אֵת הַשָּׁמַיִם וְאֵת הָאָרֶץ— Hashem made everything in the world,” and He’s been running it since the beginning of creation. Hashem is inside everything, even in the pickles of my sandwich that make me strong so I can serve Him. The Yetzer Hara can’t fool me! I know who’s Boss!

If you don’t mind, I was about to make a bracha on my lemonade. !ּבָרּוְך אַּתָה ה' אֱלֹוקֵינּו מֶלְֶך הָעֹולָם שֶׁהַּכֹל נִהְיָה ּבִדְבָרֹו
