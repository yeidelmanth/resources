While looking for her rings, Mommy noticed a plain white envelope. What could be inside?

On the special day of Yud-Gimmel Tammuz, the day that the Frierdiker Rebbe was freed from prison, Mommy stood at the Rebbe’s Ohel.

Looking down at the pile of torn up letters, Mommy felt a great sense of awe. She thought of all the people who had poured out their hearts in this holy place, coming to the Rebbe for comfort, hope, and strength.

Just the day before, someone had told her how she had seen a revealed sign from the Rebbe right after davening at the Ohel. Mommy wished that she, too, could get such a clear sign from the Rebbe.

She read her pan, feeling the kedusha so strongly. How can I bring this holiness along when I go back home? she silently wondered. How can I feel that the Rebbe is with me?

Back in her car, Mommy suddenly felt an urgent need to look for her rings, which she had put away a week earlier. She opened the inner zipper of her purse where she thought she had put them, but they weren’t there.

Getting nervous, Mommy took everything out to see if her rings were between the papers and dollar bills. As she searched, she noticed a plain white envelope. What is this? Mommy wondered as she opened it up. Imagine her shock when she discovered that inside was a dollar from the Rebbe!
