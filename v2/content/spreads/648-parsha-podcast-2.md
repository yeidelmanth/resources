chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Because in Parshas Tzav, the Yidden brought all the parts for the Mishkan to Moshe Rabbeinu, and then the days of the shivas yemei hamilu’im began, and then, Moshe finally put it all together! Hooray!!!

Moshe Rabbeinu is so, so special! He also got to teach the kohanim in the Mishkan all of the stuff that they needed to learn! Can we call him this week?

It looks like we can only call Eliezer from the family of Merari.

Ooh. That’s nice! They’re the family of Levi’im who helped put up the Mishkan. Maybe he can give us some tips on how to finally get the Beis Hamikdash built.

I don’t know. It was working fine last week. It’s almost like someone specifically doesn’t want us to call Eliezer.

Hmm. That sounds familiar. I think I know who that would be.

The Yetzer Hara! Ugh! He’s so mean! Yossi, do you know how to fix it? We can’t give up and just let the Yetzer Hara stop us from learning our podcast lesson this week.

Yeah, I’m trying to figure this thing out. I think—oh hey! The mic stopped making noises. Now we can call our guest.

Yeah. The Yetzer Hara really didn’t want us to speak to you, but we didn’t give up! Are you all done setting up the Mishkan?

Yes! We have finally set it up. And, I hear that you chayolim are about to set up the Beis Hamikdash, as well! You’re right at the end of a long Golus of preparation, and your hard work is really paying off.

So, that’s what we wanted to ask you! Since you helped put the Mishkan together, maybe you can also tell us how to get the Beis Hamikdash up?

Ah, but I don’t need to tell you. You are already doing it perfectly! When you ignored the Yetzer Hara and kept trying to do your podcast, you were completing some of the last steps of building the Beis Hamikdash: finishing off that Yetzer Hara, leaving no trace of him. You’ll have the Geulah Ha’amitis V’hashleima in no time!

We were right! It was the Yetzer Hara trying to stop us!

Yeah, because we were doing something really good—building the Beis Hamikdash!

But, we’re not gonna let him win! We’re gonna get all the Yiddishe kids to join Tzivos Hashem and fight the Yetzer Hara! Then, we’re all going to march to Yerushalayim and bring karbanos in the perfect, Yetzer Hara- free Beis Hamikdash! Yay!

Yasher ko’ach! And now, let’s ask all the other chayolim to think about it: How will you fight your Yetzer Hara and build the Bais Hamikdash?

...and that’s another fabulous episode of the Parsha Podcast. Thank you, Eliezer from the family Merori, for joining us! We’ll be sure to think the question over. See you all chayolim next week!
