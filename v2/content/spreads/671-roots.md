In between Torah discussions, Baruch asked Zalman Meir and Moshe Leib some questions about the yeshivos from which they came. Which sefarim did they like to study? What was their preferred method of limmud Torah?

Both Zalman Meir and Moshe Leib described their love for analyzing the Gemara along with Rishonim and poskim. When immersing oneself in the “sea of Gemara”, they told Baruch, there was no time—and no need—for anything else.
