Yerushalayim will be \[so safe and overflowing

with people, that it will be\] lived in like a

city without walls.

spread way beyond the walls,” says Freidel. “And it’ll be so safe that it won’t even need them.”

Yochi’s eyes widen. “No walls at all?”

“Because everyone will know Hashem,” says Freidel. “So there won’t be wars anymore.”

Shneur suddenly drives a toy truck straight through the shoebox wall.

“GEULA!” he shouts.

The wall tumbles apart.

Yossi stares at the broken boxes for a moment. Then he grins.

“Actually,” he says, “Yerushalayim does look better without them.”

Soon all four children are singing as they rebuild their city bigger and wider than before, with more streets, more houses, and room for everybody.

A city ready for Moshiach.
