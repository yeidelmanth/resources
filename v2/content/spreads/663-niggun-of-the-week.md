<h3>Different and Special</h3>

Look at the bochur on the bongo drums. Did you ever see a smile like that?

His name is Dovid and his simcha is contagious, just like the simcha in this niggun. When you sing it, you can feel the world of Moshiach’s times!

Dovid has Down syndrome. Some mitzvos—like davening in shul and sitting and learning Torah—are hard for him to do. He isn’t so different that way from other people, and his mother always tells him that Hashem chose him especially for her. She says that all his mitzvos are precious to Hashem, especially the ones that are more challenging. And she tells him that even if a mitzvah

is hard now, later, Hashem will give us many brachos.

So Dovid likes to be an example to his siblings on how to be happy no matter what. Around Yom Tov time, that’s easy, he says, because there are so many mitzvos special for these days that Hashem gave especially to His special nation.

“Vesomachta! Bechogecha!” Dovid sings as he taps to the beat.

It’s a mitzvah to be happy on Yom Tov, and happiness is something that Dovid excels at.
