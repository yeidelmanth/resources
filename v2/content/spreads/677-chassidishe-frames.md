The Story Lives On: It’s easy to feel that our small actions have no value. "I am just a young chayol, so what difference can I make in the world?" Yet the Alter Rebbe returned for even a pair of slippers. If the Alter Rebbe worried that something as small as that could be taken by Napoleon and used against Hashem, then no matter what small action we do, we can definitely make a difference and bring Moshiach!

They had forgotten a pair of slippers, and so they (went back and) took the slippers also. As soon as the Alter Rebbe fled the second time, the (Frierdiker) Rebbe recounts, Napoleon

24 Teves missions arrived on the other side of Liadi."
