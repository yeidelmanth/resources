chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Listen, Chaim. I think it’s incredible that you can sit and do that all day. I wish I could, too, but it’s so hard. Especially when everyone else is having so much fun playing, and you know, there’s just so much to do! How can I possibly sit still?

I was just trying to encourage him to use his spare time for limmud Torah, but he seems to think that it’s something only I can do.

Chaim, we have this discussion at least once a week. I don’t think anything is gonna change Yossi’s mind. Let’s just call our guest and move on.

Oh, oh, oh! It’s Nachshon ben Aminadav! He’s so cool!

Listen, my friend, this isn’t just any jumping. This is the jumping that saved us from the Mirzrim, and that got us the Torah, the Mishkan, and into Eretz Yisroel! This is what Hashem was waiting for to make so many miracles happen!

Yes! I’m ready! I love jumping and I love all those things, too!

Ah my friend, there is much more to this jumping. This is the jumping that made the sea split! We were all stuck at the big Yam Suf with the evil Mirzrim chasing right behind us, threatening to bring us back to slavery. Hashem needed us to go right in. Right into the mighty waters— not knowing what would happen next.

With complete mesiras nefesh— that’s why the sea split for you!

Yes! Yes! I would’ve done it! I love jumping into water! And then the sea would have split and I would've marched right through, waving at my friends through the “water-wall windows” and holding my shevet’s flag for all to see!

Umm, sorry Nachshon, my brother’s a bit on the adventurous side. Don’t mind him.

Oh, I love it! This boy is going to split some seas! Now, I’ve got one for you— a place where you can do this jump.

Well, listen to this. You come back from school each day, tired and hungry. So, you eat to gain strength to serve Hashem, you rest a bit to clear your head, and maybe also play a bit of ball or jump rope. Then, you’re all good and ready to gather some mesiras nefesh and dive right in!

Dive right into Torah!. Everyone out there is telling you that you need to play all day long, and that you have no time to do Torah and mitzvos b’hiddur—beautifully and carefully. But, do you know what those messages really are? They’re a big, giant Yam Suf blocking your way, saying that you can’t do what Hashem wants.

But nowadays, no one tells us that we can’t do what Hashem wants! If Hashem says to do something, I’ll for sure jump right in!

Perfect! So then, don’t let anyone stop you from learning Torah in your spare time. Just jump right into it! Here, let’s open a sefer up and learn together. You’ll see all your distractions slip away; see how doing mitzvos b’hiddur is very possible. And when you do incredible things for Hashem, Hashem does incredible miracles for you.

Okay! I’m in! Hey, Chaim, maybe you were right after all…

And now, let’s ask all the other chayolim to think about it: What will you do in your spare time to jump into the “Yam Suf”?

…and that’s another fabulous episode of the Parsha Podcast. Thank you, Nachshon ben Aminadav, for joining us! We’ll be sure to think the question over. See you all chayolim next week!
