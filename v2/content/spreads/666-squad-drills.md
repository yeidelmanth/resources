I still remember the first time I met her. It was a hot Sunday afternoon, and I was loafing around my father’s meat stand in the marketplace of Charan. I was busy trying to sneak a pomegranate from the nearby fruit stand, and had almost successfully slipped it into my pocket, when I looked up and saw her walking towards me. I nearly dropped the pomegranate in shock. She was beautiful! The most beautiful person I had ever tzniusdik… No pretenses, no showing off5. And, as I found out later, even her name was royal - Sara means “princess”!

Sara gently cradled my hands - which were still holding the pomegranate - and looked lovingly into my eyes. She patiently explained, "There is one Hashem. And Hashem tells us that we are not allowed to steal." I had never heard such a thing in my life! Yet somehow, I understood the simple truth in her words, and quietly returned the pomegranate to its rightful stand. This was the first of many important lessons that I would learn from her.
