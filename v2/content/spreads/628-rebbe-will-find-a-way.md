So when Shula was born just two weeks after Mrs. Kazen passed away, Mommy decided to name her baby after this special woman.

Mrs. Kazen’s family was so touched by this. To show how much it meant to them, they wanted to give baby Shula the special gift of a Rebbe dollar.

As they looked through the dollars, they noticed that Mrs. Kazen had written a note on each one, saying which bracha the Rebbe had said when handing her the dollar. They chose to give a dollar with the words, “bracha v’hatzlacha”.

The very next day, Mommy took baby Shula to pick it up. She was so excited that she called Tatty right away to tell him the news!

“They gave us a dollar for bracha v’hatzlacha!” she told him.

and Mommy knew that the Rebbe had heard their tefillos.

After the first time Tatty and Mommy davened in the Rebbe’s room, they felt that their tefillos were heard.

Tatty and Mommy’s wedding anniversary was coming up, and Mommy had an idea for the perfect gift.

“I would love to daven in the Rebbe’s room on our special day,” she told Tatty. “Things have been hard lately, and I want to ask for a bracha.”

They lived near the Five Towns, so the trip wasn’t far. But for Tatty and Mommy, visiting the Rebbe’s room was a big deal! Neither of them had been zoche to go before, because they are baalei teshuvah who learned about Chassidus later in life.

Tatty made all the plans, and finally, the special day arrived. For the first time ever, they had the z’chus of davening in the Rebbe’s room.

And little Shula Shifra, who was only five months old, came along, too.

A little while after they left, Mommy’s phone rang. It was Mrs. Kotlarsky, the daughter of Mrs. Shula Shifra Kazen, aleha hashalom.

“I have a special gift for your baby,” she said. “Our family wants to give her a dollar from the Rebbe—a dollar that belonged to our mother.”

You see, our sister Shula was the very first baby girl to be named after Mrs. Kazen.

Mommy had been very inspired by Mrs. Kazen. During a hard time, she read a story about her in the N’shei Chabad Newsletter. Mrs. Kazen’s strength gave Mommy strength, too.
