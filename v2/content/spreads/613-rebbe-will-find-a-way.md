Mariela’s friends turned to her. “You know Mariela, I had such a hard time choosing a birthday gift for you.”

“Oh, it’s okay,” Mariela said. “I don’t need a present. I’m just happy that you came to my party!”

Her friend smiled and opened her purse. She took out an envelope and handed it to Mariela. “This is the present I chose,” she said simply. “I think you deserve it.”

Mariela opened the envelope and was shocked to see that there was a dollar from the Rebbe inside. She began to cry, unable to contain her excitement at receiving such a precious gift.

Just that morning, she had asked the Rebbe for a sign that he was with her. And now, she was holding a dollar from the Rebbe in her hands.

The lost Rebbe dollar helped bring Yiddishkeit and friendship to an elderly Jewish woman.

Unfortunately, one day, when Mariela wasn’t home, thieves broke into her house. They worked quickly and quietly, looking in every hiding spot to steal all the money and valuables they could find.

When Mariela came home, she was shocked to find her house a total mess. Realizing what had happened, she ran straight to her room and opened her closet, hoping that the thieves hadn’t found what was hidden inside.

But what she saw made her heart drop. The thieves had found her dollar from the Rebbe, which

had been hidden inside the closet. Her most precious possession was gone!

Mariela began to cry. How would she ever get another Rebbe dollar?

Years later, on her birthday, Mariela organized a big breakfast to celebrate with all of her friends.

Just before the celebration, she pictured the Rebbe in her mind. Rebbe, it’s been so long since I had that dollar from you, she thought. Please send me a sign that you are with me.

Then, she called her parents, who had flown to New York to visit the Ohel. “Perfect timing,” they told her. “We just got to the Ohel. We’ll daven for you and your family.”
