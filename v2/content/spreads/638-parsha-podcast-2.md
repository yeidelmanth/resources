chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Okay, be honest, do you all think we’ll ever really get out of this golus?

Yossi! Don’t speak like that. Of course we’ll be leaving Golus! Moshiach is coming very soon!

I know, I know, but sometimes it just seems impossible. We’ve been here for so long, and the Yetzer Hara is so strong … and I just feel stuck. I feel like this whole battle is too hard.

You know, Yossi? Sometimes I feel the same. Golus can get really tough.

You two are speaking nonsense! We’re gonna win, and we’re gonna win really soon. Now, Yossi, who are we calling today?

Um, Mommy gave me a list of options. Moshe, Aharon, Wahankh, Yocheved…

Wahankh?! Who is that? How is there someone in the Torah that I don’t know about? We’ve gotta talk to him! Or is it “her”…?

It’s a man, Chaim. Alright, I also have no idea who this person is. This is gonna be fun!

This is Wahanakh, for all your dark magic needs. How may I help you?

Uhh, we don’t need any dark magic. We just want to talk.

I see. Well, I am Paroh’s greatest magician of all. I run the headquarters for all of the kelipa in Mitzrayim. This is the place where the darkest evil comes from!

Oookay, you got me scared enough, thank you very much.

Here’s the story: You see, Paroh was a very powerful king. He had an extreme amount of control over Mitzrayim. No slave had ever escaped. Ever!

Yeah, yeah, Paroh is evil and horrible and a massive chunk of kelipa—and then Moshe came and whacked him out of there!

Well, yeah, but it wasn't so simple. Even Moshe didn’t want to approach Paroh at first. He knew just how strong the kelipa of Paroh was, and he realized that he would never be able to overcome him alone. At first, Moshe thought Paroh would never listen to someone like him, someone who couldn’t speak well—

Wait—what? Moshe was afraid of Paroh? But Moshe was the greatest tzaddik ever!

Well, okay, yes. He was able to do it. But only after Hashem gave him the power.

Hashem made Moshe His shliach! From that moment, Moshe had the power to win over Paroh and destroy the kelipos of Mitzrayim. That’s perfect, because Moshe was the leader of Bnei Yisroel, so he could help the rest of the Yidden have the power to win against kelipa, too.

Yes, and it changed the course of history! By giving that power to Moshe, Hashem gave it to every nasi hador, because every rebbe has a bit of Moshe inside of him.

So now, every nasi hador can destroy big kelipos, just like Moshe destroyed Paroh?
