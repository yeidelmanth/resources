“Relax. Smile. Only Hashem is in charge. אֵין עֹוד מִלְּבַּדֹו.”

Mommy looked around. Who had put this card in her Tehillim? She didn’t know, but right away she felt calmer inside.

Hashem is in charge, she reminded herself. The promotion will come at the right time.

Later that day, in Crown Heights, Mommy went to a sofer to buy new mehudar mezuzos for our home. Just as she was about to leave, the sofer stopped her.

“Can you do me a favor?” he asked. “I have a bag of tefillin and mezuzos for some Yidden in Brazil. Could you please bring them along with you? That would get them there much faster than if I’d send them in the mail.”

“Of course!” Mommy said with a smile. She was happy to do a mitzvah.

That night, she checked her computer—and gasped. There it was! The governor had finally signed her promotion!

After so many months of waiting, the bracha came right after visiting the Ohel. Mommy smiled as she remembered the little card: Relax. Smile. Only Hashem is in charge. .אֵין עֹוד מִּלְבַּדֹו

Months went by, and the papers sat on the governor’s desk— unsigned. Would Mommy ever get her promotion?

Mommy had worked at her government job for many years. Finally, it was time for her to get a promotion to an even better job! We were all so excited for her.

She filled out all the papers and gave them to the people in charge. They smiled and said, “You really deserve this! All that’s left is for the governor to sign the papers.”

Every day, Mommy checked her computer, hoping to see that the promotion was approved. But weeks went by, and nothing changed. Months passed, and there was still no promotion. The papers just sat on the governor’s desk, waiting to be signed.

“Isn’t there anything we can do to make it happen faster?” Mommy asked her boss one day.

He shook his head. “I wish there was,” he said. “But only the governor can sign the promotion. It could happen soon, or it could take a very long time.”

That Chof-Beis Shvat, Mommy flew all the way to New York to daven at the Ohel. She got there early in the morning, when the sky was still dark and the Ohel was quiet and peaceful.

At that moment, Mommy felt very connected to the Rebbe. She davened with kavana and read her letter, asking for a bracha that the promotion should come quickly.
