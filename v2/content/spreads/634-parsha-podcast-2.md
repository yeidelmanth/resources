to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

I don’t know what to do. My friends keep telling me it’s okay because it’s what everyone here does. But that doesn’t make sense!

Music! He wants to listen to songs that aren’t very chassidish. He says it’s okay because everyone else listens to them. Like, it’s what the stores play.

Uh oh. That’s no good! It must be an attack from the Yetzer Hara! What are you going to tell him?

That’s a good idea. Let’s see… Yosef Hatzaddik himself isn’t available this week, so I’m gonna call his guard. Sounds like a cool option, no?

Hello. This is His Royal Highness Yosef’s Head Guard. State your name and intentions.

Hi, Mr. Head Guard, I have no idea what half of that means, but my name is Yossi Levin and my family would like to learn something about Yosef Hatzaddik.

Ah, yes. His Royal Highness Yosef. I’ve known him since he was just a prisoner.

So you got to see him at every stage. How did he change?

Nothing has changed. His Highness is still the same stubborn Jew he always was. Doesn’t act like the rest of us Mitzrim here. He always says that “his King” is more important, and he’ll only do what He says. I used to invite him to the royal servant parties, hoping he’d become more like us, but he always refused. And now, why, it’s simply wondrous! He’s second-in-command; second only to Paroh himself!

Yeah, Paroh told him, “ּובִלְעָדֶיָך ֹלא יָרִים אִיׁש אֶת יָדֹו וְאֶת רַגְלֹו ּבְכָל אֶרֶץ מִצְרָיִם—Without your permission, no one can raise his hand or foot in the entire Land of Mitzrayim.”

Indeed. But even more than that: He’s trying to turn us into good people; to be like him.

Hey, this got me thinking. Yosef Hatzaddik didn’t do what the Mitzrim did, even though he was living with them. He still only listened to Hashem!

Just like we should do in Golus, even when the Yetzer Hara says to act like everyone else.

Right. If the Yetzer Hara tells us to eat the same food as the rest of the world, or listen to the same music, we should be like Yosef Hatzaddik.

His Royal Highness Yosef didn’t let anyone stop him from listening to his King.

Because in Hashem’s Army, we keep Torah and mitzvos!

We must follow the laws of the place we live, but only if they don’t go against the Torah.

So, we need to tell the Yetzer Hara to be quiet—because some things are only for people who aren’t in Hashem’s Army!

Oh boy, you kids are going to be just like His Royal Highness Yosef. Next thing you know, you’ll be ruling the land as second-in-command.

But we already are! We rule over our choices. No one can tell us to go against Hashem, because Hashem put us in charge of our actions!
