chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Baruch Hashem! Me, too. I have the best morahs ever!

I’m with you, Basya. My rebbi this year is really taking us places in Gemara. How about you, Yossi?

I don’t know … it feels like it’s still too hot for us to be back in school. Y’know, sitting inside in our uniforms and learning all day.

Yeah, even though I love school, my uniform is always sticking to me because it’s so hot. The kids in the goyishe school nearby can wear whatever they want, and they don’t look nearly as sweaty as us. Sometimes it’s really challenging to be different.

I guess so. But he sure is good at his job! Sometimes he sounds like a friend who just wants me to be happy and have fun. The Yetzer Hara is so tricky!

Yossi, I just thought of a guest who could help us. Someone really inspiring. He always speaks so passionately at the rallies.

Are you talking about General Shimmy Weinbaum? I’ve always wanted to meet him!

Yes, he is a great guest to kick off this year. Calling him now!

Baruch Hashem! But we need some help with our very tricky Yetzer Hara.

Especially when he seems like a friend. It can be so confusing!

Yossi, did you know that the Rebbe spoke to chayolim just like you and said exactly that?!

Yes! Hashem made a really tricky Yetzer Hara who sometimes pretends that he just wants what’s good for us, when really it’s the opposite. He pretends to be our friend and then gets us to do aveiros, which are not good for us at all.

Yeah, can’t he just leave us without a Yetzer Hara? Then it would be so much easier to do the right thing!

A geshmake point. But that is exactly why we are called Tzivos Hashem— Hashem’s Army. Because there’s an enemy that we need to fight and win!

Okay, but still. Mimi’s right. Hashem could've at least made it a bit easier to win. You know, like, give us a better chance.

“כִּי תֵצֵא לַמִלְחָמָה עַל אֹיְבֶיָך—when you go out to war on your enemies.” It’s actually interesting that it says “on your enemies,” and not “against your enemies.”

Yes! And that’s exactly it! Because Hashem is telling us about the war with our biggest enemy—the Yetzer Hara! Hashem tells us that, true, we need to wage war against the Yetzer Hara, but the moment we go out to fight, the very instant we decide to battle with him, we are “on” our enemy.

And wouldn’t that give us the advantage? In a real war, if you’re on top of your enemy—like if you’re flying in a plane over your enemy who’s on the ground— then you’re much more likely to win, simply because you’re higher up.

That’s right! The Rebbe tells us that from the beginning, we are above our Yetzer Hara, because we have Hashem on our side! We are stronger than him!

Yay! I’m not gonna let some silly little Yetzer Hara confuse me! I’m a soldier in Hashem’s Army! Hashem is on my side, and that means I’m gonna win!
