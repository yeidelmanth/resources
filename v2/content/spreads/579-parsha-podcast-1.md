chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Remember how last week I decided to ask Hashem for help with my kavana during davening? Guess what? It’s working!

Well, my mashpia suggested that I choose to focus on one tiny part of davening at a time. Working with one small paragraph sounds much less scary than trying to have kavana for the entire davening.

Because it’s Elul and we’re blowing the shofar every day, I chose the bracha called “תקע בשופר” from Shemona Esrei. It's about when Moshiach comes, and Hashem will blow the shofar to announce his arrival! I can’t wait!

Me neither! Do you think we could bring in Moshiach on our podcast this week?

Actually, I arranged for a guest from the kingdom of Feyold again. This time, it’s Ovadia, royal guard to King Souvrain! Yossi, you know how to use the phone best, and anyway, you called last time. Could you make the call again?

That, ma’am, was my trumpet! Instruments don't play themselves, you know. King Souvrain's Grand Coronation is tomorrow, so I’ve been awfully busy practicing!

Oh, hi. You must be Ovadia? We’re the Levin kids, and we’re excited to host you on our podcast!

Reb Ovadia, can you tell us what the coronation ceremony is like? It’s probably super fancy, right?

Oh, the coronation shall be the most wonderful celebration! King Souvrain will be heralded with great fanfare and joy, as is befitting for such a wonderful king. Dressed in our finest uniforms, we shall blow our trumpets and proclaim him as our king. Oh, what glory!

You know, one of the reasons that we blow the shofar on Rosh Hashana is because we are crowning Hashem as our king, and announcing that He is the Master of the universe.

And you are one bright young woman! The truth is, we don’t only accept Hashem as our king on Rosh Hashana itself. Blowing the shofar throughout Elul reminds us of the fact that we accept Hashem as our king every day of the year.

Wow, I never knew that before. What else are you doing for the king’s coronation?

Excellent question young lady! This morning I brought a special gift to the king: an umbrella spun from the silk of 912 silkworms fed on a diet of pure rose leaves. Its handle was encrusted with 26.8 carats of rare purple diamonds. The umbrella was a gift from the Duke of Richton to King Souvrain in honor of his coronation!

Wow, that is really something! Although I’m not sure how good a job it will do in protecting the king from the rain…

Yes they are! And, of course, the king will give plenty of gifts in return.

So, if we crown Hashem as king every day, what gifts can we give Him for His coronation ceremonies?

Oh, there are some very special things that you soldiers in Tzivos Hashem do, which He really appreciates. In fact, they are the very reason why He created the world!

Oh, do you mean that our presents are Torah and mitzvos?

Indeed they are, my young soldiers. And in return, Hashem shall bestow upon you the ultimate gift: the greatest shofar blast announcing Moshiach’s arrival!

So, chayolim, think about it: What can you do differently as you hear the shofar’s coronation sound every day of Elul?
