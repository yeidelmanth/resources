Hi there! We’re the Levin Family: Basya, Mimi, Chaim and

Yossi—chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Chaim, you’re so smart and you know so much Torah. So it’s easy for you to be good. But not me.

Yeah, Chaim. You can sit for hours on end and just focus on learning. I don’t know how you do it! I can’t sit for longer than ten minutes without doing some jumping jacks! If any of us grow up to be a tzaddik, it’ll be you, Chaim. Not me!

Oh, come on guys. You can learn too! The Baal Shem Tov says that every Yid has to serve Hashem in “avodah tama”. That means with t’mimus, in a way that is whole and perfect. Not just me. You, too!

So maybe Hashem should make us all able to do it easily, just like you. Then we all could serve Him with t’mimus.

Okay everyone, time for our podcast. Let’s get to work on Parshas Lech Lecha.

Good idea, Mimi. Yossi, how about you call in Avraham Avinu? I think he’ll have something to tell you.

Whoa, Avraham Avinu? Our great- zeidy? The perfect tzaddik? That makes me nervous! Lemme just tidy my uniform up first…

Wow! My children! Moderntechnology is just amazing! Now I can talk to my wonderful einiklach!

Avraham Avinu, I think Basya and Yossi could use some help. They’re saying that only Yidden like me, who like to learn, can serve Hashem right. I told them that every Yid has to serve Hashem like a tamim, but they don’t think it’s possible.

Oy, mine kinder, have you forgotten your great-zeidy already? Hashem said to me, ”“הִתְהַלְֵך לְפָנַי וֶהְיֵ’ה תָמִים, that I should go before Him and be perfect. So I did! I served Hashem perfectly, with all my heart.

With the Torah’s help, of course. We just have to look inside, see what Hashem wants from us, and do it! It’s ”“ּתֹורַת ה’ ּתְמִימָה, perfect and complete, so it can show us how to be perfect and complete as well.

But we’re not Avraham Avinus. We’re little kids with big Yetzer Haras. We can’t do that!

An inheritance! Like the Torah. All the Yidden inherit the Torah, just for being Yidden. It’s something we got from birth.

Yes! You all inherited the Torah. Do you know whatelse you inherited?

All of Avraham Avinu’s ruchniyus’dik strength! From the day we were born, we were given the ko’ach to be as wonderful and perfect as you, Avraham Avinu!

And since we also inherited the Torah, we have where to look to know how to achieve that.

Okay, okay! Maybe it was just my silly Yetzer Hara talking before. Will I bring Moshiach if I serve Hashem with all my heart, in a perfect way?

Of course! Hashem’s brachos will come down with t’mimus—perfection! And when we’ll have the Geulah ha’amitis v’hashleima, every Yid will be perfect, because then they’ll know the whole Torah! So, chayolim, think about it: How can each and every one of you serve Hashem with t’mimus?

…and that’s another fabulous episode of the Parsha Podcast. Zeidy Avraham Avinu, thank you for joining us! We’ll be sure to think the question over. See you all chayolim next week!
