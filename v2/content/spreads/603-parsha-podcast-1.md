What? Why?! Yossi, you’re amazing at violin! You play “Ani Maamin” so nicely that Mommy even starts crying.

Yeah, but playing violin doesn’t feel so chassidish.

Hmm. Yossi, I think you can use some advice from the Rambam. And this has to do with Parshas Teruma, so you can bring him onto the podcast.

Okay. I’ll just dial this number for the Rambam and—

Aha! Hello to the chayolim of the Parsha Podcast! What would you children like to know today?

Yossi stopped playing violin. Even though he is very talented and so good at it.

Hmm. Let’s talk about the Beis Hamikdash for a moment. In Parshas Teruma, we learn about building a mikdash for Hashem.

Yes, it was. And if you learn my sefarim, then you’ll see the halacha: “If it is possible to cover the Beis Hamikdash with gold, then it is a mitzvah to do so.”

I have a gold bracelet, but I don’t have a Beis Hamikdash to put it on.

Ahh, but you children do have a Beis Hamikdash! Every Yid is supposed to build a mikdash in their heart, and serve Hashem from there.

Good question. We are supposed to make the mikdash as beautiful as we can. Hashem gave everyone their own gifts to use for beautifying their own mikdash. Some people have a great memory, so they must use it to remember a lot of Torah.

Yes. Some people have a lot of money, which they must use to give lots of tzedakah. Others might be able to understand and explain ideas very well— so they are to teach Torah to others in their own special way. Someone else might have a beautiful voice for singing beautiful davening and niggunim.

And someone might be able to play the violin very well!

Yes, that someone was given a special gift so that he could make his mikdash beautiful with it. Maybe that someone can play beautiful niggunim which inspire people to get closer to Hashem, or happy tunes to get everyone dancing at a simcha!

But is it really a good thing to play violin so much? It’s not like it’s a mitzvah or anything.

If Hashem gave you the special gift of music, then yes—it’s even a mitzvah for you to play violin! It’s your own special mitzvah to play sweet tunes to Hashem and use the violin to make your mikdash beautiful!

So playing violin is a mitzvah for me? I mean, I do love playing niggunim and feeling the tunes…

It most certainly is a mitzvah for you. And when you use this gift from Hashem to make your mikdash beautiful, then Hashem will give you even greater gifts! Hashem will help you understand Torah better, and give you greater chances to do more mitzvos!

Yossi, you’ll be able to serve Hashem in an outstanding way!
