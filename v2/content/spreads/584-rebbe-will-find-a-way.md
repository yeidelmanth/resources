And it all started when the Rebbe sent encouragement

When Tatty and Mommy were feeling nervous about moving on shlichus, the Rebbe found a way to encourage them through their non-Jewish mover!

The big moving truck pulled up in front of our new home in West Hollywood, California. The mover, a kind man from Jamaica, opened the back of the truck and began to unload.

“So, what brings you from New York to California?” the mover asked Tatty. Beads of sweat shone on his forehead as he schlepped the heavy boxes out of his moving truck and into our new home.

Tatty thought for a moment. How could he explain what shlichus was to this non- Jewish man? “It’s like this,” he began, “there are many Jews living in this area who don’t know what it means to live like a Jew. My wife and I, together with our baby boy, are moving out here to help them connect with their faith.”

The mover whistled in amazement. “Incredible! You must be so excited to get started with your important work!”

“We are,” Tatty said with a sigh, “but we’re also nervous. We don’t know anyone who lives here yet, and it’s a very expensive place to live. I hope things will work out okay.”

“You bet everything will be okay, Rabbi!” exclaimed the mover. “Wait right there.” He jumped into the front of his truck and returned a moment later … holding a picture of the Rebbe!

Huh? This man from Jamaica, who isn’t even Jewish, keeps a Rebbe picture with him in his truck?! Tatty couldn’t believe his eyes.
