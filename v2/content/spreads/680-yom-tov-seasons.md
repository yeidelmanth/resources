But they affect a Yid's conduct throughout his whole life \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.

We recall Eretz Yisroel with the seven fruits we eat,

Producing more fruit, which are endless good \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.

“ִּכּי ָהָָאָָדָם ֵעֵץ ַהַָּׁשăֶָדֶה—Every person is like a tree.” Do you

Just as a tree's purpose is to produce fruit, ours is to “produce” Torah and mitzvos. Fruits contain seeds that can grow into more trees, and our mitzvos inspire more

The more we grow in our own Yiddishkeit, the more “fruit”—

Torah and mitzvos—we can spread to those around us.

And just like it’s true that the way a seed is handled—every tiny detail!—affects how it grows, every small act in chinuch has a lasting impact on our future in Torah and mitzvos.

The Shivas Haminim, the special fruits of Eretz Yisroel, remind us that every mitzvah we "plant" helps bring Moshiach closer.
