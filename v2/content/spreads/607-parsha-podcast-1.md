chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Guess what! Our school is doing a play about the story of Pesach, and Mommy, Mimi, Bubby, and Tante Sara are all gonna come watch!

That's so cool! Who’s building the props? Can I help build stuff? I have some really cool ideas.

Calm down Yossi, the older girls are making all the stuff already. Basya, why don’t we do the podcast and then you can tell us about your play?

Well actually, the part that my friend Chaya is playing is from this week’s parsha, and we think she has a very important lesson for us.

Me and Chaya and Mommy and my teacher. And I already asked Yossi if I could make the call this week. So, we’re gonna have her as our guest!

Hi, Chaya! Basya was very excited about your part in the play. Who are you gonna be? Yocheved? Or maybe little Miriam?

I’m gonna be a girl leaving Mitzrayim! I’ll have a tambourine and a pretty dress and a gigantic bag of fancy jewelry. It’s super heavy, but that’s okay because it’s also super important.

Umm, I know it’s a mitzvah to wear nice jewelry for Shabbos and Yom Tov, but why would a little girl need so much? Why is such a big bag so important?

Oh, I’m not bringing it for myself! Everyone is bringing gold and silver and precious materials to build the Mishkan, and I am too!

Wow! The kids also brought things to build the Mishkan? Did the adults even let the kids carry stuff?

Of course! All the kids brought whatever they could! They were so excited! Even in Mitzrayim they prepared to build the Mishkan.

Oh, remember in Parshas Shemos when Yocheved told us about the kids saying “ּזֶה קֵלִי וְאַנְוֵה—This is my Hashem!”?

Yeah, when the Yidden came out of Mitzrayim, the kids were the first ones to recognize Hashem.

Yup. It was amazing! We were able to point to Hashem! We said that we want to serve Him in the most beautiful way we can.

And I also want to be able to point to Hashem and say, “יִזֶה קֵל—There He is!”

Well, lucky for us, Moshe said that we kids have the same neshamos as the ones who left Mitzrayim. When we leave Golus and build the Mikdash Hashlishi, we’ll also get to see Hashem.

YAY! But how do we prepare to build the Beis Hamikdash when we’re still in Golus? I don’t have any gold!

Well, we make our home into a Mikdash Me’at for Hashem to live in, right?

Yeah, Yossi. When everyone in the house acts the way Hashem wants, then it’s a mini Beis Hamikdash.

Right. Especially when the kids do, because we kids have a special connection to Hashem. That’s why we’ll get to see Hashem first, just like the kids who left Mitzrayim!
