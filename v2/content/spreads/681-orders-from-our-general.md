<p class="lead">The first ger tzedek (righteous convert) mentioned in the Torah is Yisro, Moshe’s father-in-law. Yisro was an important person living in Midyan, far from the midbar. What made him leave everything behind and become a Yid?</p>

All the way in Midyan, Yisro heard about the miracles that Hashem did for the Yidden during Yetzias Mitzrayim and

Krias Yam Suf. He was so impressed that he decided to join the Yidden at Har Sinai. He wanted to receive the Torah and become part of this special nation, too.

This shows us that Hashem is always with every Yid and does open miracles for them—so much so, that even someone as far away as Yisro could hear about it!

What is special about how Hashem treats us Yidden?

He gives us what we need and then leaves us alone.

He is always right there with us and performs open miracles for our sake.

And Yisro heard about what Hashem did for Moshe and for

Even now in Golus, Hashem stands with every Yid. Usually, soldiers are not in the same place as their commanders. But Hashem is different; He is with every single chayol at every moment. Like the passuk learn, daven, and do mitzvos, and He is ready to bring the Geulah as soon as our avodah has been completed.

<span class="mc">Just like the miracles of Yetzias Mitzrayim were clear for all to see, the same will happen in our final Geulah. The entire world will watch as Hashem takes every Yid out of Golus with open and revealed nissim.</span>

This will start with us chayolim, just like the children in Mitzrayim recognized Hashem before anyone else. Let’s keep looking for ways to share our love for Torah and mitzvos even better than before. After all, Hashem is with us, waiting to perform the biggest miracles yet!

If Yisro lived so far away, how do you think he heard about the miracles?

If someone watched the way you acted every day, what would show them that you know Hashem is with you?

What is Hashem waiting for us to do now?

News of Hashem’s miracles spread all the way to Yisro, and when Moshiach comes, the news of the Geulah will spread even faster!
