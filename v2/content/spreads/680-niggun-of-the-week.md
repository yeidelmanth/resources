<h3>The Geula is coming NOW!</h3>

What are you doing?"

"Making a bracha on your cookies?" grins Esther Miriam.

Esther Miriam watches and sighs. "Little kids can say that! But I’m eleven. I’ve been waiting much longer and the Geula still hasn’t come."

"Sure!" says Sara. "And thinking about what to do is also positive. Good thoughts lead to good deeds," she adds, pulling the chocolate crinkle cookies from the oven.

"So what?" says fourteen-year-old Sara. "If the Rebbe says Geula is coming, it’s coming! Anyway, we can’t just wait; we have to do something. Freidel makes farbrengens, Yossi, Yochi, and Shneur play and sing, and I’m baking cookies for the farbrengen.

"I’ve got a great idea!" says Esther Miriam. "I’ll do some mivtzoyim soon!"

"Be quick!" urges Sara. "The Geula is already coming!"
