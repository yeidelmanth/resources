Dr. K. began: “My wife and I got married about fifty years ago. For a long time, we waited and waited for children. We went to the best doctors, but nothing helped. Then, soon after moving to Zaire, Africa, my wife became pregnant with our daughter—the one who lives here now! Later, we were also blessed with a son.

“For years, we wondered how that miracle happened. And now we know!”

“Recently, the Chabad rabbi where I live showed me an old photo from Zaire in תשד”מ (1984),” Dr. K. said. “It was found in a collection of pictures sent to the Rebbe years ago. My wife was in the photo! On the back, it said that she lit Shabbos candles and kept some mitzvos.

“But the most surprising thing was the date. The photo was taken one month before my wife became pregnant! I immediately understood that the Rebbe had seen the picture and blessed us with children.”

“Who knows,” Dr. K. added with a smile. “Maybe the Rebbe blessed us with a daughter so that one day she would live in Olympia, and we’d be able to help your Chabad House! Well, let me tell you: I’ll continue my support for as long as I can!”

Baruch Hashem, Dr. K. kept his word and continued to support our Chabad House. And the brachos didn’t stop there. Soon afterward, Tatty saw more help with the finances, and paying the bills became much easier.

He had been so worried about our financial situation, and the Rebbe sent a clear message: Long before our family moved on shlichus, the Rebbe’s brachos had already begun.

Many years before our shlichus, the Rebbe’s bracha had already begun.

“You won’t believe what I just heard,” Tatty told Mommy one day. “There’s a big, beautiful property for sale right here in Olympia!”

They could already imagine it: shiurim, farbrengens, and children’s programs, with Yidden coming and going. A real home for our Chabad House.

Tatty and Mommy raised money for the down payment, but getting a bank loan was harder. The bank needed a guarantor— someone who would promise to pay if Tatty couldn’t.

As the closing deadline came closer, Tatty wrote to the Rebbe for a bracha. Then he met with supporters to see if they could help. But when the meeting ended, he still had no guarantor.

Right then, his phone pinged. It was a message from Dr. K.

Dr. and Mrs. K. live far away in California, but they support our Chabad House because their daughter lives here.

Tatty opened the message. “Amazing news!” he exclaimed. “Dr. K. agreed to be the guarantor!”

Baruch Hashem, we bought the property. But we lived an hour’s walk away, so we would need to move closer.

Then, a few days later, the house near the property went up for sale!

The Rebbe encouraged shluchim to buy homes to show that they’re there to stay, so Tatty and Mommy decided to buy it. They raised money, and once again, Dr. and Mrs. K. helped.

Our shlichus was growing, but so were our expenses. It was hard for Tatty and Mommy to cover the bills.

One day, Tatty got a message from Dr. K. We’re coming to visit our daughter. Can we have a meeting?

Uh oh. Tatty’s heart began to pound. Were they upset? Would they stop helping our Chabad House?

Before the meeting, Tatty wrote a heartfelt letter to the Rebbe. Please give us a bracha that everything should work out in the best way, Tatty wrote.

At the meeting, Dr. K. leaned forward. “Chabad rabbis always tell miracle stories about the Rebbe,” he said. “Now I have one of my own.”
