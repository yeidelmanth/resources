Did you look under your chair? A hair clip couldn’t have gone too far.

Yes, I did, but I still can’t find it. Tante Rochel bought that clip for me. It was my favorite one, and now it’s gone!

Here, Basya, you can use my headband for now and we’ll keep looking for your hair clip after the podcast.

Thank you for taking the time to talk to us, Avraham Avinu. We know how busy it must be with all the guests you have.

Baruch Hashem, it has been a busy day with travelers coming in from all sides, but there is always time to talk to my dear einiklach!

Oh, thank you. We learned all about your big tent in parsha class, but I never saw a tent with four whole doors in real life. I wonder what that’s like?

I made a mini one! It’s right under here, somewhere … just give me a minute … and, tada! I got it! A tent with a door on each of the four sides, just like yours, Avraham Avinu! Oh, and would you look at that! Basya’s clip is in here, too!

You found my clip? Oh yes, it’s my long-lost-most-favorite clip! Thank you so, so, so much, Yossi! You’re the best! Hashem should bentch you with all the brachos and a very happy life, and lots of ice cream and chocolate and Lego, and may you be a big chossid, yarei Shamayim, and lamdan!

Amein! What wonderful brachos! Basya’le, you just reminded me of a bracha that Hashem gave to me. Hashem said “ךָוַאֲבָרְכָה מְבָרֲכֶי—I will bentch those who bentch you.” This means that anyone who gives a Yid a bracha will receive many great brachos from Hashem!

Well then, Basya is about to get bentched by Hashem big time!

Mm-hmm. Hashem is going to shower brachos on her in the greatest way!

Well, let’s think about a regular bracha. You have to work for it, right?

Yeah, I mean, for a really good grade, you have to study really hard before Hashem helps you succeed. And to be a great chossid, you need to spend a lot of time working on yourself. Even if you got a bracha.

So then what would be different about getting a bracha in the “greatest way”?

Well, when Hashem is extremely happy with us, He doesn’t even wait for us to work hard! He just showers all the brachos on us nice and easy!

Like when I do something extra nice for someone, and then Mommy runs up to me and gives me tons of hugs and kisses, and makes my favorite supper without me even asking?

Exactly! Hashem gets so excited when one Yid gives a bracha to another. Hashem especially loves it when soldiers in Tzivos Hashem do that, because you kids are powering up the world with your pure tefillos and limmud Torah. When you bentch another Yid, Hashem gives you all the brachos without you having to work for them.

Ooh, that’s awesome! Avraham Avinu, may you have tons of nachas from all of your children! Chaim, may Hashem bentch you to understand everything you learn! Yossi, may you grow up to be a great chossid of the Rebbe! And Basya, may you always have great Yiras Shamayim—and never lose your hair clip ever again!

Amein! And now let’s ask all the other chayolim to think about it: How will you make Hashem happy with your brachos?
