chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

Mimi, I heard you had a tough day today. I’m so sorry! You must be really upset.

Yeah, I heard you forgot you lunch, and then slipped and scraped your knee at recess…

…and then, in class, someone accidentally spilled juice on your worksheet! Even I would cry from all that!

Oh, yeah, it was hard. But it’s okay, really! I know it was all Hashgacha Pratis. Hashem planned for everything to happen just the way it did. I’m sure He has a good reason.

Wow, Mimi. You're like the Baal Shem Tov! Or Rabi Yochanan!

Really? I mean, I know the Baal Shem Tov was always pointing out Hashgacha Pratis, but what does Rabi Yochanan have to do with it?

Rabi Yochanan is our guest today! Let’s call him and ask.

Hi Rabi Yochanan. My sister Mimi has been having such a positive attitude, I think you’ll be so proud.

It’s not so amazing. I just remember that Hashem controls everything that happens to me, and then I feel fine. It’s all Hashgacha Pratis.

Ah, that’s beautiful, my child. Did you know that Hashgacha Pratis is not just for the Jewish people, but it also includes the birds, fish, and all other creations?

Yeah!​ The Baal Shem Tov always talked about it. He said it’s even for a worm and a leaf! Hashem really cares about every single thing that He created!

Exactly! For example, right now I’m watching as the seagulls swoop down to the sea and catch some fish for dinner. In Hebrew, a seagull is a “ׁשָלְָך”, which is similar to the shoresh for “send—שלח”.

Oh, oh, oh! I know! It’s cuz Hashem sends them to eat the fish!

You got it, Yossi. Whenever I see this, I’m reminded of the passuk in Tehillim, “מִׁשְּפָטֶיָך ּתְהֹום רַּבָה.”

“Hashem’s justice is the great deep.” Hashem even watches over what happens to fish deep in the ocean, so of course He takes care of what happens to us, His children.

Wow. This is all so amazing. Hashem is awesome. But what does this have to do with the parsha?

In Parshas Shmini, we learn the list of birds that are tamei. One of them listed is…

Wow. I guess it’s also Hashgacha Pratis that we were talking about Hashgacha Pratis during the week when the parsha teaches us about Hashgacha Pratis!

Absolutely. And remember, children: Everything in Hashem’s world is Hashgacha Pratis—whether they seem to “click” together or not. Every single detail is meant to be. And now, let’s ask all the other chayolim to think about it: How will you make sure to appreciate the Hashgacha Pratis in your life?
