over the next few days, her eye continued to get better, until there was no redness at all!

Bubby was thrilled. On Motzei Yom Tov, she told our uncle the amazing news.

“Baruch Hashem!” he exclaimed. “Please tell me, which one of Devoiry’s eyes had the issue?”

Our uncle shared that over Yom Tov, he had read a letter of the Rebbe. The Rebbe was writing to a person who had a problem in his left eye. The Rebbe told him that he should not have an operation and that he would have a refuah shleima!

Bubby was filled with awe. It was after Gimmel Tammuz, and yet the Rebbe had

heard her tefillos and gave the exact bracha she had asked for!

Over the next few months, it was clear that Devoiry’s eye had truly healed on its own. Until

today, she feels so privileged and grateful that she received such a direct bracha from the Rebbe: A complete recovery with no need for surgery!

Worry filled Bubby’s heart as she listened to the doctor’s words. Would her precious baby be okay?

It all began when our Aunt Devoiry was just a few weeks old. Bubby noticed that her left eye was red and teary, so she took her to the doctor.

The doctor examined her eye closely. “I’m prescribing eye drops for your baby,” he said. “Her redness and tearing should clear up soon.”

While the eye drops helped a bit, the problem continued to flare up again and again. As Devoiry’s first birthday came closer, Bubby decided to bring her to an eye specialist.

The specialist peered into Devoiry’s eye. “I’m sorry to have to tell you this,” he said. “The condition of your daughter’s eye is very serious. In order to fix it, we need to operate.”

Bubby was very scared for her small baby to have surgery.

At the time, Bubby and Zeidy were living in Argentina. It was Tishrei, and our uncle, Devoiry’s older brother, was preparing to go to New York to spend Sukkos with the Rebbe. Bubby sat down and wrote a heartfelt letter to the Rebbe: Rebbe, please bentch Devoiry to have a refuah shleima, with no need for surgery.

She gave our uncle the letter and asked him to bring it to the Ohel as soon as he arrived in New York. Our uncle did exactly as Bubby asked, and then continued on to Crown Heights.

Meanwhile, in Argentina, Devoiry awoke bright and early on the first day of Yom Tov. Her eye looked fine, but Bubby thought it was because of the drops she had put in.
