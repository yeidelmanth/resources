chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Chayolim, in what way can this week’s lesson make a difference to you? What changes can you make in your own life?

But … don’t let it stop here! Conduct your own interviews at the Shabbos table. Choose someone

to be the podcast’s guest, and together explore how to live with the messages from the parsha.

Oh no, Yossi thinks he’s going to build the Mishkan this week!

Yeah, it’s one part. Moshe counts all the things that the Yidden gathered for the Mishkan, and then, on Rosh Chodesh Nissan, they set it up for the first time ever!

That’s exactly why I brought the special cedar wood! I’m gonna get all the instructions from Betzalel, and then build my own Mishkan!

Hi Betzalel! To give a little intro for the rest of us, you’re the one who was put in charge of building the Mishkan. And you were just thirteen years old at the time! Pretty amazing.

Yeah, so cool! Anyway, Betzalel, I have all this cedar wood over here. I wish I could’ve sent some to you for the Mishkan. And y’know, how did you even get cedar wood out there in the desert?

Not quite. When the Yidden went down to Mitzrayim, Yaakov Avinu brought along cedar trees from Eretz Yisroel. He planted and took care of them, and told his children to bring the trees along when they would finally leave. He said that one day, they would need cedar trees to build the Mishkan. And, guess what?

That’s what they did! Yay for Yaakov Avinu and yay for the Yidden of Mitzrayim! It’s all because they started preparing for the Mishkan while they were still in golus!

Aww … Betzalel, you’re so lucky you got to have such a big part in building the Mishkan. I still wish I could send you some wood, though, so I could also have a part in the special mitzvah.

But Yossi, you get to build something far better—the Beis Hamikdash, which is going to last forever! And, Hashem says that you will build it in the same way that the Mishkan was built.

So, we’ll also start getting ready while we’re in golus?

Yup! By making every home into a mikdash me’at where Hashem can live!

But then, what am I gonna do with all this cedar wood that I collected?

Yossi, I’m a professional carpenter. I’d be glad to teach you how to build some things for your mikdash me’at.

Maybe you can help us make a pretty pushka? Or some other things we can put inside to do mitzvos beautifully?

How about a besamim case that’s also a Havdalah candle holder? And with a special compartment to store matches?

And then, we can give classes to other kids, so we can all prepare for the Beis Hamikdash Hashlishi with beautiful things and in a beautiful way!

Sure! Those are all great ideas. I’ll happily teach you how to build them all, and as Hashem says, “וְׁשָכַנְּתִי ּבְתֹוכָם— I will live amongst them.” The next thing you know, we’ll all fly over to the Beis Hamikdash! And now, let’s ask all the other chayolim to think about it: How will you make your house a mikdash me’at to prepare for Moshiach?

...and that’s another fabulous episode of the Parsha Podcast. Thank you, Betzalel, for joining us! We’ll be sure to think the question over. See you all chayolim next week!
