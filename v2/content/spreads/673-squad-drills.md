<span class="mc" style="color:#ba1126">Getting to know the enemy’s weapons and tactics before planning</span>

<span class="mc" style="color:#ba1126">In a war, the first step toward victory is to expose the enemy’s strategy. This way, we avoid falling into his trap. In Tzivos Hashem we face the trickiest enemy: the Yetzer Hara.</span>

<span class="mc" style="color:#ba1126">Here’s his strategy: First, the Yetzer Hara plants negative thoughts in our heads. Then, those thoughts grow into worse feelings that can lead to even more dangerous actions.</span>

One of the ways that the Yetzer Hara tricks us is by telling us to be defiant: “I live MY life, I can do what I want, and no one else should control me. If I listen to my parents or teachers, I would be giving in to their control and not be in charge of my own life. I must stand up for myself; I will not forfeit my freedom!”

Look at the following scene and identify where the Yetzer Hara could instigate your defiance and trigger your thoughts, speech, and actions.

We have just uncovered another one of the Yetzer Hara’s tricks. He encourages us to defy our parents and teachers - to defy Hashem - by telling us it’s the only way to stay in control of our lives.

Ask each squad member to think of a time when they fell into the enemy’s trap and behaved defiantly. Identify what thoughts and feelings were created, and how each of you acted.
