Rabi Akiva’s students are also remembered this day;

Anything forbidden, we are careful not to \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ .

With our Torah and mitzvos, it’s all in our hands,

We reveal the great z’chus of our Avos in the past.

And Moshiach will surely be here so very \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ !

Returns the world to the way that it’s meant to \_\_\_\_\_\_\_ !

There are two different reasons for which it is so.
