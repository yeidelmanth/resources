Over the years, he’s had many special experiences with the Rebbe, but this story is a favorite. (Rabbi Groner, the Rebbe’s secretary, who also appears in this story, is our zeidy, too!)

It was the middle of the night in the early 5740’s (1980’s),

and Zeidy Benjaminson was fast asleep. Suddenly, his phone began to ring. It rang and rang until it woke him up.

Looking at the clock next to his bed, he saw that it was two o’clock in the morning! Zeidy thought to himself, “Who could be calling at this hour? It must be important!”

Curious and a little concerned, Zeidy answered the phone.

“It’s me, Leibel Groner,” said the voice on the other end of line. Rabbi Groner? Wow! Zeidy knew it must be urgent.

“Rabbi Groner,” he quickly responded, “What can I do for you?”

Zeidy Groner replied, “Rabbi Benjaminson, are you in your office by any chance?”

“Rabbi Groner, I’m not at my office. I’m at home—it’s two o’clock in the morning!”

“Well then, I need you to go there immediately. The Rebbe has an urgent mission for you to complete at your office.”

Of course, as soon as Zeidy Benjaminson heard that, he immediately reached for his clothes and prepared to leave. “What does the Rebbe need me to do?” he asked.

“A young girl was in a terrible car accident tonight,” Zeidy Groner explained. “She’s now at the hospital, and in critical condition. The Rebbe says that in order for her to recover, she must be promoted

immediately to a higher rank in Tzivos Hashem. This will lift her up b’ruchniyus, and it will help her get better b’gashmiyus.”

“I’m on my way!” Zeidy Benjaminson assured him, and ran out the door.

In those days, there were no computer systems to record everyone's rank on the internet. Instead, Tzivos Hashem used index cards to store each chayol’s information, including their name, address, and rank. Zeidy’s office had over 60,000 index cards stored in filing cabinets. When Zeidy got to the office, he searched and searched until he finally found the sick girl’s card. He took a pen, crossed out the old rank, and wrote in her new, higher rank. He then went home and got back

A few days later, Zeidy Groner called Zeidy Benjaminson again. “Baruch Hashem, a miracle has happened for the little girl! She has fully recovered from her injuries! I just got off the phone with her mother; they’re on the way home from the hospital now.”

“Baruch Hashem!” Zeidy Benjaminson exclaimed. How grateful he was to have played a part in her refuah shleima, and how lucky he felt to be directing Tzivos Hashem, the most powerful army in the world!

Indeed, the Rebbe spoke about the power of Tzivos Hashem many times, telling us that it is not just a game or a fun idea. Tzivos Hashem is a serious matter. We are real soldiers in a real army.

In this story, we see clearly how important it is to be part of Tzivos Hashem, and how much it can truly affect the life of every chayol. Every time we hear this story, we feel re-inspired to strengthen our commitment to Tzivos Hashem. How important it is to succeed in our missions, move up in rank, and help others join this powerful army too—so that together, we can bring Moshiach NOW!

Our bubby and zeidy, Rabbi and Mrs. Yerachmiel Benjaminson, got married in the early 5740’s (1980’s). As dedicated chassidim, they wanted to go out on shlichus. While they were looking into various ideas, Zeidy was approached and asked if he would instead stay in Crown Heights and take charge of the Rebbe’s army, Tzivos Hashem. Zeidy didn’t know what to do, and turned to the Rebbe for advice. The Rebbe’s answer was clear: He should accept the position.

And so, Zeidy was hired to be the Executive Director of Tzivos Hashem.

Levi Yitzchak ,10 Shaina, 8 Tzemach Groner, 7 MyShliach, Calgary, Alberta, Canada
