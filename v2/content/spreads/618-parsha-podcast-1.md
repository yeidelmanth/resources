chayolim just like you. Welcome to our podcast, where

Wow, we’ve been running the Parsha Podcast for a long time now!

I hope a lot of chayolim have learned from our parsha lessons!

Who was your favorite Parsha Podcast guest so far? Mine was the moon. Oh, and the mountain climber!

Hmm, I really enjoyed the discussion with Yosef Hatzaddik.

Well, the Yetzer Hara was scary, but I think I learned the most from when we called for a robber, and he showed up instead!

I liked Aharon Hakohen. He was so nice and really made me feel good. Could we bring Aharon again? Please?

Bringing Aharon Hakohen is a good idea! I'm sure he has something good to teach us from this parsha, too.

Oh yeah, because Hashem actually gave a special mitzvah to Aharon in this week's parsha! “When you light the neiros, the seven neiros should lean towards the center—called the “panim”— of the Menorah.” And I wanna know why.

Uh, I don't know how you guys know all this stuff by heart, but it's really neat that you do. I guess I'll do my job and call Aharon, he’s already saved to favorites…

Oh, it's the fantastic Parsha Podcast again! The kids who are bringing Moshiach so we can light the Menorah again!

Aharon, we missed you! And we wanted to ask you a question about something about—well, that … uh … Mimi what was it?

Hashem told you that when you light the menorah, the candles have to lean towards the center. Why did they have to do that?

Good question! But first, I have a question for you! What are the neiros on the Menorah compared to?

Our neshamos. And we light them up with the fire of Torah and mitzvos.

Exactly! Did you know that my job as Kohen Gadol is to light up all Yiddishe neshamos?

Ohhh! That's what you’re doing when you light the candles on the Menorah!

You figured it out! The lights of the Menorah in the Beis Hamikdash are the lights of every Yiddishe neshama shining with Torah and mitzvos. And where do we all get the light of Torah and mitzvos from?

Yes! Learning Torah and doing mitzvos connects us to Hashem, who gives us the light! That's why we always keep Hashem in the center of our lives.

Oh, I get it now! That's why the candles face the middle: because the center of the Menorah is Hashem!
